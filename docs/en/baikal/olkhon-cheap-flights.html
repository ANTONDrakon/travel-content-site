How to Find Cheap Flights to Olkhon Island 2026

Updated: July 2026 • Expert guide • ⏱ 5 min read

If you're dreaming of the crystal-clear waters of Lake Baikal, the rugged beauty of Olkhon Island, and the mystical shamanic rocks of Khoboy Cape, you're not alone. Olkhon is one of Russia's most magical destinations, but getting there can feel like a puzzle—especially if you're on a budget. I've been there myself, and I know that finding affordable flights is half the battle. Let me walk you through everything you need to know to score cheap flights to Olkhon Island in 2026, from direct routes to sneaky hacks that'll save you thousands of rubles.

First things first: Olkhon Island doesn't have its own commercial airport. The nearest major gateway is Irkutsk International Airport (IKT), which sits about 250 kilometers west of the island. From Irkutsk, you'll take a bus or drive to the ferry port at Sakhyurta, then hop on a ferry or ice road (depending on the season) to Olkhon. So when we talk about "flights to Olkhon," we really mean flights to Irkutsk. And the good news? Irkutsk is well-connected, especially from Moscow, St. Petersburg, and several Asian hubs. Let's break down your options.

Direct Flights

Direct flights to Irkutsk (IKT) are your quickest option, and they're surprisingly affordable if you book smart. Here are the main airlines and their typical departure cities:

  • S7 Airlines — The most frequent operator, with direct flights from Moscow (DME) and Novosibirsk (OVB). From Moscow, expect prices around ₽12 000 (≈ $130) to ₽25 000 (≈ $270) one-way in economy. From Novosibirsk, it's cheaper: about ₽8 000 (≈ $87) to ₽15 000 (≈ $163).
  • Aeroflot — Direct flights from Moscow (SVO) to Irkutsk. Prices range from ₽15 000 (≈ $163) to ₽30 000 (≈ $326) one-way, depending on the season. Aeroflot often has sales, so keep an eye on their website.
  • Pobeda — The budget king. Pobeda flies from Moscow (VKO) to Irkutsk, but only a few times a week. Prices start as low as ₽5 000 (≈ $54) one-way if you book months in advance—but add luggage fees (around ₽2 000 / ≈ $22 per bag).
  • Ural Airlines — Direct flights from Yekaterinburg (SVX) and St. Petersburg (LED) to Irkutsk. From Yekaterinburg, prices are about ₽10 000 (≈ $109) to ₽18 000 (≈ $196) one-way. From St. Petersburg, expect ₽15 000 (≈ $163) to ₽28 000 (≈ $305).
  • NordStar and Yamal Airlines — Seasonal direct flights from Krasnoyarsk (KJA) and Norilsk (NSK) to Irkutsk. These are less frequent but can be cheap: around ₽6 000 (≈ $65) to ₽12 000 (≈ $130) one-way.

If you're flying from outside Russia, you'll likely connect through Moscow or Novosibirsk. Check Aviasales or Hotellook (for bundled flight+hotel deals) to compare all airlines at once—they aggregate prices from dozens of carriers, including low-cost options.

Connecting Flights

Connecting flights are often the cheapest way to reach Irkutsk, especially if you're flexible with layovers. Here are the best budget-friendly routes:

  • Via Novosibirsk (OVB) — Novosibirsk is a major hub for S7 Airlines. A flight from Moscow to Novosibirsk (about ₽4 000 / ≈ $43) plus Novosibirsk to Irkutsk (₽8 000 / ≈ $87) can total ₽12 000 (≈ $130) round-trip. Total travel time: 7-9 hours (including layover).
  • Via Krasnoyarsk (KJA) — Similar to Novosibirsk, but often cheaper. Flights from Moscow to Krasnoyarsk (₽5 000 / ≈ $54) plus Krasnoyarsk to Irkutsk (₽3 000 / ≈ $33) = ₽8 000 (≈ $87) one-way. Layover times vary from 2 to 6 hours.
  • Via Yekaterinburg (SVX) — Good if you're coming from Europe. Ural Airlines and S7 offer connections. Moscow to Yekaterinburg (₽3 000 / ≈ $33) plus Yekaterinburg to Irkutsk (₽10 000 / ≈ $109) = ₽13 000 (≈ $141) one-way. Duration: 8-10 hours.
  • Via Vladivostok (VVO) — If you're traveling from East Asia (e.g., Tokyo, Seoul, Beijing), consider flying to Vladivostok first. Flights from Seoul to Vladivostok (₽10 000 / ≈ $109) plus Vladivostok to Irkutsk (₽8 000 / ≈ $87) = ₽18 000 (≈ $196) one-way. Total time: 10-14 hours.

Remember that flights can be compared across dates using flexible search tools on Aviasales or Booking (which also offers flight search). I always set a price alert for my desired route—Aviasales will email you when prices drop. For example, a flight from Moscow to Irkutsk in March might be ₽8 000 (≈ $87) one-way, but in July it jumps to ₽25 000 (≈ $272). Patience pays off.

10 Hacks for Cheap Flights

Ready to save serious money? Here are my top 10 hacks, tested and proven:

  1. Be flexible with airports. Instead of Moscow, consider flying from St. Petersburg (LED) or Kazan (KZN). A flight from Kazan to Irkutsk via S7 can be ₽6 000 (≈ $65) one-way—half the Moscow price. Use Aviasales' "Everywhere" search to find the cheapest departure city near you.
  2. Book connecting flights separately. Sometimes booking Moscow-Novosibirsk and Novosibirsk-Irkutsk as separate tickets is cheaper than a single itinerary. Just leave at least 3 hours between flights to avoid missed connections. Check both options on Aviasales.
  3. Use incognito mode. Airlines and booking sites track your searches and may raise prices if they see you're interested. Always search in private/incognito mode, or clear your cookies.
  4. Fly on Tuesdays and Wednesdays. Midweek flights are consistently cheaper. For example, a Tuesday flight from Moscow to Irkutsk might be ₽8 000 (≈ $87), while a Friday flight is ₽15 000 (≈ $163).
  5. Book 6-8 weeks in advance. For domestic Russian flights, the sweet spot is 45-60 days before departure. Last-minute deals are rare for Irkutsk, so plan ahead.
  6. Consider budget airlines with add-ons. Pobeda's base fares are tiny, but luggage costs ₽2 000 (≈ $22) per bag. If you travel light (carry-on only), you can fly Moscow-Irkutsk for ₽5 000 (≈ $54) one-way. But if you need checked luggage, S7 might be cheaper overall.
  7. Use a VPN to search from different countries. Sometimes prices appear lower when searching from a Russian IP address vs. a foreign one. Try a VPN set to Moscow—I've seen differences of ₽2 000 (≈ $22) on the same flight.
  8. Sign up for airline newsletters. Aeroflot, S7, and Pobeda often send promo codes for 10-20% off. Also follow them on Telegram for flash sales.
  9. Combine flights with a hotel. Sites like Hotellook and Agoda sometimes offer bundle discounts. For example, a flight+hotel package to Irkutsk might save you ₽3 000 (≈ $33) compared to booking separately.
  10. Check alternative airports near Olkhon. Ulan-Ude (UUD) is about 400 km east of Olkhon, but flights there can be cheaper. From Ulan-Ude, take a bus to Irkutsk (₽1 500 / ≈ $16) and then to Olkhon. Flights from Moscow to Ulan-Ude start at ₽9 000 (≈ $98) one-way—worth considering if you have time.

When to Book

Timing is everything. Here's my rule of thumb for booking flights to Irkutsk in 2026:

  • For summer travel (June-August): Book by March or April. Prices start climbing in May. I once booked a July flight in February and paid ₽12 000 (≈ $130) one-way—my friend booked in June and paid ₽28 000 (≈ $305).
  • For winter travel (December-February): Book 2-3 months ahead. Winter is less popular, so you'll find deals even a month before. But if you want the ice road (usually February), book by December to secure your spot.
  • For shoulder seasons (March-May, September-November): You can book as late as 2 weeks before and still find good prices. In April 2025, I saw Moscow-Irkutsk for ₽6 000 (≈ $65) one-way just 10 days out.
  • Holiday periods: New Year's (late December) and May holidays (early May) are expensive. Book at least 4 months ahead for these periods, or avoid them entirely.

One more tip: use Kiwitaxi for airport transfers once you land in Irkutsk—it's often cheaper than a taxi from the airport rank. And if you need mobile data while traveling, grab an eSIM from Airalo before you leave; it's affordable and works instantly.

I hope this guide helps you find the perfect flight to Olkhon Island in 2026. Whether you're chasing the summer sun or the winter ice, a little planning goes a long way. Happy travels!
