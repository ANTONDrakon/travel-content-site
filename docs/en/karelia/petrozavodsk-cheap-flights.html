Petrozavodsk Airport (PES): What You Need to Know

Petrozavodsk Airport (PES), also known as Besovets Airport, is located about 12 kilometers west of the city center. It's a compact airport with limited international flights, but it handles domestic routes efficiently. Airlines operating to PES include Severstal Aircompany, RusLine, and occasional charters. Direct Flights to Petrozavodsk

Direct flights to Petrozavodsk are limited but convenient. Here are the main routes and estimated prices for 2026:

  • Moscow (SVO, DME, VKO) to Petrozavodsk (PES): Operated by Severstal Aircompany and RusLine. Estimated round-trip price: ₽12 000 – ₽18 000 (≈ $130 – $195). Flight time: ~1.5 hours.
  • Saint Petersburg (LED) to Petrozavodsk (PES): Seasonal direct flights by Severstal Aircompany. Estimated round-trip price: ₽8 000 – ₽14 000 (≈ $87 – $152). Flight time: ~1 hour.
  • Murmansk (MMK) to Petrozavodsk (PES): Occasional direct charters. Estimated round-trip price: ₽10 000 – ₽16 000 (≈ $109 – $174). Flight time: ~1.5 hours.

Direct flights are ideal if you're short on time, but they often cost more than connecting options. For the best rates, use Hotellook to compare flight and hotel packages — you might find a combo deal that saves money.

Connecting Flights: Cheapest Options

Connecting flights to Petrozavodsk are often significantly cheaper, especially if you're flexible with layovers. Here are the most affordable routes for 2026:

  • Via Moscow: From most Russian cities (e.g., Kazan, Yekaterinburg, Sochi), fly to Moscow (SVO, DME, or VKO) then connect to PES. Estimated round-trip price: ₽7 000 – ₽15 000 (≈ $76 – $163). Total duration: 3–6 hours depending on layover.
  • Via Saint Petersburg: From European Russia or Baltic countries, fly to LED then to PES. Estimated round-trip price: ₽6 000 – ₽12 000 (≈ $65 – $130). Duration: 2.5–5 hours.
  • Via Helsinki (HEL): For international travelers, fly to Helsinki, then take a connecting flight (often with Finnair codeshare) to PES. Estimated round-trip price: ₽15 000 – ₽25 000 (≈ $163 – $272). Duration: 4–8 hours.

10 Hacks for Cheap Flights to Petrozavodsk

Here are my top strategies to save money on your 2026 flight to Petrozavodsk, tested and proven with real services:

  1. Use Aviasales for Price Alerts: Set up alerts for PES from your home airport. You'll get notified when prices drop — sometimes by 30-40% during sales. It's free and easy.
  2. Book Connecting Flights via Saint Petersburg: As noted, flying via LED can save you up to 50% compared to direct flights. Check Aviasales for multi-city options.
  3. Fly on Tuesdays or Wednesdays: Midweek flights are typically cheaper than weekend departures. For example, a Tuesday flight from Moscow to PES might cost ₽10 000 (≈ $109) vs. ₽15 000 (≈ $163) on Friday.
  4. Combine with a Hotel Package: Use Hotellook to bundle your flight and accommodation. Sometimes the combo saves ₽3 000–₽5 000 (≈ $33–$54) compared to booking separately.
  5. Check Budget Airlines for Connecting Routes: While no budget airlines fly directly to PES, consider low-cost carriers like Pobeda for the first leg (e.g., to Moscow). Then book a separate ticket to PES. Total cost: ₽8 000–₽12 000 (≈ $87–$130).
  6. Use Kiwitaxi for Airport Transfers: Once you land, pre-book a transfer via Kiwitaxi to avoid taxi surcharges. A ride from PES to city center costs around ₽1 500 (≈ $16) if booked in advance.
  7. Book 2–3 Months in Advance: For domestic flights, booking 60–90 days ahead often yields the best prices. Last-minute deals are rare for PES due to limited capacity.
  8. Consider Nearby Airports: If PES is expensive, fly to Helsinki (HEL) or Saint Petersburg (LED) and take a train or bus to Petrozavodsk. The train from LED takes about 6 hours and costs ₽2 000–₽3 500 (≈ $22–$38).
  9. Use Airalo for eSIM Data: Stay connected without roaming fees. Download an Airalo eSIM for Russia (about ₽800 (≈ $9) for 1GB) to access flight apps and maps on the go.
  10. Check GetYourGuide for Activities: After booking your flight, use GetYourGuide to reserve tours (like Kizhi Island day trips) in advance — often cheaper than buying locally, and you can plan around your flight times.

When to Book: Optimal Timing for 2026

Timing is everything when it comes to cheap flights. Based on historical data for Petrozavodsk, here's my advice for 2026:

  • For summer travel (June–August): Book by March 2026. Prices rise sharply after April, so early booking is crucial. Expect to pay ₽18 000–₽20 000 (≈ $195–$217) from Moscow.
  • For shoulder seasons (April–May, September–October): Book 6–8 weeks in advance. Prices are moderate, and you might find flash sales in February or August.
  • For winter (November–March): Book 4–6 weeks ahead. Demand is low, so last-minute deals sometimes appear — but don't wait too long, as flights can sell out during holidays like New Year's.
  • Best day to book: Tuesday or Wednesday, around 3 PM local time. Airlines often release sales early in the week.

Remember to use Aviasales to compare prices across dates — their calendar view is a lifesaver. For example, a flight from Moscow on April 15 might cost ₽11 000 (≈ $120), while the same route on April 20 jumps to ₽16 000 (≈ $174). Flexibility pays off!

Final Tips for a Smooth Trip

Once you've secured your cheap flight, here are a few extras to make your Petrozavodsk visit seamless:

  • Visa requirements: If you're flying from abroad, check if you need a Russian visa. Most nationalities require one, so apply at least 4 weeks in advance.
  • Packing for weather: Petrozavodsk can be chilly even in summer (average 15–20°C in July). Bring layers and a waterproof jacket.
  • Local transport: From PES, take bus #7 or a taxi to the city center. Buses cost about ₽100 (≈ $1), while taxis are ₽1 000–₽1 500 (≈ $11–$16).
