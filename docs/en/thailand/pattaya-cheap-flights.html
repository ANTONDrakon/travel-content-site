How to Find Cheap Flights to Pattaya 2026

If you’re dreaming of sandy beaches, vibrant nightlife, and affordable luxury, Pattaya should be at the top of your list. But before you can sip a cocktail on Jomtien Beach, you need to get there—and flights can sometimes eat up your budget.

I’ve been helping travelers find cheap flights for years, and I’m here to share everything you need to know about scoring affordable tickets to Pattaya in 2026. Whether you’re flying from Moscow, Bangkok, or halfway across the world, I’ll guide you through the best airports, airlines, booking hacks, and timing tips. Let’s dive in!

Pattaya itself doesn’t have a major international airport, but you’ll likely fly into U-Tapao Rayong-Pattaya International Airport (UTP), located about 45 minutes south of the city. It’s smaller and less crowded than Bangkok’s Suvarnabhumi, making it a convenient option. Some travelers also fly into Bangkok (BKK) and take a bus or taxi (about 1.5–2 hours), but for this guide, we’ll focus on UTP since it’s closer and often cheaper for certain routes.

Direct Flights to Pattaya (UTP)

Direct flights to UTP are limited but growing, especially from Asian hubs. Here are the main airlines and estimated prices for 2026:

  • Thai AirAsia – Flies from Kuala Lumpur (KUL), Singapore (SIN), and some Chinese cities like Guangzhou (CAN). Prices start around ₽8 000 (≈ $85) one-way from Kuala Lumpur, and ₽12 000 (≈ $130) from Singapore.
  • Bangkok Airways – Offers flights from Samui (USM) and Phuket (HKT) to UTP. Expect to pay ₽10 000 (≈ $110) one-way from Samui, and ₽14 000 (≈ $150) from Phuket.
  • Air Asia (Malaysia) – Seasonal direct flights from Kuala Lumpur, often as low as ₽6 000 (≈ $65) if booked early.
  • Utair – Occasionally charters from Russia (e.g., Moscow VKO) during peak seasons, with prices around ₽25 000 (≈ $270) one-way. Check Aviasales for updated schedules.

Direct flights are rare from Europe or the Americas, so most travelers will use connecting options. Use Aviasales to search for direct routes—they aggregate all airlines and show the cheapest dates.

Connecting Flights to Pattaya

For most destinations, connecting flights are the most affordable way to reach Pattaya. You’ll typically fly into Bangkok’s Suvarnabhumi (BKK) or Don Mueang (DMK) airports, then take a bus, taxi, or short domestic flight to UTP. But you can also book a single itinerary to UTP via a hub like Kuala Lumpur, Singapore, or Dubai. Here are the cheapest options:

  • From Europe (e.g., Moscow, London, Berlin) – Fly with Turkish Airlines via Istanbul (IST), then connect to Bangkok (BKK). From BKK, take a bus to Pattaya (≈ ₽1 000 / $11). Total flight cost: ₽30 000–₽40 000 (≈ $325–$435) round-trip. Alternatively, fly to Kuala Lumpur with AirAsia, then connect to UTP for around ₽8 000 (≈ $85) extra.
  • From Russia (e.g., Moscow, St. Petersburg) – Direct flights to Bangkok with Aeroflot or S7 start at ₽25 000 (≈ $270) one-way in low season. From Bangkok, take a bus (≈ ₽1 000 / $11) or a domestic flight to UTP (≈ ₽5 000 / $55). Best deals on Aviasales.
  • From Asia (e.g., Seoul, Tokyo, Shanghai) – Fly to Bangkok with budget carriers like AirAsia or Scoot for ₽12 000–₽18 000 (≈ $130–$195) one-way. Then, a bus to Pattaya is just ₽1 000 ($11).
  • From Australia (e.g., Sydney, Melbourne) – Jetstar and AirAsia offer flights to Bangkok via Kuala Lumpur or Singapore for ₽25 000–₽35 000 (≈ $270–$380) one-way.

10 Hacks for Cheap Flights to Pattaya

After years of trial and error, here are my top 10 tips to save money on your Pattaya flight in 2026:

  1. Use Incognito Mode – Airlines and booking sites track your searches. Open a private browser window to avoid price hikes. Then check Aviasales for the best deals.
  2. Fly to Bangkok Instead of UTP – UTP direct flights are often pricier. Flying to BKK and taking a bus (≈ ₽1 000 / $11) or taxi (≈ ₽2 500 / $27) can save you ₽5 000–₽10 000.
  3. Book Connecting Flights via Kuala Lumpur – AirAsia and Malaysia Airlines offer cheap connections. A flight from Moscow to KL with a stop in Dubai costs around ₽20 000 (≈ $215), then a separate ticket to UTP for ₽6 000 (≈ $65). Use Aviasales to combine.
  4. Travel in Low Season – September and October are cheapest. I booked a flight for ₽22 000 (≈ $240) round-trip from Moscow to Bangkok last September.
  5. Use Budget Airlines for Short Hops – For domestic connections (BKK to UTP), try Nok Air or Thai AirAsia. Prices start at ₽3 000 (≈ $33) one-way. Book on Aviasales or directly on the airline’s site.
  6. Set Price Alerts – On Aviasales, you can set alerts for your route. I got a notification for a ₽19 000 flight to Bangkok in May—snapped it up immediately!
  7. Consider Nearby Airports – If you’re in Asia, check flights to Don Mueang (DMK) instead of Suvarnabhumi. From DMK, buses to Pattaya cost ₽1 000 ($11).
  8. Book a Package with Hotels – Sometimes flight+hotel deals on Hotellook or Booking are cheaper than separate bookings. For example, a 7-night stay at a 4-star hotel in Pattaya with flights from Moscow might cost ₽45 000 (≈ $490) total.
  9. Use Points and Miles – If you have credit card rewards, transfer them to airline partners like Aeroflot Bonus or Thai Royal Orchid Plus. A round-trip from Moscow to Bangkok can be as low as ₽5 000 in taxes.
  10. Check for Student or Youth Discounts – If you’re under 26, some airlines like AirAsia offer youth fares. Also, student travel sites like STA Travel often have deals.

When to Book

Timing is everything. For flights to Pattaya (via Bangkok), here’s my rule of thumb:

  • For high season (November–February, July–August): Book at least 3–4 months in advance. Prices spike 2 weeks before departure. I booked my January flight in October and saved ₽8 000.
  • For low season (March–June, September–October): Book 6–8 weeks ahead. Last-minute deals can appear, but they’re risky. I once found a ₽20 000 round-trip from Moscow to Bangkok just 3 weeks before departure in September.
  • Best day to book: Tuesday or Wednesday at 3–4 AM (airline time). Airlines often release new deals early in the week.
  • Use Aviasales calendar: Their “Cheapest Month” feature shows you the lowest prices across the year. For 2026, September looks like the sweet spot.

Flight Insurance and Compensation

Even with careful planning, delays and cancellations happen—especially with budget airlines. Here’s what to do:

  • Travel insurance: I always use Airalo for eSIM data, but for insurance, check get travel insurance. They offer policies covering flight delays, cancellations, and lost baggage. For example, a ₽5 000 policy (≈ $55) might cover up to ₽30 000 (≈ $325) in delay compensation.
  • EU Regulation 261/2004: If you’re flying from the EU, you may be entitled to compensation (up to $686 (€600)) for delays over 3 hours or cancellations. File a claim through services like AirHelp or directly with the airline.
  • For Thai domestic flights: If your flight from BKK to UTP is delayed, Thai law requires airlines to provide meals and accommodation for delays over 4 hours. Keep your boarding pass and receipts.
  • What to do if delayed: Contact the airline immediately, ask for written confirmation of the delay, and save all receipts (food, hotel, transport). Then submit a claim to get travel insurance for reimbursement.

I once had a 6-hour delay on AirAsia from Bangkok to UTP—they gave me a ₽500 meal voucher and I claimed ₽5 000 from my insurance. It’s worth it!

Final Thoughts

Finding cheap flights to Pattaya in 2026 is all about flexibility, timing, and using the right tools. Start your search on Aviasales to compare prices, then check Hotellook for accommodation deals. For local transport, Kiwitaxi offers affordable transfers from Bangkok to Pattaya (from ₽2 500 / $27). And don’t forget an eSIM from Airalo to stay connected—I use their Thailand plan for ₽500 (≈ $5.50) for 5GB. Happy travels, and I’ll see you on the beach!
