15 Best Things to Do in Koh Samui 2026

Koh Samui is more than just a beach paradise — it’s an island where ancient temples meet buzzing night markets, jungle waterfalls hide behind coconut groves, and every sunset feels like a private show. I’ve spent weeks exploring this gem in the Gulf of Thailand, and I’m excited to share the best things to do here in 2026. Whether you’re a first-time visitor or a returning fan, this guide will help you plan an unforgettable trip. Let’s dive in!

15 Best Attractions

From iconic landmarks to hidden gems, here are the top 15 attractions in Koh Samui. Each includes practical details to make your visit smooth and enjoyable.

1. Big Buddha Temple (Wat Phra Yai)

This 12-meter golden Buddha sits on a small island connected by a causeway, visible from miles away. The serene atmosphere and panoramic views of the coastline make it a must-visit. Climb the stairs for a closer look and a blessing.

  • Ticket price: Бесплатно (free, donations welcome)
  • Hours: Daily 6:00 AM – 6:00 PM
  • How to get there: Take a songthaew (shared taxi) from Chaweng or Bophut for about ₽200 (≈ 80 THB).
  • Tip: Visit early morning to avoid crowds and heat.

2. Wat Plai Laem

A colorful Chinese-style temple complex featuring a striking 18-arm Guanyin statue. The lake around the temple is filled with fish you can feed. It’s a peaceful spot for photos and reflection.

  • Ticket price: Бесплатно (free)
  • Hours: Daily 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
  • How to get there: Walk from Big Buddha (15 minutes) or take a songthaew from Chaweng for ₽150 (≈ 60 THB).
  • Tip: Combine with Big Buddha for a half-day temple tour.

3. Na Muang Waterfalls

Two waterfalls in the jungle — Na Muang 1 is easy to reach, while Na Muang 2 requires a short hike. The water is cool and refreshing, perfect for a dip. The surrounding landscape is lush and photogenic.

  • Ticket price: ₽100 (≈ 40 THB) for parking or entry to the nature park
  • Hours: Daily 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
  • How to get there: Rent a scooter from Lamai or Chaweng (₽300/day ≈ 120 THB) or join a tour.
  • Tip: Bring sturdy shoes for the hike to Na Muang 2.

4. Hin Ta and Hin Yai Rocks

These natural rock formations on Lamai Beach are famous for their phallic shapes, but the area also offers great views and a small market. It’s a quirky stop that locals love to joke about.

  • Ticket price: Бесплатно (free)
  • Hours: Always open
  • How to get there: Walk from Lamai Beach or take a songthaew for ₽100 (≈ 40 THB).
  • Tip: Visit at sunset for fewer tourists and better light.

5. Ang Thong National Marine Park

A stunning archipelago of 42 islands with emerald lagoons, limestone cliffs, and snorkeling spots. The park is a protected area, so expect pristine nature. Kayaking and hiking are popular activities.

  • Ticket price: ₽1 000 (≈ 400 THB) for adults, ₽500 (≈ 200 THB) for children (includes boat transfer and lunch)
  • Hours: Day trips typically 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM
  • How to get there: Book a tour from any travel agency in Samui (₽3 500/≈ 1 400 THB per person).
  • Tip: Bring sunscreen and a waterproof bag.

6. Fisherman's Village (Bophut)

A charming area with wooden Chinese shophouses, boutiques, and restaurants. Every Friday, it hosts a walking street market with food, crafts, and live music. The vibe is relaxed and romantic.

  • Ticket price: Бесплатно (free)
  • Hours: Market Fridays 5:00 PM – 10:00 PM
  • How to get there: Songthaew from Chaweng for ₽200 (≈ 80 THB).
  • Tip: Arrive early for parking and grab a seat at a beachfront bar.

7. Samui Elephant Sanctuary

An ethical sanctuary where rescued elephants roam freely. You can feed, bathe, and observe them without riding. It’s a heartwarming experience that supports conservation.

  • Ticket price: ₽3 000 (≈ 1 200 THB) per person for a half-day visit
  • Hours: Tours at 8:00 AM and 1:00 PM
  • How to get there: Free pickup from your hotel included in the tour.
  • Tip: Book in advance via GetYourGuide to secure a spot.

8. Secret Buddha Garden

A hidden garden in the hills of Koh Samui, filled with statues depicting Buddhist and Hindu mythology. The views over the island are breathtaking. It’s a bit off the beaten path, which adds to the charm.

  • Ticket price: ₽300 (≈ 120 THB)
  • Hours: Daily 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
  • How to get there: Rent a 4WD or join a tour from Lamai (₽1 500/≈ 600 THB).
  • Tip: The road is steep — consider hiring a driver.

9. Lad Koh Viewpoint

One of the best sunset spots on the island, overlooking the southwest coast. The view from the cliff is expansive, with islands dotting the horizon. It’s free and easily accessible.

  • Ticket price: Бесплатно (free)
  • Hours: Always open
  • How to get there: Drive or take a songthaew from Lamai (₽150/≈ 60 THB).
  • Tip: Bring a camera and arrive 30 minutes before sunset.

10. Samui Aquarium and Tiger Zoo

A family-friendly attraction with marine life and tiger shows. While some animal lovers may have concerns, the aquarium offers educational exhibits. It’s a popular choice for kids.

  • Ticket price: ₽750 (≈ 300 THB) for adults, ₽350 (≈ 140 THB) for children
  • Hours: Daily 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM
  • How to get there: Songthaew from Chaweng for ₽250 (≈ 100 THB).
  • Tip: Check reviews online before visiting.

11. Coco Splash Adventure & Waterpark

A large waterpark with slides, wave pools, and lazy rivers. Perfect for cooling off on a hot day. It’s clean, well-maintained, and fun for all ages.

  • Ticket price: ₽1 200 (≈ 480 THB) for adults, ₽800 (≈ 320 THB) for children
  • Hours: Daily 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM
  • How to get there: Free shuttle from Chaweng or taxi for ₽300 (≈ 120 THB).
  • Tip: Bring your own towel and sunscreen.

12. Overlap Stone

A unique rock formation on Lamai Beach where a large boulder balances on another. It’s a popular photo spot, especially at sunrise. The area is quiet and less crowded.

  • Ticket price: Бесплатно (free)
  • Hours: Always open
  • How to get there: Walk from Lamai Beach (10 minutes).
  • Tip: Go early to avoid crowds.

13. Samui Cultural Center

An interactive museum showcasing the island’s history, from ancient settlements to modern tourism. Exhibits include artifacts, photos, and traditional costumes. It’s a great rainy-day activity.

  • Ticket price: ₽200 (≈ 80 THB)
  • Hours: Tuesday–Sunday 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM
  • How to get there: Songthaew from Nathon for ₽100 (≈ 40 THB).
  • Tip: Combine with a visit to Nathon Town.

14. Chaweng Night Market

A lively market on the main street of Chaweng, offering street food, clothes, and souvenirs. The energy is electric, with music and crowds. Try local dishes like pad thai and mango sticky rice.

  • Ticket price: Бесплатно (free entry, food from ₽100/≈ 40 THB)
  • Hours: Daily 5:00 PM – 11:00 PM
  • How to get there: Walk from any hotel in Chaweng.
  • Tip: Bargain politely for souvenirs.

15. Magic Alambic Rum Distillery

A boutique distillery producing premium rum from local sugarcane. Tours include tastings and a look at the production process. The setting is rustic and charming.

  • Ticket price: ₽500 (≈ 200 THB) for a tour and tasting
  • Hours: Tours at 11:00 AM and 2:00 PM
  • How to get there: Rent a scooter from Lamai (15 minutes) or book a taxi for ₽600 (≈ 240 THB).
  • Tip: Buy a bottle as a souvenir — it’s cheaper than in stores.

1-Day, 2-Day, 3-Day Itineraries

Here are optimal routes to maximize your time on Koh Samui.

1-Day Itinerary

  • Morning: Visit Big Buddha and Wat Plai Laem (free).
  • Lunch: Street food at Fisherman’s Village (₽300/≈ 120 THB).
  • Afternoon: Na Muang Waterfalls (₽100/≈ 40 THB).
  • Evening: Sunset at Lad Koh Viewpoint, then dinner at Chaweng Night Market.

2-Day Itinerary

  • Day 1: Same as 1-day, plus an evening at Fisherman’s Village market (if Friday).
  • Day 2: Full-day trip to Ang Thong Marine Park (₽1 000/≈ 400 THB). Return by 4 PM, relax on Lamai Beach.

3-Day Itinerary

  • Day 1: Temples and waterfalls.
  • Day 2: Ang Thong Marine Park.
  • Day 3: Samui Elephant Sanctuary (₽3 000/≈ 1 200 THB) in the morning, Secret Buddha Garden (₽300/≈ 120 THB) in the afternoon, and dinner at a beachfront restaurant in Bophut.

Free Attractions

You don’t need a big budget to enjoy Koh Samui:

  • Big Buddha Temple — free entry, donations optional.
  • Wat Plai Laem — free, with fish feeding for a small fee.
  • Hin Ta and Hin Yai Rocks — free, with market stalls.
  • Fisherman’s Village — free to explore, market on Fridays.
  • Lad Koh Viewpoint — free sunset spot.
  • Overlap Stone — free photo opportunity.
  • Chaweng Night Market — free entry, food costs extra.
  • Beaches — Chaweng, Lamai, Bophut, and Maenam are all free.

Practical Tips

Transportation

  • Songthaews: Shared taxis cost ₽100–₽300 (≈ 40–120 THB) per ride. Negotiate before boarding.
  • Scooter rental: ₽300/day (≈ 120 THB) — popular but requires an international driving permit.
  • Private taxis: ₽500–₽1 000 (≈ 200–400 THB) for longer trips. Book via Kiwitaxi for fixed prices.
  • Airport transfers: Use Kiwitaxi for ₽1 500 (≈ 600 THB) to Chaweng.

Combo Tickets

Some attractions offer combo deals. For example:

  • Samui Aquarium + Tiger Zoo: ₽1 000 (≈ 400 THB) for both (save ₽100).
  • Secret Buddha Garden + waterfall tour: ₽500 (≈ 200 THB) via local agencies.

Check Booking.com for hotel packages that include attraction tickets.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do attractions cost in Koh Samui?

Do I need a guide in Koh Samui?

A guide is recommended for historical sites and complex routes. Self-guided travel is possible with audio guides and maps.
