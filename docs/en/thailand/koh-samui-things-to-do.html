Koh Samui has always been a tropical dream, but in 2026, it’s more than just beaches and coconuts. The island buzzes with a mix of natural wonders, cultural gems, and modern attractions that make every moment feel like a postcard.

Whether you’re a first-timer or a returning traveler, the attractions here range from serene temples to adrenaline-pumping water parks. I’ve spent countless hours exploring these spots, and I’m excited to share the 15 best things to do that’ll make your trip unforgettable. Let’s dive in!

15 Best Attractions

1. Big Buddha Temple (Wat Phra Yai)

This iconic 12-meter gold Buddha sits majestically on a small island off the coast, accessible by a causeway. The temple complex is serene, with locals offering prayers and incense. It’s a top spot for photos and a quick cultural immersion.

  • Ticket Price: Бесплатно (Free)
  • Hours: Daily 08:00–18:00
  • How to Get There: Take a songthaew (shared taxi) from Chaweng for about ₽200–₽300 (≈ $2–$3). Or rent a scooter for ₽500 (≈ $5) per day.
  • Tip: Visit early morning to avoid crowds and heat. Dress modestly—cover shoulders and knees.

2. Wat Plai Laem

A colorful temple complex featuring a striking 18-arm Guanyin statue and a laughing Buddha. The lake surrounding the temple is filled with fish you can feed. It’s a peaceful spot with intricate architecture and a lively market nearby.

  • Ticket Price: Бесплатно (Free)
  • Hours: Daily 08:00–17:00
  • How to Get There: A 5-minute drive from Big Buddha. Rent a scooter or take a songthaew for ₽200 (≈ $2).
  • Tip: Bring small change (₽50–₽100) for fish food—kids love it.

3. Ang Thong National Marine Park

A stunning archipelago of 42 islands, this marine park is a paradise for kayaking, snorkeling, and hiking. Emerald lagoons and limestone cliffs create a dramatic landscape. It’s a full-day adventure that feels like stepping into "The Beach."

  • Ticket Price: ₽1 500 (≈ $16) for park entry; tours start at ₽4 500 (≈ $48)
  • Hours: Tours typically 08:00–17:00
  • How to Get There: Book a speedboat tour from Nathon Pier. Many operators pick you up from your hotel.
  • Tip: Bring reef-safe sunscreen and a waterproof bag. The hike to the lagoon viewpoint is steep but worth it.

4. Na Muang Waterfalls

Two waterfalls (Na Muang 1 and 2) cascade through lush jungle. Na Muang 2 requires a short hike but rewards you with a natural pool for swimming. The water is cool and refreshing, perfect after a trek.

  • Ticket Price: Бесплатно (Free)
  • Hours: Daily 08:00–17:00
  • How to Get There: Drive from Lamai (15 minutes) or take a songthaew for ₽300 (≈ $3).
  • Tip: Wear sturdy shoes for the hike to Na Muang 2. Avoid weekends for fewer crowds.

5. Secret Buddha Garden (Magic Garden)

Hidden in the hills, this garden is filled with whimsical statues created by a local farmer. It’s a peaceful, quirky spot with panoramic views. The garden feels like a secret world of art and nature.

  • Ticket Price: ₽500 (≈ $5)
  • Hours: Daily 08:00–17:00
  • How to Get There: Hire a 4x4 taxi from Lamai for ₽1 500 (≈ $16) round trip. The road is steep.
  • Tip: Combine with a visit to the nearby Tarnim Magic Garden viewpoint.

6. Chaweng Beach

The most famous beach on the island, with powdery white sand and crystal-clear water. It’s lined with bars, restaurants, and water sports. Perfect for sunbathing, swimming, or a sunset cocktail.

  • Ticket Price: Бесплатно (Free)
  • Hours: Always open
  • How to Get There: Walk from most hotels in Chaweng. Songthaews from other areas cost ₽200–₽400 (≈ $2–$4).
  • Tip: Head to the northern end for quieter spots. Rent a jet ski for ₽2 000 (≈ $21) per hour.

7. Fisherman's Village (Bophut)

A charming area with wooden Chinese shophouses, boutiques, and a night market every Friday. The vibe is relaxed, with beachfront restaurants serving fresh seafood. It’s a cultural and culinary hotspot.

  • Ticket Price: Бесплатно (Free)
  • Hours: Shops open 10:00–22:00; Friday market 17:00–23:00
  • How to Get There: Songthaew from Chaweng for ₽300 (≈ $3).
  • Tip: Visit on Friday for the market—try the grilled squid and mango sticky rice.

8. Lamai Beach

A more laid-back alternative to Chaweng, with soft sand and a relaxed vibe. The southern end has the famous "Grandmother and Grandfather" rocks. It’s great for swimming and people-watching.

  • Ticket Price: Бесплатно (Free)
  • Hours: Always open
  • How to Get There: Songthaew from Chaweng for ₽300 (≈ $3).
  • Tip: The rocks are fun to see, but the real charm is the beach itself.

9. Wat Khunaram (Mummified Monk)

This temple houses the mummified body of a monk who died in meditation in 1973. It’s a fascinating and respectful site, with the monk’s body preserved in a glass case. The temple grounds are serene.

  • Ticket Price: Бесплатно (Free)
  • Hours: Daily 08:00–17:00
  • How to Get There: Drive from Lamai (10 minutes) or take a songthaew for ₽200 (≈ $2).
  • Tip: Photography is allowed, but be respectful. Donations are welcome.

10. Samui Elephant Sanctuary

An ethical sanctuary where elephants roam freely in a natural habitat. You can feed, bathe, and observe them without riding. It’s a heartwarming experience that supports conservation.

  • Ticket Price: ₽3 500 (≈ $37) for a half-day visit
  • Hours: Tours at 08:00 and 13:00
  • How to Get There: Free pickup from your hotel included in the ticket.
  • Tip: Book in advance online—spaces fill quickly. Wear comfortable clothes you don’t mind getting muddy.

11. Hin Ta and Hin Yai (Grandfather and Grandmother Rocks)

These natural rock formations resemble male and female genitalia, and they’re a popular photo stop. The surrounding area has souvenir stalls and a small beach. It’s quirky and fun.

  • Ticket Price: Бесплатно (Free)
  • Hours: Always open
  • How to Get There: Walk from Lamai Beach (15 minutes) or take a songthaew for ₽100 (≈ $1).
  • Tip: Go at low tide for better views of the rocks.

12. Overlap Stone (Hin Lad Waterfall)

A hidden gem near Lamai, this waterfall has a natural pool surrounded by jungle. It’s less crowded than Na Muang and offers a peaceful escape. The hike is short but rewarding.

  • Ticket Price: Бесплатно (Free)
  • Hours: Daily 08:00–17:00
  • How to Get There: Drive from Lamai (10 minutes) or hike from the road.
  • Tip: Bring insect repellent—the jungle has mosquitoes.

13. Samui Aquarium and Tiger Zoo

A family-friendly attraction with a large aquarium, tiger shows, and a bird park. The aquarium features local marine life, including sharks and turtles. It’s a fun half-day outing.

  • Ticket Price: ₽1 200 (≈ $13) for adults, ₽600 (≈ $6) for children
  • Hours: Daily 09:00–17:00
  • How to Get There: Songthaew from Chaweng for ₽400 (≈ $4).
  • Tip: Skip the tiger show if you’re concerned about animal welfare—focus on the aquarium.

14. Coco Splash Adventure & Waterpark

A large water park with slides, wave pools, and a lazy river. It’s perfect for families and thrill-seekers. The park is clean and well-maintained, with plenty of sun loungers.

  • Ticket Price: ₽2 500 (≈ $27) for adults, ₽1 500 (≈ $16) for children
  • Hours: Daily 10:00–18:00
  • How to Get There: Free shuttle from Chaweng and Lamai. Check their website for pickup points.
  • Tip: Bring your own towel and sunscreen. Lockers cost ₽300 (≈ $3).

15. Lad Koh Viewpoint

A scenic viewpoint overlooking the southern coast, with panoramic views of the ocean and islands. It’s a popular sunset spot, with a small café for drinks. The drive up is scenic itself.

  • Ticket Price: Бесплатно (Free)
  • Hours: Always open
  • How to Get There: Drive from Lamai (20 minutes) or take a songthaew for ₽400 (≈ $4).
  • Tip: Arrive by 17:00 to grab a good spot for sunset. Bring a camera.

1-Day, 2-Day, 3-Day Itineraries

1-Day Itinerary: Samui Highlights

Start at Big Buddha (08:00–09:00), then Wat Plai Laem (09:15–10:00). Head to Na Muang Waterfalls (10:30–12:00). Lunch at a local restaurant (₽500–₽800). Afternoon at Chaweng Beach (13:00–16:00). End with sunset at Lad Koh Viewpoint (17:00–18:00). Dinner at Fisherman’s Village (19:00).

2-Day Itinerary: Culture & Nature

Day 1: Same as above, but swap Chaweng for Lamai Beach in the afternoon. Day 2: Full-day Ang Thong Marine Park tour (08:00–17:00). Evening at Fisherman’s Village market (if Friday) or a beachfront dinner.

3-Day Itinerary: Complete Samui

Day 1: Big Buddha, Wat Plai Laem, Na Muang Waterfalls, Chaweng Beach. Day 2: Ang Thong Marine Park. Day 3: Samui Elephant Sanctuary (morning), Secret Buddha Garden (afternoon), and sunset at Lad Koh Viewpoint. Add a water park if traveling with kids.

Free Attractions

Koh Samui has plenty of free activities:

  • Beaches: Chaweng, Lamai, Bophut, and Maenam—all free.
  • Temples: Big Buddha, Wat Plai Laem, Wat Khunaram—no entry fee.
  • Viewpoints: Lad Koh, Chaweng Viewpoint, and Overlap Stone—free and scenic.
  • Markets: Fisherman’s Village (Friday) and Lamai Night Market (daily)—free to browse.
  • Waterfalls: Na Muang and Hin Lad—free to visit.

Practical Tips

Transportation

Songthaews (shared taxis) cost ₽100–₽400 (≈ $1–$4) per ride. Rent a scooter for ₽500–₽800 (≈ $5–$9) per day—but wear a helmet and have an international license. For airport transfers, use Kiwitaxi from ₽1 500 (≈ $16).

Combo Tickets

Some attractions offer combos, like the Samui Aquarium + Tiger Zoo for ₽1 500 (≈ $16) instead of ₽1 200 each. Check GetYourGuide for packages.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do attractions cost in Koh Samui?

Entrance fees vary: from free attractions to $20-30 for museums. Many venues have discount days.

Do I need a guide in Koh Samui?

A guide is recommended for historical sites and complex routes. Self-guided travel is possible with audio guides and maps.
