Welcome to Krabi, a stunning province on Thailand's Andaman coast that feels like nature’s own masterpiece. Imagine towering limestone karsts rising from emerald-green waters, pristine white-sand beaches fringed by casuarina trees, and a laid-back atmosphere that instantly melts away stress.

Unlike the bustling party scene of Phuket or the crowded streets of Bangkok, Krabi offers a perfect blend of adventure, relaxation, and authentic Thai charm. The main town, Krabi Town, is a humble riverside hub with a local market vibe, while the beach areas like Ao Nang and Railay Beach are where most travelers base themselves.

What makes this a great destination in 2026? The province is a gateway to some of Thailand’s most iconic islands—Phi Phi, Koh Lanta, and the Four Islands—without the overwhelming crowds of more touristy spots. You can kayak through mangrove forests, rock climb on world-class limestone cliffs, or simply float in the warm, crystal-clear waters of the Andaman Sea.

The local food scene is a revelation: from fiery curries to fresh seafood grilled on the beach, every meal is an adventure. Plus, Krabi remains remarkably affordable compared to other Thai destinations, making it a smart choice for budget-conscious travelers who still want luxury experiences.

In 2026, Krabi is more accessible than ever, with improved infrastructure, new eco-friendly tours, and a growing focus on sustainable tourism. Whether you’re a solo backpacker, a couple seeking romance, or a family with kids, Krabi delivers.

The sunsets here are legendary—paint the sky in shades of orange, pink, and purple over the Andaman Sea—and the warmth of the Thai people will make you feel like you’ve found a second home. So pack your swimsuit, grab your sense of adventure, and let’s dive into everything Krabi has to offer.

How to Get There

The main gateway is Krabi International Airport (KBV), located about 15 kilometers from Krabi Town and 30 kilometers from Ao Nang. Direct flights from major cities like Bangkok (BKK and DMK), Singapore, Kuala Lumpur, and even some European hubs (seasonal) make it easy to reach. From Bangkok, flights take about 1 hour 15 minutes, with average prices ranging from ₽4 500 – ₽9 000 (≈ 1 800 – 3 600 THB / $50 – $100) for one-way tickets.

Budget airlines like AirAsia, Nok Air, and Thai Lion Air operate frequently, while full-service carriers like Thai Airways offer more comfort. For the best deals, check prices on Aviasales — they compare hundreds of airlines to find the cheapest options. Remember, booking 2-3 months in advance can save you up to 30%.

Where to Stay

Krabi offers accommodation for every budget, from rustic bungalows to five-star resorts. Ao Nang is the most popular area, with a lively beachfront, tons of restaurants, and easy access to island tours. Mid-range hotels here cost ₽2 500 – ₽5 000 (≈ 1 000 – 2 000 THB / $28 – $56) per night. For luxury, Railay Beach (accessible only by boat) offers stunning resorts like Rayavadee, with prices from ₽15 000 (≈ 6 000 THB / $168) per night.

Budget travelers love Krabi Town, where hostels start at ₽500 (≈ 200 THB / $5.60) per night. For a quieter stay, Klong Muang Beach has beautiful resorts with fewer crowds. Use Hotellook to compare prices across booking sites — you can often find discounts of 20-40% on top-rated properties. For a seamless booking experience, check Booking or Agoda, which specialize in Asian hotels.

Top 15 Attractions

  1. Railay Beach — A stunning peninsula with four beaches, accessible only by longtail boat. Perfect for rock climbing, kayaking, and sunset views. Free entry, but longtail boat from Ao Nang costs ₽200 (≈ 80 THB / $2.20) per person. Open 24/7.
  2. Phi Phi Islands — Iconic turquoise waters and Maya Bay (made famous by "The Beach"). Day tours from Krabi cost ₽1 500 – ₽3 000 (≈ 600 – 1 200 THB / $17 – $34) including snorkeling gear. Tours run 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM.
  3. Emerald Pool (Sa Morakot) — A natural spring-fed pool with emerald-colored water in Thung Teao Forest. Entry ₽200 (≈ 80 THB / $2.20). Open 8:30 AM – 5:00 PM.
  4. Hot Springs (Klong Thom) — Natural thermal waterfalls in a jungle setting. Entry ₽250 (≈ 100 THB / $2.80). Open 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM.
  5. Four Islands Tour — Visit Chicken, Poda, Tup, and Phra Nang beaches. Snorkeling and lunch included. Cost ₽1 200 – ₽2 500 (≈ 480 – 1 000 THB / $13 – $28). Tours 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM.
  6. Hong Islands — A quieter alternative to Phi Phi, with a stunning lagoon. Day tours ₽1 500 (≈ 600 THB / $17). Open 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM.
  7. Tiger Cave Temple (Wat Tham Suea) — A Buddhist temple with 1,237 steps to a panoramic viewpoint. Free entry, but donations welcome. Open 6:00 AM – 6:00 PM.
  8. Krabi Town Night Market — A vibrant market with street food, souvenirs, and live music. Free entry. Open Fridays to Sundays, 5:00 PM – 10:00 PM.
  9. Khao Khanab Nam Caves — Twin limestone hills with caves and a river cruise. Entry ₽200 (≈ 80 THB / $2.20). Open 8:30 AM – 4:30 PM.
  10. Diamond Cave (Tham Phra Nang) — A cave complex with stalactites and stalagmites on Railay Beach. Free entry. Open 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM.
  11. Thung Teao Forest Natural Park — A pristine rainforest with a boardwalk trail to Emerald Pool and Blue Pool. Entry ₽200 (≈ 80 THB / $2.20). Open 8:30 AM – 5:00 PM.
  12. Koh Lanta — A laid-back island with long beaches and a relaxed vibe. Ferry from Krabi costs ₽600 (≈ 240 THB / $6.70). Departs 8:00 AM and 1:00 PM.
  13. Wat Kaew Korawaram — A beautiful white temple in Krabi Town with intricate architecture. Free entry. Open 6:00 AM – 6:00 PM.
  14. Nopparat Thara Beach — A long, peaceful beach near Ao Nang, great for picnics. Free entry. Open 24/7.
  15. Krabi Elephant Sanctuary — An ethical sanctuary where you can feed and bathe elephants. Tours cost ₽2 500 (≈ 1 000 THB / $28). Open 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM.

Getting Around

Krabi’s transport options are straightforward. Tuk-tuks (shared taxis) are the main way to get around, with fares from ₽50 – ₽150 (≈ 20 – 60 THB / $0.56 – $1.70) for short trips within Ao Nang. Songthaews (converted pickup trucks) run between Krabi Town and Ao Nang for ₽100 (≈ 40 THB / $1.10).

Scooter rental is popular and costs ₽300 – ₽500 (≈ 120 – 200 THB / $3.40 – $5.60) per day — you’ll need an International Driving Permit (IDP). For longer distances, private taxis cost ₽500 – ₽1 000 (≈ 200 – 400 THB / $5.60 – $11). There’s no metro or bus system, so plan ahead. Use Kiwitaxi to book reliable private transfers with fixed prices — no haggling needed.

Where to Eat

Krabi’s food scene is a delicious mix of Thai classics and fresh seafood. Street food is the cheapest option, with dishes like pad thai, som tam (papaya salad), and grilled fish costing ₽100 – ₽200 (≈ 40 – 80 THB / $1.10 – $2.20). In Ao Nang, Thanya Kitchen serves authentic Thai curries for ₽200 – ₽400 (≈ 80 – 160 THB / $2.20 – $4.50). For seafood, Krua Panida offers grilled prawns and tom yum goong at ₽400 – ₽800 (≈ 160 – 320 THB / $4.50 – $9).

In Krabi Town, Ruan Mai Restaurant is a local favorite for its crab fried rice and green curry (₽300 – ₽600 / ≈ 120 – 240 THB / $3.40 – $6.70). For fine dining, The Grotto at Rayavadee serves Thai-European fusion inside a cave, with mains from ₽1 500 (≈ 600 THB / $17). Don’t miss the Krabi Town Night Market on weekends for grilled meats, mango sticky rice, and fresh fruit shakes — all under ₽150 (≈ 60 THB / $1.70) per item.

Tours & Excursions

Krabi is a paradise for day trips. The most popular is the Four Islands Tour, which includes snorkeling, lunch, and visits to Chicken, Poda, Tup, and Phra Nang beaches — prices start at ₽1 200 (≈ 480 THB / $13). For a more exclusive experience, a private longtail boat tour to Hong Islands costs ₽3 000 (≈ 1 200 THB / $34) for up to 4 people. Rock climbing at Railay Beach is a must for adventurers, with half-day courses from ₽1 500 (≈ 600 THB / $17).

Elephant sanctuary visits (ethical ones) cost ₽2 500 (≈ 1 000 THB / $28) and include feeding and bathing. For a unique experience, try a sunset kayak tour through mangrove forests — ₽1 000 (≈ 400 THB / $11). You can easily book tours via GetYourGuide, which offers free cancellation and customer reviews — just search for "Krabi tours" to compare options and prices.

Airport Transfer

From Krabi Airport, you have several options to reach your accommodation. Shared minivans to Ao Nang cost ₽200 – ₽300 (≈ 80 – 120 THB / $2.20 – $3.40) per person and take about 40 minutes. Private taxis are faster and more comfortable, with fixed fares to Ao Nang at ₽600 – ₽800 (≈ 240 – 320 THB / $6.70 – $9).

To Krabi Town, a taxi costs around ₽400 (≈ 160 THB / $4.50). Public buses are rare, so pre-booking is wise. For a stress-free arrival, book a Kiwitaxi transfer — they meet you at the arrivals hall with a name sign, and prices are fixed with no hidden fees. A typical transfer to Ao Nang costs ₽700 (≈ 280 THB / $7.80).

Connectivity

Staying connected in Krabi is easy. Most hotels, cafes, and restaurants offer free Wi-Fi, though speeds can vary. For reliable internet on the go, buy a local SIM card at the airport or 7-Eleven. AIS and TrueMove offer tourist SIMs with 15 GB of data for 15 days at ₽500 (≈ 200 THB / $5.60).

Alternatively, get an eSIM before you travel — no physical card needed. Airalo offers affordable eSIM plans for Thailand, starting at ₽300 (≈ 120 THB / $3.40) for 1 GB of data, with easy activation via their app. This is perfect for staying in touch with family or using Google Maps without worrying about local SIM queues.

Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit Krabi is from November to April (high season), when the weather is dry, sunny, and the sea is calm. Temperatures range from 25°C to 32°C (77°F – 90°F). This is peak tourist season, so book accommodation 2-3 months in advance. May to October is the monsoon season, with heavy rain and rough seas, but prices drop by 30-50%.

September and October are the wettest months. March and April are the hottest, with temperatures hitting 35°C (95°F). For a balance of good weather and lower prices, visit in November or March. Expect hotel rates to be 20-30% cheaper than in December-January.

Visa & Documents

For Russian citizens, Thailand offers visa-free entry for up to 60 days. You just need a passport valid for at least 6 months from your arrival date and a confirmed return ticket (though it’s rarely checked). No visa application is needed — just show up at immigration and get a stamp. If you want to stay longer, you can extend your stay by 30 days at any immigration office (costs ₽5 000 / ≈ 2 000 THB / $56).

For other nationalities, check with the Thai embassy in your country. Important for Maldives visa sections: Перед вылетом заполните декларацию IMUGA на imuga.immigration.gov.mv и получите QR-код — без него не посадят на рейс. (This applies only if you’re combining a trip to the Maldives with Krabi.)

Currency & Money

The local currency is the Thai baht (THB), symbolized as ฿. Exchange rates fluctuate, but as of 2026, ₽1 ≈ 0.40 THB (so ₽100 ≈ 40 THB). You can exchange money at banks, exchange booths (look for SuperRich for best rates), or ATMs. ATMs are widely available in Ao Nang and Krabi Town, but charge a fee of ₽550 (≈ 220 THB / $6.20) per withdrawal.

Credit cards are accepted at larger hotels, restaurants, and tour operators, but cash is king for street food, markets, and local taxis. Bring a mix: about ₽10 000 – ₽15 000 (≈ 4 000 – 6 000 THB / $112 – $168) in cash for a week, plus a card for emergencies. Avoid exchanging at the airport — rates are 5-10% worse than in town.

Time Zone

Krabi operates on Indochina Time (ICT), which is UTC+7. This means it’s 4 hours behind Moscow (when Moscow is on standard time), 1 hour behind Singapore, 2 hours behind Beijing, and 12 hours ahead of New York (EST). There’s no daylight saving time, so the time difference stays constant year-round. Plan your calls home accordingly — a good time to call Moscow is around 9:00 AM Krabi time (1:00 PM Moscow).
