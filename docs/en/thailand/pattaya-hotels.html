Pattaya has transformed dramatically over the past few years. What was once known primarily as a party town has evolved into a diverse beach destination with world-class hotels, family-friendly attractions, and a surprisingly sophisticated dining scene. Whether you're traveling on a shoestring or looking to splurge on a five-star resort, Pattaya's hotel scene in 2026 offers something for every budget and travel style.

The city's hotel infrastructure stretches along the coastline from the northern luxury enclaves like Wong Amat and Na Kluea, through the bustling Central Pattaya and Jomtien, down to the quieter southern beaches. Prices vary wildly depending on location, season, and amenities. A basic guesthouse in South Pattaya might cost as little as ₽1 000 (≈ $11) per night, while a beachfront suite at a top-tier resort can run ₽45 000 (≈ $490) or more.

Most of the hotels I recommend below are concentrated in the central areas, where you'll find the best balance of convenience, value, and atmosphere. To get the best deals, you can compare hotel prices on Hotellook or check Booking.com for flexible cancellation options — I always do this before booking anything.

Budget Hotels (under $50/night)

1. Siam View Guesthouse

Located in the heart of Central Pattaya, just a 5-minute walk from Pattaya Beach, Siam View Guesthouse is a solid choice for budget travelers who want cleanliness and a central location. Rooms are simple but spotless, with air conditioning, hot water, and free Wi-Fi. The rooftop terrace offers a nice view of the city skyline.

Prices: ₽2 300-₽3 700 (≈ $25-40) per night for a standard double room.

  • Pros: Great location near Walking Street and beach, friendly staff, good value for money.
  • Cons: No pool, rooms are small, noise from the street can be an issue at night.

2. LK Metropole

This budget-friendly hotel in South Pattaya offers surprisingly good amenities for the price, including a small pool, a restaurant, and a fitness center. Rooms are modern and clean, with comfortable beds and decent bathrooms. It's a 10-minute walk to the beach and close to many bars and restaurants.

Prices: ₽2 800-₽4 600 (≈ $30-50) per night for a standard room.

  • Pros: Pool, good location for nightlife, decent breakfast included.
  • Cons: Can be noisy at night, pool is small, some rooms lack natural light.

3. The BASE Central Pattaya

Actually a condominium building with many individually owned units available for short-term rental, The BASE offers a more home-like experience. You'll find studios and one-bedroom apartments with kitchenettes, living areas, and access to a large rooftop infinity pool with stunning city views. It's a 5-minute walk from Central Festival shopping mall and the beach.

Prices: ₽2 300-₽4 600 (≈ $25-50) per night depending on the unit and season.

  • Pros: Rooftop pool with amazing views, kitchenette, spacious compared to hotel rooms.
  • Cons: No daily housekeeping (varies by owner), check-in can be complicated, no reception desk.

Mid-Range Hotels ($50-150/night)

4. Amari Pattaya

Perched on a hillside overlooking Pattaya Bay, Amari Pattaya is a long-standing favorite for travelers who want a resort feel without the luxury price tag. Rooms are spacious and comfortable, many with sea views. The property features multiple pools, a private beach area, several restaurants, and a spa. It's a 10-minute walk to Walking Street but feels worlds away.

Prices: ₽5 500-₽9 200 (≈ $60-100) per night for a deluxe sea-view room.

  • Highlights: Stunning bay views, excellent breakfast buffet, great pool complex, good for couples.

5. Centara Pattaya Hotel

Located on Central Pattaya Road, the Centara Pattaya is a reliable mid-range option with a prime location near shopping and dining. The hotel has a large free-form pool, a fitness center, and a decent restaurant. Rooms are modern and well-maintained, with some offering city or pool views. It's a 10-minute walk to the beach.

Prices: ₽4 600-₽7 400 (≈ $50-80) per night for a superior room.

  • Highlights: Central location, large pool, good for families, friendly service.

6. Holiday Inn Pattaya

7. Mövenpick Siam Na Jomtien

A bit south of the main Pattaya action, the Mövenpick Siam Na Jomtien offers a quieter, more upscale mid-range experience. It's set on a private beach with lush tropical gardens, multiple pools, and a spa. Rooms are stylish and spacious, with balconies overlooking the ocean or gardens. The hotel also has a great restaurant and a kids' club.

Prices: ₽7 400-₽13 800 (≈ $80-150) per night for a garden-view room.

  • Highlights: Private beach, beautiful grounds, great for relaxation, good for families.

An iconic Pattaya institution, the Royal Cliff is a sprawling complex of four hotels perched on a cliff overlooking the Gulf of Thailand. It offers the ultimate in luxury with multiple pools (including an infinity pool), a world-class spa, several gourmet restaurants, and impeccable service. The rooms are elegantly appointed, many with panoramic sea views. This is where you come for a truly pampered experience.

Prices: ₽13 800-₽36 800 (≈ $150-400) per night for a deluxe room.

  • Features: Infinity pool, Michelin-starred dining, private beach access, extensive spa facilities.

9. Hilton Pattaya

Located right in the center of it all, the Hilton Pattaya sits atop the Central Festival shopping mall and offers direct access to the beach. The hotel has a stunning infinity pool with city and sea views, a fitness center, and several dining options. Rooms are sleek and modern, with floor-to-ceiling windows. The location is unbeatable for those who want to be in the heart of the action.

Prices: ₽18 400-₽32 200 (≈ $200-350) per night for a sea-view room.

  • Features: Rooftop infinity pool, direct mall access, central location, excellent service.

10. Pullman Pattaya Hotel G

Set on a hillside in the quieter Na Kluea area, the Pullman Pattaya Hotel G is a modern luxury resort with stunning ocean views. The hotel features a massive free-form pool, a private beach area, a spa, and several restaurants. Rooms are contemporary and spacious, with large balconies. It's a bit removed from the main tourist areas, making it ideal for those seeking peace and quiet.

Prices: ₽13 800-₽27 600 (≈ $150-300) per night for a deluxe room.

  • Features: Large pool complex, private beach, great for couples, quiet location.

How to Choose the Right Area

Pattaya is spread out, and choosing the right neighborhood can make or break your trip. Here's a quick guide based on your travel style:

  • For beach lovers: Jomtien Beach offers a more relaxed vibe with fewer crowds and plenty of water sports. The southern beaches like Na Jomtien are even quieter. Hotels like Mövenpick Siam Na Jomtien or Pullman Pattaya are perfect here.
  • For sightseeing and shopping: Central Pattaya is your best bet. You'll be close to Central Festival mall, the Art in Paradise museum, and the Sanctuary of Truth. Amari Pattaya or Holiday Inn Pattaya offer great bases.
  • For nightlife: South Pattaya near Walking Street is the epicenter of Pattaya's famous nightlife. Hotels like LK Metropole or Siam View Guesthouse put you right in the middle of the action, but expect noise. If you want nightlife access but a quieter sleep, consider the Amari Pattaya, which is a short walk away.

Airport Transfers

Getting from Bangkok's Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK) or Don Mueang Airport (DMK) to your Pattaya hotel is straightforward. The most common options are:

  • Private transfer: The most comfortable and hassle-free option. A car will be waiting for you at arrivals and take you directly to your hotel. A convenient transfer can be booked through Kiwitaxi, where you can choose from various vehicle types and pay online. Prices start around ₽3 700 (≈ $40) for a standard car.
  • Bus: Public buses run from both airports to Pattaya's bus terminal. The journey takes about 2 hours and costs around ₽550 (≈ $6). From the bus terminal, you'll need a taxi or songthaew to your hotel.
  • Taxi: Metered taxis from the airport are available but can be expensive (around ₽4 600-₽5 500, or $50-60). Make sure to use the official taxi stand.

For a stress-free arrival, I recommend pre-booking a private transfer. It's not much more expensive than a taxi, and you avoid the hassle of negotiating or waiting in line.

Booking Tips

Here are some practical tips to save money and get the best experience when booking a hotel in Pattaya:

  • Book early for peak seasons: Pattaya gets busy during Thai holidays (Songkran in April, New Year), Chinese New Year, and European winter holidays. Prices can double or triple, so book at least 2-3 months in advance.
  • Use comparison tools: You can compare prices on Hotellook to see rates across different booking sites like Booking.com, Agoda, and Expedia. This often reveals significant savings.
  • Consider flexible cancellation: Many hotels on Booking.com offer free cancellation up to 24 or 48 hours before check-in. This is great if your plans might change.
  • Check for package deals: Sometimes booking a hotel + flight through Aviasales or similar services can save you money, especially for longer stays.
  • Look for promotions: Many hotels offer early-bird discounts (10-20% off for booking 30 days in advance) or last-minute deals. Sign up for hotel newsletters to get alerts.
  • Consider location carefully: A cheaper hotel in a less convenient area might cost you more in transport and time. Factor in taxi or songthaew costs when comparing prices.

To get the best deal, I always start by comparing hotel prices on Hotellook and then check Booking.com for flexible cancellation options. For luxury stays, Agoda often has exclusive deals in Southeast Asia. And if you need a reliable airport transfer, Kiwitaxi is my go-to for pre-booking.

Pattaya in 2026 is a destination that caters to all budgets and tastes. Whether you're here for the beaches, the nightlife, or just a relaxing getaway, there's a hotel waiting for you. Happy travels!
