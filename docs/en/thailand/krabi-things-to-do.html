15 Best Things to Do in Krabi 2026

Updated: July 2026 · Expert guide · ⏱ 5 min read

Table of Contents

  • Tours & Excursions

Krabi, Thailand, is a tropical paradise that stole my heart from the moment I first set foot on its powdery beaches. Unlike the bustling chaos of Phuket or the party vibe of Pattaya, Krabi offers a serene yet adventurous escape with its dramatic limestone cliffs, emerald-green waters, and lush jungles.

Whether you're a beach lover, a rock climber, or a culture enthusiast, Krabi has something magical for you. In 2026, the destination continues to charm with improved infrastructure and hidden gems waiting to be discovered. Let me share the 15 best things to do here—each experience is a story worth telling.

15 Best Attractions

1. Railay Beach

Description: Railay is a peninsula accessible only by boat, famous for its towering limestone karsts and crystal-clear waters. It's a haven for rock climbers, with over 700 bolted routes, but also perfect for sunbathing and swimming. The west side offers stunning sunset views, while the east side has a more laid-back vibe with mangrove forests. You'll feel like you've stepped into a postcard.

Ticket Price: Free entry; longtail boat from Ao Nang: ₽400 (≈ 160 THB) per person one way

Hours: Always open; best visited early morning (8:00 AM) to avoid crowds

How to Get There: Take a longtail boat from Ao Nang Beach (10 minutes) or from Krabi Town pier (30 minutes).

Tip: Bring water shoes—the rocks can be sharp, and you'll want to explore hidden lagoons like the one at Phra Nang Cave Beach.

2. Emerald Pool (Sa Morakot)

Description: This natural spring-fed pool in the Thung Teao Forest Park is a mesmerizing shade of emerald green, surrounded by dense jungle. You can swim in the cool, mineral-rich waters, which are said to have healing properties. The pool is fed by a natural hot spring nearby, making it a unique two-in-one experience. It's a bit of a hike from the parking area, but the reward is pure bliss.

Ticket Price: ₽300 (≈ 120 THB) for adults; ₽150 (≈ 60 THB) for children

Hours: 8:30 AM – 5:00 PM (last entry at 4:30 PM)

How to Get There: Drive or take a taxi from Krabi Town (45 minutes); you can also join a tour from Ao Nang. Check GetYourGuide for guided options.

Tip: Arrive before 9:00 AM to have the pool almost to yourself—it gets packed by midday.

3. Tiger Cave Temple (Wat Tham Sua)

Description: This Buddhist temple is set amidst limestone caves and forests, but the main attraction is the 1,237-step climb to the summit. The reward? Panoramic views of Krabi's coastline and surrounding islands that will leave you breathless—literally and figuratively. The temple itself houses a giant Buddha statue and several meditation caves. It's a spiritual and physical challenge.

Ticket Price: Free (donations appreciated)

Hours: 6:00 AM – 6:00 PM

How to Get There: Located 9 km north of Krabi Town; take a tuk-tuk (₽200 ≈ 80 THB) or rent a scooter.

Tip: Wear sturdy shoes and bring water—the climb is steep, and monkeys might try to steal your snacks. Start early to avoid the heat.

4. Four Islands Tour

Description: This classic tour takes you to Phra Nang Cave Beach, Chicken Island, Tup Island, and Poda Island. Each island has its own charm: snorkeling at Chicken Island, sandbars connecting Tup and Chicken at low tide, and the stunning lagoon at Phra Nang. It's a full-day adventure that showcases Krabi's best marine scenery.

Ticket Price: ₽2 500 (≈ 1 000 THB) per person for a group tour (includes lunch, snorkeling gear, and transfers)

Hours: Typically 8:30 AM – 4:00 PM

How to Get There: Book through GetYourGuide or local agencies in Ao Nang.

Tip: Bring reef-safe sunscreen and a waterproof bag—the boat rides can get splashy.

5. Hong Islands

Description: The Hong Islands are a group of islets in the Andaman Sea, known for their emerald lagoons and vibrant coral reefs. The main island, Koh Hong, has a stunning beach and a lagoon you can kayak through. The water is so clear you can see fish swimming beneath your kayak. It's less crowded than the Four Islands, offering a more serene escape.

Ticket Price: ₽1 500 (≈ 600 THB) for national park fee; tours from ₽3 000 (≈ 1 200 THB) per person

Hours: 8:00 AM – 4:30 PM (closed during monsoon season, November–April)

How to Get There: Join a speedboat tour from Ao Nang or Krabi Town; book via GetYourGuide.

Tip: Kayak early (around 8:30 AM) to avoid crowds in the lagoon—it's magical when it's quiet.

6. Ao Nang Beach

Description: Ao Nang is the main tourist hub of Krabi, with a long stretch of soft sand and a lively promenade lined with restaurants, bars, and shops. It's not the most pristine beach, but it's perfect for people-watching and sunset strolls. You can rent a kayak or paddleboard here, or simply relax with a coconut in hand.

Ticket Price: Free

Hours: Always open; best visited in the late afternoon

How to Get There: Walk from most hotels in Ao Nang; taxis from Krabi Town cost ₽400 (≈ 160 THB).

Tip: For a quieter spot, walk to the end of the beach near the cliff—there's a small hidden cove.

7. Krabi Hot Springs (Namtok Ron)

Description: Nestled in the jungle, these natural hot springs cascade down limestone rocks into warm, mineral-rich pools. The temperature is around 40°C (104°F), perfect for soothing tired muscles after a day of hiking. The water flows through a series of pools, each with a different temperature. It's a natural spa experience.

Ticket Price: ₽300 (≈ 120 THB) for adults; ₽150 (≈ 60 THB) for children

Hours: 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM

How to Get There: Located near the Emerald Pool; drive or take a taxi from Krabi Town (40 minutes).

Tip: Visit after the Emerald Pool—they're only 10 minutes apart, and you can buy a combo ticket for ₽500 (≈ 200 THB).

8. Thung Teao Forest Park

Description: This protected forest park is home to the Emerald Pool, Blue Pool, and several hiking trails through ancient rainforest. The Blue Pool is a deep, crystal-clear pool that's too cold for swimming but stunning to photograph. The park is a biodiversity hotspot with birds, monkeys, and exotic plants.

Ticket Price: ₽300 (≈ 120 THB) for adults; ₽150 (≈ 60 THB) for children (same as Emerald Pool entry)

Hours: 8:30 AM – 5:00 PM

How to Get There: Same as Emerald Pool; the park entrance is adjacent.

Tip: Wear insect repellent—the jungle is alive with mosquitoes, especially near the Blue Pool.

9. Khao Khanab Nam Caves

Description: These two towering limestone hills rise from the sea near Krabi Town, with caves inside that contain ancient paintings and stalactites. You can explore them by boat, which takes you through mangrove forests and into the dark caverns. It's a mix of adventure and history, with legends of hidden treasures.

Ticket Price: ₽600 (≈ 240 THB) per person for a 1-hour boat tour

Hours: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM

How to Get There: Take a longtail boat from Krabi Town pier (10 minutes).

Tip: Bring a flashlight—the caves are dark, and you'll want to see the stalactites clearly.

10. Koh Lanta (Day Trip)

Description: Koh Lanta is a laid-back island south of Krabi, known for its long beaches, clear waters, and relaxed vibe. It's less developed than Phuket, with charming bungalows and beachfront restaurants. You can explore the Mu Ko Lanta National Park, go snorkeling at Koh Ngai, or simply cycle around the island.

Ticket Price: Ferry from Krabi: ₽500 (≈ 200 THB) per person one way; national park fee: ₽500 (≈ 200 THB)

Hours: Ferries run 8:00 AM – 3:00 PM

How to Get There: Take a ferry from Krabi Town or Ao Nang; book via Booking.com for packages.

Tip: Stay overnight if you can—the sunset at Long Beach is unforgettable.

11. Krabi Elephant Sanctuary

Description: This ethical sanctuary rescues elephants from logging and tourism, allowing them to roam freely in a natural habitat. You can observe them, feed them, and learn about conservation efforts. No riding here—just respectful interaction. It's a heartwarming experience that supports animal welfare.

Ticket Price: ₽3 500 (≈ 1 400 THB) per person (includes transfer, lunch, and guide)

Hours: 8:00 AM – 3:00 PM (half-day sessions)

How to Get There: Book a tour through GetYourGuide; pick-up from Ao Nang or Krabi Town.

Tip: Wear comfortable clothes and closed shoes—you'll be walking in muddy areas.

12. Thale Waek (Separated Sea)

Description: During low tide, a sandbar emerges between Chicken Island and Tup Island, creating a "separated sea" effect. You can walk between the islands, surrounded by shallow, clear water. It's a unique natural phenomenon that lasts only a few hours each day.

Ticket Price: Included in Four Islands tour; national park fee ₽500 (≈ 200 THB) if visiting independently

Hours: Low tide times vary; check local tide tables

How to Get There: Join a longtail boat from Ao Nang (₽1 500 ≈ 600 THB for a private boat).

Tip: Check the tide schedule online—you need to be there at low tide, usually around 10:00 AM.

13. Krabi Town Night Market

Description: This bustling market on the riverfront is a feast for the senses—grilled seafood, coconut pancakes, and fresh fruit smoothies fill the air with aromas. You'll find local crafts, clothing, and souvenirs, all at bargain prices. It's the perfect place to experience local life and try authentic Thai street food.

Ticket Price: Free entry; food from ₽100 (≈ 40 THB) per dish

Hours: 5:00 PM – 10:00 PM (Friday to Sunday)

How to Get There: Walk from Krabi Town center; taxis from Ao Nang cost ₽400 (≈ 160 THB).

Tip: Try the grilled squid and mango sticky rice—they're the best here.

14. Kayaking in Ao Thalane

Description: Ao Thalane is a stunning bay with limestone karsts, mangrove forests, and hidden lagoons. Kayaking through the calm waters lets you explore caves and narrow channels, with monkeys and birds overhead. It's a peaceful adventure that connects you with nature.

Ticket Price: ₽1 200 (≈ 480 THB) per person for a 2-hour guided tour; ₽800 (≈ 320 THB) for rental only

Hours: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM

How to Get There: Drive or take a taxi from Ao Nang (20 minutes); tours available on GetYourGuide.

Tip: Go early (8:00 AM) when the water is calm and the light is perfect for photos.

15. Diamond Cave (Tham Phra Nang)

Description: Located on Railay Beach, this cave is filled with stalactites and stalagmites that sparkle like diamonds when light hits them. It's a short, easy walk from the beach, but the interior is dark and mysterious. The cave is also a sacred site, with offerings left by local fishermen.

Ticket Price: Free (part of Railay Beach access)

Hours: Always open; best visited in the morning

How to Get There: Walk from Railay West Beach (5 minutes).

Tip: Bring a headlamp or use your phone flashlight—the cave gets dark quickly.

1-Day, 2-Day, 3-Day Itineraries

1-Day Itinerary: Krabi Highlights

Morning (7:00 AM – 12:00 PM): Start at Tiger Cave Temple for sunrise (6:30 AM arrival) and climb to the summit. Then, head to Emerald Pool (9:30 AM) for a swim before crowds arrive. Lunch (12:00 PM): Eat at a local restaurant near the pool (₽200 ≈ 80 THB). Afternoon (1:00 PM – 5:00 PM): Visit Krabi Hot Springs for a relaxing soak. Evening (5:00 PM – 8:00 PM): Explore Ao Nang Beach for sunset, then dinner at a beachfront restaurant (₽400 ≈ 160 THB).

2-Day Itinerary: Beach and Islands

Day 1: Follow the 1-day itinerary. Day 2 (8:00 AM – 4:00 PM): Take a Four Islands Tour (book via GetYourGuide). Enjoy snorkeling, lunch on Poda Island, and the sandbar at Thale Waek. Evening (6:00 PM): Visit Krabi Town Night Market (if it's a weekend) for dinner and shopping.

3-Day Itinerary: Adventure and Relaxation

Day 1: Same as 1-day itinerary. Day 2: Four Islands Tour. Day 3 (7:00 AM – 3:00 PM): Kayak at Ao Thalane (book a morning tour), then visit Hong Islands for a speedboat tour. Evening (4:00 PM – 6:00 PM): Relax at Railay Beach and watch the sunset.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do attractions cost in Krabi?

Entrance fees vary: from free attractions to $20-30 for museums. Many venues have discount days.

Do I need a guide in Krabi?

A guide is recommended for historical sites and complex routes. Self-guided travel is possible with audio guides and maps.
