Planning a trip to Phuket in 2026? You're in for a treat. This island has everything from quiet coves and bustling markets to world-class diving and vibrant nightlife. But with so many options, choosing the right hotel can feel overwhelming.

I've been there myself—scrolling through hundreds of listings, wondering if that "beachfront" room actually has a view of the sea or just a glimpse of a palm tree. That's why I've put together this guide to the top 10 hotels in Phuket for 2026, covering every budget and style.

Phuket's hotel scene is incredibly diverse. In the south, near Patong, you'll find high-energy resorts and budget-friendly hostels, while the west coast (Kamala, Surin, Bang Tao) offers serene luxury and family-friendly villas. Prices vary dramatically by season: high season (November to April) can see rates double or triple compared to the rainy months (May to October).

Generally, budget hotels start at around ₽4 600 (≈ $50) per night, mid-range options run from ₽4 600 to ₽13 800 (≈ $50–$150), and luxury properties climb from ₽13 800 (≈ $150) upwards. For the best deals, you can compare hotel prices in Phuket on Hotellook or book directly through Booking.com to secure early-bird discounts.

Budget Hotels (under $50/night)

1. Lub d Phuket Patong

If you're traveling solo or on a tight budget, Lub d is a fantastic social hostel-hotel hybrid. Located just a 5-minute walk from Patong Beach and Bangla Road, it offers clean, air-conditioned dorms and private rooms. The rooftop pool and bar are perfect for meeting fellow travelers. Prices start at around ₽1 840 (≈ $20) for a dorm bed and ₽4 600 (≈ $50) for a private room.

  • Pros: Great location, lively social scene, modern facilities.
  • Cons: Can be noisy at night, limited privacy in dorms.

3. The Memory at On On Hotel

This historic hotel in Phuket Town was featured in the movie "The Beach." It's a budget option with character: think vintage tiles, high ceilings, and a charming courtyard. Rooms are simple but spotless. Prices hover around ₽2 760 (≈ $30) for a standard double. It's ideal if you want to explore the old town's cafes and Sino-Portuguese architecture.

  • Pros: Unique atmosphere, great value, central location in Phuket Town.
  • Cons: No pool, far from beaches (20-minute drive), basic breakfast.

Mid-Range Hotels ($50-150/night)

4. Novotel Phuket Kamala Beach

Perched on a hillside overlooking Kamala Beach, this Novotel offers a perfect balance of comfort and value. Rooms are spacious with balconies, and the infinity pool is a highlight. It's a 10-minute walk to the beach and local restaurants. Standard rooms start at around ₽6 900 (≈ $75) per night.

  • Highlights: Stunning sea views, family-friendly, good breakfast buffet.

5. The Shore at Katathani

6. Holiday Inn Resort Phuket (Patong)

A reliable choice for families, this Holiday Inn is set back from the Patong chaos but still close enough to walk to the action. It has a large pool with a water slide, kids' club, and spacious rooms. Rates typically range from ₽7 360 (≈ $80) to ₽11 040 (≈ $120) per night.

  • Highlights: Kids' activities, convenient location, free shuttle to the beach.

7. The Vijitt Resort Phuket

Located on the quieter Rawai Beach in the south, Vijitt offers traditional Thai-style villas surrounded by tropical gardens. It's a peaceful escape with a lovely pool and a restaurant serving local cuisine. Doubles cost around ₽8 280 (≈ $90) per night.

  • Highlights: Authentic Thai design, serene atmosphere, good for couples.

How to Choose the Right Area

Phuket is big—about the size of Singapore—so picking the right neighborhood is crucial. Here's how to decide based on your travel style:

For Beach Lovers

Head to the west coast. Kata, Karon, and Kamala offer soft sand and calm waters, perfect for swimming and sunbathing. Bang Tao has a long, wide beach with luxury resorts. Avoid Patong if you want peace—it's crowded and loud.

For Sightseeing & Culture

Base yourself in Phuket Town. It's the cultural heart, with colorful Sino-Portuguese shophouses, temples like Wat Chalong, and the Big Buddha. You'll need a scooter or taxi to reach beaches, but you'll save on accommodation and eat amazing street food.

For Nightlife

Patong is the epicenter. Bangla Road is packed with bars, clubs, and shows. If you want to party but sleep in peace, stay in a quieter part of Patong (like the southern end) or choose Kata for a more relaxed scene with a few bars.

Airport Transfers

Phuket International Airport (HKT) is on the island's north end. To reach your hotel, you have several options:

  • Taxi: Metered taxis cost around ₽920–₽1 840 (≈ $10–$20) depending on distance. Always negotiate or use the official airport taxi stand.
  • Private transfer: A convenient transfer can be booked in advance via Kiwitaxi. Prices range from ₽1 840 (≈ $20) for a car to Patong to ₽3 680 (≈ $40) for a van to the southern beaches. It's worth it for peace of mind, especially after a long flight.
  • Shared minivan: Cheaper at around ₽550 (≈ $6) per person, but you'll wait for other passengers and make multiple stops.
  • Rental car: Available at the airport from ₽1 380 (≈ $15) per day. Driving is on the left, and traffic can be chaotic, but it gives you freedom.

Booking Tips

Here's how to get the best deals on Phuket hotels in 2026:

  • Book early for high season: November to April rooms fill up fast. Reserve 3–6 months ahead to lock in lower rates.
  • Use price comparison sites: You can compare hotel prices in Phuket on Hotellook to see rates across multiple platforms. Also check Agoda for Asia-specific deals—they often have exclusive discounts.
  • Consider mid-week stays: Hotels are cheaper Monday–Thursday. Weekend rates spike, especially in Patong.
  • Look for packages: Some booking sites bundle flights and hotels. Check Aviasales for flight+hotel deals.
  • Read cancellation policies: Many hotels offer free cancellation up to 24–48 hours before check-in. Use this to your advantage—book a refundable rate now, and if a better deal appears later, rebook.
  • Sign up for loyalty programs: Hotels like Novotel (Accor) and Holiday Inn (IHG) often have member-only rates.
  • Check for hidden fees: Some resorts add a "resort fee" or service charge. Always read the fine print.
