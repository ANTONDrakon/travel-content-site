How to Find Cheap Flights to Krabi 2026

Updated: July 2026 · Expert guide · ⏱ 5 min read

Planning a trip to Krabi in 2026 is an exciting prospect — think turquoise waters, limestone cliffs, and serene beaches. But before you can lounge on Railay Beach or explore the Phi Phi Islands, you need to snag a flight that doesn't break the bank. As someone who's tracked airfare trends for years, I can tell you that finding cheap flights to Krabi is all about timing, flexibility, and knowing where to look.

Krabi is served by Krabi International Airport (KBV), which handles both domestic and international flights. The main airlines serving KBV include Thai AirAsia, Thai Lion Air, Bangkok Airways, Thai Vietjet, and seasonal charters from Europe and Asia. For Russian travelers, direct flights are limited, but connecting options through Bangkok, Kuala Lumpur, or Dubai can be budget-friendly. Let me walk you through everything you need to know to score affordable airfare in 2026.

Direct Flights

Direct flights to Krabi are a dream — no layovers, no stress. However, they tend to be pricier and less frequent than connecting options. In 2026, expect direct services from these cities:

  • Bangkok (BKK/DMK): Multiple daily flights with Thai AirAsia, Thai Lion Air, and Bangkok Airways. Prices range from ₽4 500–₽9 000 (≈ $50–$100) one-way.
  • Kuala Lumpur (KUL): AirAsia operates direct flights. Expect ₽6 000–₽12 000 (≈ $65–$130) one-way.
  • Singapore (SIN): Scoot and Jetstar Asia offer seasonal direct flights. Prices around ₽10 000–₽18 000 (≈ $110–$200) one-way.
  • Hong Kong (HKG): Hong Kong Express and Thai AirAsia have occasional direct routes. Prices from ₽14 000–₽25 000 (≈ $150–$270) one-way.
  • Moscow (SVO/DME): Seasonal charter flights (e.g., Azur Air, Nordwind) operate during peak winter months. Prices start at ₽30 000–₽55 000 (≈ $330–$600) round-trip.

For the best deals on direct flights, check Aviasales or Skyscanner — they aggregate all airlines and show the cheapest dates. Note that direct flights from Russia are rare, so most travelers will need a connecting route.

Connecting Flights

Connecting flights are where the real savings happen. The cheapest options often involve a layover in a major hub. Here are the most popular routes:

  • Via Bangkok: Fly from Moscow (SVO/DME) to Bangkok (BKK) with Aeroflot, Thai Airways, or Etihad, then take a short hop to Krabi with Thai AirAsia or Bangkok Airways. Total duration: 12–16 hours. Prices: ₽35 000–₽55 000 (≈ $380–$600) round-trip.
  • Via Dubai: Emirates or flydubai from Moscow to Dubai, then connect to Krabi with AirAsia or Thai Lion Air. Duration: 14–18 hours. Prices: ₽40 000–₽60 000 (≈ $440–$660) round-trip.
  • Via Kuala Lumpur: AirAsia X from Moscow to Kuala Lumpur, then a short flight to Krabi. Duration: 15–20 hours. Prices: ₽30 000–₽50 000 (≈ $330–$550) round-trip.
  • Via Istanbul: Turkish Airlines to Istanbul, then to Bangkok or Kuala Lumpur, and finally to Krabi. Duration: 18–24 hours. Prices: ₽38 000–₽58 000 (≈ $420–$640) round-trip.

Flights can be compared across dates using Aviasales — their calendar view shows the cheapest days to fly. For example, flying on a Tuesday or Wednesday is often 20–30% cheaper than weekends. I always recommend booking connecting flights with at least 2–3 hours between connections to avoid stress, especially in busy airports like Bangkok Suvarnabhumi.

10 Hacks for Cheap Flights

After years of hunting for deals, here are my top 10 hacks to save money on flights to Krabi:

  1. Use Incognito Mode: Airlines and booking sites track your searches. Open a private browser window to avoid price hikes. I always do this on Aviasales before comparing.
  2. Book Connecting Flights Separately: Sometimes booking a Moscow–Bangkok flight on Aviasales and a Bangkok–Krabi flight on AirAsia separately is cheaper than a single booking. Just leave 3–4 hours between flights.
  3. Fly Mid-Week: Tuesdays and Wednesdays are cheapest. Avoid Fridays and Sundays. Use the calendar view on Aviasales to spot the lowest days.
  4. Set Price Alerts: On Aviasales or Skyscanner, set alerts for your route. You'll get notified when prices drop. I once saved ₽15 000 by waiting a week.
  5. Consider Nearby Airports: Phuket (HKT) is 2–3 hours by bus from Krabi, but flights there can be ₽10 000–₽20 000 cheaper. Then take a minivan to Krabi for ₽700 (≈ $8). Check Aviasales for both airports.
  6. Use Budget Airlines for Short Hops: For the Bangkok–Krabi leg, Thai AirAsia or Thai Lion Air offer prices as low as ₽2 500 (≈ $28) one-way. Book directly on their sites or via Aviasales.
  7. Book Red-Eye Flights: Overnight flights from Moscow to Bangkok are often cheaper. You'll arrive in the morning and can catch a connecting flight to Krabi.
  8. Check for Student or Group Discounts: Some airlines offer 10–15% discounts for students or groups of 10+. Check with Thai AirAsia or Emirates directly.
  9. Use Miles or Points: If you have credit card miles (e.g., Sberbank, Tinkoff), redeem them for flights. A Moscow–Krabi round-trip can cost as little as 30 000 miles + taxes.
  10. Book During Sales: Thai AirAsia and AirAsia X have frequent sales (e.g., "Big Sale" in March, September). Follow them on social media or check Aviasales for promo codes.

For accommodation, use Hotellook to find hotels near Krabi Airport or on Ao Nang Beach. For tours like island hopping, check GetYourGuide for early-bird discounts.

When to Book

Timing is everything. Based on 2025 data, here's the optimal booking window for 2026:

  • For peak season (November–March): Book 4–6 months in advance. Prices rise sharply 2 months before departure. I booked my January 2025 flight in September 2024 and saved ₽20 000.
  • For shoulder season (April, October): Book 2–3 months ahead. You'll find good deals, but avoid last-minute bookings during Songkran (April 13–15).
  • For low season (May–September): Book 1–2 months ahead. Airlines often drop prices to fill seats. I once got a Moscow–Krabi round-trip for ₽28 000 in August by booking just 3 weeks before.
  • General rule: The cheapest time to book is 8–10 weeks before departure. Use Aviasales to track price trends — their "Price History" feature shows you if current prices are high or low.

Flight Insurance

Even with careful planning, flights can be delayed or cancelled — especially during monsoon season (May–October) or due to airline strikes. Here's what to do:

  • Check your rights: If your flight is delayed by 3+ hours or cancelled, you may be entitled to compensation under EU Regulation 261/2004 (if flying from EU) or Russian Air Code (for flights from Russia). Compensation ranges from ₽15 000–₽45 000 (≈ $165–$500) depending on distance.
  • Use flight insurance: I always recommend compensation services like AirHelp or ClaimCompass — they handle the paperwork for a 25% fee. You can also check Booking.com for trip insurance when booking hotels.
  • For Russian travelers: If you booked through Aviasales, they often provide support for rebooking. Alternatively, contact the airline directly — Aeroflot and Thai Airways are generally responsive.
  • Pro tip: Always carry a printed copy of your itinerary and travel insurance. For Krabi, I use Airalo for eSIM data — it costs ₽500 (≈ $5.50) for 5 GB, so you can stay connected if plans change.

If your flight is delayed overnight, airlines are usually required to provide accommodation. In Krabi, use Hotellook to find nearby hotels like the Krabi Airport Inn (from ₽1 500/night).

I hope this guide helps you find affordable flights to Krabi in 2026. Remember, flexibility is your best friend — try different dates, airports, and airlines. For the best deals, start your search on Aviasales, and don't forget to check GetYourGuide for tours once you arrive. Safe travels, and enjoy the stunning beaches of Krabi!
