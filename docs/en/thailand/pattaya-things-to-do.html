15 Best Things to Do in Pattaya 2026

When I first visited Pattaya, I expected just a beach party town. But after spending weeks exploring its temples, floating markets, and islands, I realized this city is a treasure chest of experiences.

Whether you're traveling with family, friends, or solo, Pattaya offers everything from adrenaline-pumping water sports to serene Buddhist sanctuaries. In 2026, the city has only gotten better with new attractions and improved infrastructure. Let me walk you through the absolute best things to do here.

15 Best Attractions

1. Sanctuary of Truth

This isn't just a temple—it's a philosophical masterpiece carved entirely from wood. Standing 105 meters tall on the coast, the Sanctuary of Truth blends Hindu and Buddhist motifs with intricate hand-carved sculptures. You'll spend at least two hours marveling at the details, from celestial beings to mythical creatures. The sea breeze makes the experience even more magical.

Ticket price: ₽1 800 (≈ $20) for adults, ₽900 (≈ $10) for children.
Hours: Daily 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM.
How to get there: Take a songthaew (shared taxi) from Pattaya Beach Road for ₽100 (≈ $1.10). Alternatively, use Kiwitaxi for a private transfer from your hotel.
Tip: Visit early morning to avoid crowds and heat. The wooden structure is best photographed at sunrise.

2. Koh Larn (Coral Island)

A 30-minute speedboat ride from Pattaya, Koh Larn offers powdery white sand and crystal-clear turquoise water. You can snorkel among colorful fish, rent a jet ski, or simply lounge on a beach chair with a coconut. The island has several beaches, with Tawaen Beach being the most popular. For a quieter spot, head to Samae Beach.

Ticket price: Ferry ₽150 (≈ $1.70) round trip; speedboat ₽600 (≈ $6.70) per person.
Hours: Ferries run 7:00 AM – 6:30 PM.
How to get there: From Bali Hai Pier, buy a ferry ticket at the counter. You can also book a day trip through GetYourGuide for ₽2 500 (≈ $28), which includes lunch and snorkeling gear.
Tip: Bring reef-safe sunscreen and water shoes—the coral can be sharp.

3. Pattaya Floating Market

This sprawling market on canals features wooden stalls selling Thai handicrafts, street food, and souvenirs. You can take a long-tail boat through the waterways or wander the four themed sections representing different regions of Thailand. Try the mango sticky rice and grilled seafood. It's a sensory overload in the best way.

Ticket price: ₽700 (≈ $7.80) for adults, ₽350 (≈ $3.90) for children.
Hours: Daily 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM.
How to get there: Take a songthaew from Central Pattaya for ₽150 (≈ $1.70).
Tip: Go on a weekday to avoid weekend crowds. Bargain politely for souvenirs.

4. Nong Nooch Tropical Botanical Garden

This 500-acre garden is a paradise for plant lovers. You'll find themed gardens like the French Garden, Stonehenge replicas, and a spectacular orchid collection. The daily cultural shows feature Thai boxing, elephant performances, and traditional dances. It's easily a half-day outing.

Ticket price: ₽1 200 (≈ $13.50) for garden only; ₽1 800 (≈ $20) with cultural show.
Hours: Daily 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM.
How to get there: Take a taxi from Pattaya for ₽600 (≈ $6.70). You can also book a tour through GetYourGuide that includes transport.
Tip: Rent a bicycle inside for ₽200 (≈ $2.20) to cover more ground.

5. Walking Street

By night, this iconic street transforms into a neon-lit carnival of bars, clubs, and street performers. It's loud, chaotic, and utterly unforgettable. Even if you're not into nightlife, the sheer energy is worth experiencing. Grab a beer at a rooftop bar and people-watch.

Ticket price: Free entry.
Hours: 6:00 PM – 2:00 AM (pedestrian-only after 7 PM).
How to get there: Walk from any hotel on Beach Road; it's at the south end.
Tip: Keep your valuables secure and be aware of scams—stick to well-lit areas.

6. Big Buddha Hill (Wat Phra Yai)

A 18-meter tall golden Buddha sits atop a hill overlooking Pattaya Bay. The climb up 120 steps is rewarded with panoramic views and a peaceful temple complex. Monks often bless visitors with holy water. It's a spiritual respite from the city's hustle.

Ticket price: Free (donations welcome).
Hours: Daily 6:00 AM – 6:00 PM.
How to get there: Walk 15 minutes from Walking Street, or take a songthaew for ₽50 (≈ $0.55).
Tip: Dress modestly—cover shoulders and knees.

7. Underwater World Pattaya

This aquarium features a 100-meter long underwater tunnel where you walk surrounded by sharks, rays, and tropical fish. Touch pools let you interact with starfish and sea cucumbers. It's a hit with kids and a cool escape from the heat.

Ticket price: ₽1 200 (≈ $13.50) for adults, ₽800 (≈ $9) for children.
Hours: Daily 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM.
How to get there: It's on Sukhumvit Road, a 10-minute taxi ride from Central Pattaya (₽200/≈ $2.20).
Tip: Book tickets online via Hotellook or GetYourGuide for a 10% discount.

8. Pattaya Elephant Village

This ethical sanctuary lets you observe elephants in a natural setting without riding. You can feed them, watch bathing sessions, and learn about conservation. The shows are educational, not circus-like. It's a responsible way to interact with these gentle giants.

Ticket price: ₽1 500 (≈ $17) for standard visit; ₽3 000 (≈ $34) for feeding and bathing experience.
Hours: Daily 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM.
How to get there: Take a taxi from Pattaya for ₽500 (≈ $5.60).
Tip: Avoid any venue offering elephant rides—they're harmful to the animals.

9. Mini Siam

A miniature park featuring replicas of famous Thai landmarks like Wat Arun, the Grand Palace, and the Bridge on the River Kwai. It's divided into two sections: Mini Siam (Thai heritage) and Mini Europe (global icons). Perfect for a quick photo tour of Thailand.

Ticket price: ₽800 (≈ $9) for adults, ₽400 (≈ $4.50) for children.
Hours: Daily 7:00 AM – 10:00 PM.
How to get there: On Sukhumvit Road, take a songthaew for ₽100 (≈ $1.10).
Tip: Visit at sunset for beautiful lighting on the miniatures.

10. Art in Paradise

This 3D art museum is an interactive wonderland where you become part of the paintings. There are over 100 optical illusion exhibits—pose as if you're holding up a waterfall or escaping a dinosaur. It's hilarious for groups and kids.

Ticket price: ₽700 (≈ $7.80) for adults, ₽400 (≈ $4.50) for children.
Hours: Daily 9:00 AM – 9:00 PM.
How to get there: Located on Pattaya Second Road, a short walk from Central Festival mall.
Tip: Wear comfortable shoes and bring a camera with a wide-angle lens.

11. Pattaya Beach

The main beach stretches 4 km along the coast, lined with restaurants and water sports vendors. Jet skiing, parasailing, and banana boat rides are available. The water isn't the clearest, but the lively atmosphere makes up for it. Sunset here is spectacular.

Ticket price: Free. Water sports start at ₽500 (≈ $5.60) per ride.
Hours: Always open.
How to get there: It's right in the city center—walk from any hotel.
Tip: Rent a sun lounger for ₽100 (≈ $1.10) and stay for the sunset.

12. Jomtien Beach

A quieter alternative to Pattaya Beach, Jomtien is popular with families and expats. The water is cleaner, and there are fewer vendors. You can windsurf, kiteboard, or just relax. The promenade has excellent seafood restaurants.

Ticket price: Free.
Hours: Always open.
How to get there: Take a songthaew from Pattaya for ₽100 (≈ $1.10).
Tip: Try the grilled squid at a beachfront stall—it's divine.

13. Wat Yansangwararam

A stunning temple complex set in peaceful gardens with a giant golden Buddha and a Chinese-style shrine. The main chedi (stupa) contains relics of the Buddha. It's less crowded than other temples, offering a serene escape.

Ticket price: Free.
Hours: Daily 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM.
How to get there: Take a taxi from Pattaya for ₽400 (≈ $4.50).
Tip: Visit during a Buddhist holiday for special ceremonies.

14. Pattaya Park Tower

This 240-meter tower offers a revolving restaurant, a skywalk, and a terrifying drop tower ride. The observation deck gives 360-degree views of Pattaya Bay. For thrill-seekers, the "Sky Jump" is a 50-meter free-fall experience.

Ticket price: ₽400 (≈ $4.50) for observation deck; ₽800 (≈ $9) for Sky Jump.
Hours: Daily 10:00 AM – 10:00 PM.
How to get there: It's on Pattaya Second Road, a 5-minute walk from Central Festival.
Tip: Go at sunset for the best photos.

15. The Million Years Stone Park & Crocodile Farm

A quirky park with petrified wood, dinosaur fossils, and a crocodile show where trainers put their heads in croc mouths. There's also a small zoo with monkeys and birds. It's kitschy but fun for a couple of hours.

Ticket price: ₽700 (≈ $7.80) for adults, ₽400 (≈ $4.50) for children.
Hours: Daily 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM.
How to get there: Take a taxi from Pattaya for ₽300 (≈ $3.40).
Tip: Skip the crocodile show if you're sensitive to animal performances.

1-Day, 2-Day, 3-Day Itineraries

1-Day Itinerary

Morning: Start at Sanctuary of Truth (8 AM – 11 AM). Then head to Pattaya Floating Market for lunch (11:30 AM – 1 PM).
Afternoon: Visit Art in Paradise (1:30 PM – 3 PM), then relax at Pattaya Beach (3 PM – 5 PM).
Evening: Dinner on Walking Street, then explore the nightlife (6 PM onwards).

2-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Morning at Sanctuary of Truth → Lunch at Floating Market → Afternoon at Nong Nooch Garden → Evening at Walking Street.
Day 2: Morning ferry to Koh Larn (8 AM – 1 PM) → Lunch on the island → Afternoon at Big Buddha Hill → Evening at Pattaya Park Tower.

3-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Sanctuary of Truth → Floating Market → Art in Paradise → Walking Street.
Day 2: Koh Larn full day (snorkeling, beach, lunch) → Evening at Underwater World.
Day 3: Nong Nooch Garden → Mini Siam → Wat Yansangwararam → Dinner at Jomtien Beach.

Free Attractions

Pattaya has several free activities:

  • Big Buddha Hill: Free entry with panoramic views.
  • Pattaya Beach & Jomtien Beach: Free to access.
  • Walking Street: Free to explore (drinks cost extra).
  • Wat Yansangwararam: Free temple complex.
  • Pattaya Night Bazaar: Free to wander (Saturdays only).
  • Lan Po Market: Free to explore local food stalls.

Practical Tips

Transportation

Songthaews (shared pick-up trucks) are the cheapest way to get around—₽50-₽150 per ride. For longer distances, use Kiwitaxi for private transfers starting at ₽600 (≈ $6.70). Taxis are more expensive but convenient. Avoid tuk-tuks as they overcharge tourists.

Combo Tickets

Some attractions offer combo deals:

  • Art in Paradise + Mini Siam: ₽1 200 (≈ $13.50) vs. ₽1 500 separately.
  • Underwater World + Pattaya Park Tower: ₽1 400 (≈ $15.70) vs. ₽1 600.
  • Nong Nooch + Sanctuary of Truth: ₽2 500 (≈ $28) vs. ₽3 000.

Check Hotellook for these deals before you go.

Best Time to Visit

November to February is the cool season (25-30°C). Avoid April-May (hottest) and October (rainy). For 2026, plan for December or January.
