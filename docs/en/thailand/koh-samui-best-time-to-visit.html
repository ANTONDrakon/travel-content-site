Best Time to Visit Koh Samui 2026: Seasons & Weather

Koh Samui is a year-round tropical paradise, but timing your visit can make all the difference. I’ve spent many months exploring this Thai island, and I know that the weather, crowds, and prices shift dramatically depending on the season. Whether you’re dreaming of lazy beach days, jungle treks, or vibrant festivals, understanding Samui’s unique microclimate—which differs from mainland Thailand—will help you plan the perfect trip. Let me walk you through everything you need to know for 2026.

Koh Samui enjoys a tropical monsoon climate, but unlike Phuket or Krabi, its rainy season peaks later in the year. The island is shielded by mountains, so it often stays drier during the southwest monsoon (May–October) that drenches the Andaman coast. Instead, Samui’s wettest months are October through December, when the northeast monsoon brings heavy showers. Temperatures remain warm year-round, typically between 25°C and 35°C (77°F–95°F).

As you can see, the driest and sunniest months are January through April, while October and November bring the most rain. Water temperatures are always pleasant for swimming, hovering around 27–30°C (81–86°F).

High Season

Koh Samui’s high season runs from December to March, when the weather is at its best—sunny skies, low humidity, and calm seas. This period coincides with winter in Europe and North America, so the island buzzes with tourists. Prices for accommodation and flights peak, especially around Christmas and New Year. A mid-range hotel room that costs about ₽5 500 (≈ $60) in low season can jump to ₽13 800 (≈ $150) in high season.

During high season, the beaches are lively, restaurants are full, and the atmosphere is festive. If you’re planning to visit then, I strongly recommend booking your hotel early. You can check options on Hotellook or Booking to secure the best rates. For budget travelers, consider staying in less touristy areas like Mae Nam or Bang Por, where prices are slightly lower.

Low Season

The low season spans May to October, with the quietest months being June through September. During this time, you’ll find fewer crowds and significantly lower prices. A beachfront bungalow might cost as little as ₽2 300 (≈ $25) per night. The weather is still warm, but you can expect short, heavy showers, usually in the afternoons. The upside? The island feels more peaceful, and you’ll often have beaches to yourself.

Pros of low season include discounts on flights and hotels, plus a more authentic local experience. Cons are the occasional rough seas (which can limit boat trips) and higher humidity. If you’re flexible and don’t mind a bit of rain, this is a fantastic budget-friendly time to visit.

Best Time for Beach Vacation

For a classic beach holiday with swimming, sunbathing, and water sports, the best months are January to April. The sea is calm, visibility is excellent for snorkeling, and rain is rare. February and March are particularly idyllic, with water temperatures around 28–30°C (82–86°F) and almost daily sunshine.

If you’re after vibrant coral reefs and clear waters for diving, March and April are top choices. The beaches on the north and east coasts (like Chaweng and Lamai) are most popular, but for a quieter experience, head to the west coast (e.g., Lipa Noi) where the sunsets are stunning.

Best Time for Sightseeing

Sightseeing in Koh Samui is most comfortable during the cooler, drier months from December to March. Temperatures are slightly lower (around 27–29°C / 81–84°F), making hikes to waterfalls like Na Muang or visits to the Big Buddha Temple more pleasant. The humidity is also lower, so you won’t feel as sticky exploring the island.

For excursions, I recommend booking through GetYourGuide—they offer guided tours to Ang Thong Marine Park, elephant sanctuaries, and cooking classes. Many operators provide hotel pickup, so you don’t need to worry about transport. Just avoid the rainy months (October–November) for outdoor activities, as trails can get muddy and boat tours may be canceled.

Rainy Season

Koh Samui’s rainy season is concentrated in October and November, though September and December also see significant rainfall. During these months, you can expect daily downpours, often lasting a few hours, followed by brief sunshine. The rain is usually heavy but not constant—think tropical storms that clear the air.

Is it worth visiting? Yes, if you’re on a tight budget or want solitude. Prices drop dramatically: a luxury resort might cost ₽9 200 (≈ $100) instead of ₽23 000 (≈ $250) in high season. However, be prepared for potential ferry cancellations and rough seas.

If you’re planning a beach-focused trip, November is the worst month—many beaches have strong currents and jellyfish warnings. For sightseeing, rainy season can still work if you stick to indoor activities like spa treatments or visiting temples.

Events & Festivals

Koh Samui hosts several vibrant festivals throughout the year. Here are the major ones by month:

  • January – New Year’s celebrations with firework shows on Chaweng Beach.
  • February – Chinese New Year (date varies) with parades and dragon dances in the island’s Chinese community.
  • March – Samui Regatta (sailing event) attracts yachts and parties.
  • April – Songkran (Thai New Year, April 13–15) with water fights and street parties.
  • June–July – Samui Food Festival showcases local cuisine.
  • September – Vegetarian Festival (date varies) with processions and strict vegetarian food.
  • October – Ok Phansa (end of Buddhist Lent) with boat races and temple fairs.
  • November – Loy Krathong (full moon night) where locals float lanterns and krathongs on the water.
  • December – Christmas and New Year’s Eve celebrations with beach parties and fireworks.

If you’re visiting during Songkran or Loy Krathong, book accommodations well in advance as they’re popular with both locals and tourists.

Conclusion

So, when is the best time to visit Koh Samui in 2026? For perfect beach weather and minimal rain, January to April are unbeatable. If you want a balance of good weather and lower prices, consider May or June. For budget travelers, the low season (June–September) offers great deals, though you’ll need to pack an umbrella. Avoid October and November if your main goal is sunbathing, but they’re fine for a quiet, cheap getaway.

For flights, I recommend checking Aviasales to compare prices across airlines. Booking 2–3 months in advance usually gets you the best rates. For example, a round-trip from Moscow to Koh Samui in February might cost around ₽55 000 (≈ $600), while in September it could drop to ₽32 000 (≈ $350). Use Kiwitaxi for airport transfers, and consider an eSIM from Airalo for affordable data upon arrival.

Whichever season you choose, Koh Samui’s warm hospitality and stunning landscapes will make your trip memorable. Happy travels!
