Planning a trip to Bangkok in 2026 is an exciting prospect, and I’m here to help you navigate the process of finding affordable flights. Bangkok is served by two major airports: Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK), the main international hub, and Don Mueang Airport (DMK), which primarily handles low-cost carriers and regional flights.

Airlines like Aeroflot, S7 Airlines, Thai Airways, Emirates, Qatar Airways, and budget carriers such as AirAsia and Nok Air serve routes from various cities. To start your search, I recommend checking options on Aviasales, which aggregates flights from hundreds of airlines and shows you the cheapest combinations.

Connecting Flights

If you’re flexible with time, connecting flights are often cheaper. Budget airlines like AirAsia, Scoot, and flydubai offer routes with stops in cities like Dubai, Doha, Istanbul, or Kuala Lumpur. For example, a flight from Moscow to Bangkok via Dubai with flydubai can cost as low as ₽25 000 – ₽40 000 (≈ $270 – $440) round trip, with a layover of 3–8 hours.

10 Hacks for Cheap Flights

Here are ten specific tips to help you save money on flights to Bangkok in 2026, with services you can use:

  1. Be Flexible with Dates – Use Aviasales' “Low Price Calendar” to find the cheapest days to fly. Flying mid-week (Tuesday or Wednesday) can save up to 30% compared to weekends. Check on Aviasales.
  2. Book Connecting Flights – Opt for layovers in Dubai, Doha, or Istanbul. Use Aviasales to filter by “cheapest” and compare total travel time.
  3. Use Incognito Mode – Search for flights in your browser’s private mode to avoid price hikes based on cookies. Combine this with Aviasales for consistent results.
  4. Set Price Alerts – On Aviasales, set alerts for your route. You’ll get email notifications when prices drop, which helps you snag deals quickly.
  5. Consider Nearby Airports – Fly into Don Mueang (DMK) instead of Suvarnabhumi (BKK) for cheaper budget airline options. Use Aviasales to compare both airports.
  6. Book in Advance – For peak season (December–January), book 3–4 months ahead. For low season, 1–2 months is enough. Check Aviasales for historical price trends.
  7. Use Airline Miles or Points – If you have frequent flyer miles with Aeroflot or S7, redeem them for discounts. Combine with cash bookings on Aviasales.
  8. Book Separate Tickets – Sometimes booking two one-way tickets (e.g., Moscow to Dubai with flydubai, then Dubai to Bangkok with AirAsia) is cheaper. Use Aviasales to compare multi-city options.
  9. Travel Light – Budget airlines charge extra for checked baggage. Pack a carry-on to save ₽3 000 – ₽5 000 (≈ $33 – $54) per leg. Check baggage policies on Aviasales before booking.
  10. Use a VPN – Search for flights from a different country (e.g., Thailand or UAE) to see lower local prices. Use Aviasales with a VPN to test this.

For local transport in Bangkok, consider booking transfers via Kiwitaxi for fixed prices, and for tours, check GetYourGuide to plan activities like temple visits or floating markets.

When to Book

The optimal booking window for cheap flights to Bangkok in 2026 is 2–4 months before departure for low season (May–September) and 4–6 months ahead for peak season (December–January). Based on typical market trends, booking on a Tuesday or Wednesday often yields lower prices.

Use Aviasales to track price history and set alerts—this way, you’ll know when to pull the trigger. For example, if you’re planning a trip in June 2026, start checking prices in February or March. Also, consider booking accommodation early via Hotellook or Booking to secure good rates near the airport or city center.

Flight Insurance

Flight delays and cancellations can happen, especially during monsoon season (June–October) in Bangkok. If your flight is delayed or cancelled, you may be entitled to compensation under EU Regulation 261/2004 if you’re flying with an EU airline or from an EU airport. For other airlines, check their policies. I recommend getting travel insurance that covers flight disruptions, which you can compare on Aviasales or through specialized providers.

If you need to claim compensation, services like get travel insurance can assist with the process, helping you file claims for delays over 3 hours or cancellations. Always keep your boarding pass and receipts for expenses incurred during delays (like meals or hotels). For real-time flight updates, use Aviasales app.

For a stress-free trip, consider getting an eSIM from Airalo for affordable data in Thailand—prices start at ₽500 (≈ $5.40) for 1 GB. And remember, for visa-related travel to the Maldives, before departure, fill out the IMUGA declaration at imuga.immigration.gov.mv and get a QR code—without it, you won’t be allowed to board your flight.
