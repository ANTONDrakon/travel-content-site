Top 10 Hotels in Koh Samui 2026: Budget to Luxury

Koh Samui is one of Thailand’s most beloved islands, and for good reason. With its palm-fringed beaches, lush jungle interiors, and a vibe that ranges from laid-back backpacker to ultra-luxurious, this island has something for every traveler. Whether you’re planning a romantic escape, a family holiday, or a solo adventure, choosing the right hotel can make or break your trip. In 2026, the island continues to evolve, with new properties opening and old favorites upgrading their amenities. From budget-friendly bungalows on Chaweng Beach to five-star resorts with private pools in Bophut, the range of accommodation is staggering.

Neighborhoods like Chaweng offer vibrant nightlife and shopping, while Lamai is more relaxed and affordable. The northern beaches—Maenam and Bang Por—are quieter and perfect for those seeking peace, while the west coast (Taling Ngam) boasts stunning sunsets. Prices vary widely depending on the season, location, and amenities, so it’s smart to plan ahead. Remember that hotel prices in Koh Samui can be ₽3 500 – ₽40 000+ per night (≈ $40 – $450+), depending on your choice. To get the best deals, I always recommend checking Hotellook or Booking.com to compare prices and read recent reviews.

Budget Hotels (under $50/night)

1. The Cosy Hotel Chaweng

Located just a short walk from Chaweng Beach, this no-frills hotel is perfect for backpackers and budget travelers who want to be in the heart of the action. Rooms are clean, modern, and air-conditioned, with basic amenities like free Wi-Fi and a small fridge. The staff is friendly and can help you book tours or scooters. Downsides? The pool is tiny, and noise from nearby bars can be an issue on weekends. It’s a great base for exploring, but not for those seeking quiet.

Price: ₽2 500 – ₽4 000 per night (≈ $28 – $45)

Pros: Central location, affordable, clean rooms
Cons: Noisy at night, small pool, basic breakfast

2. Baan Samui Resort (Lamai)

Set in a lush tropical garden just 200 meters from Lamai Beach, this resort offers simple yet charming bungalows with private balconies. The vibe is relaxed and family-run, with a lovely pool and a small restaurant serving Thai classics. It’s a 10-minute walk to Lamai’s night market and bars, but far enough to feel peaceful. Perfect for couples on a budget or solo travelers who want a bit more space than a hostel.

Price: ₽3 000 – ₽4 500 per night (≈ $34 – $50)

Pros: Quiet garden setting, friendly staff, good value
Cons: No beachfront, rooms a bit dated, limited breakfast options

3. Starfish House & Bungalows (Maenam)

This hidden gem on Maenam Beach offers basic wooden bungalows right on the sand. It’s rustic—think cold showers and mosquito nets—but the location is unbeatable for the price. You can fall asleep to the sound of waves and wake up to a stunning sunrise. There’s a small café serving coffee and toast, and the owners are incredibly welcoming. Ideal for budget-conscious travelers who prioritize beach access over luxury.

Price: ₽2 000 – ₽3 500 per night (≈ $22 – $40)

Pros: Direct beach access, cheap, authentic Thai vibe
Cons: No air conditioning (only fans), basic facilities, no pool

Mid-Range Hotels ($50-150/night)

4. OZO Chaweng Samui

A sleek, modern hotel right on Chaweng Beach. OZO is part of the ONYX Hospitality Group and offers stylish rooms with floor-to-ceiling windows, rain showers, and smart TVs. The pool is impressive—long and lagoon-style—and the breakfast buffet is extensive. The location is prime: steps from the beach and a 5-minute walk to Central Festival Samui mall. Perfect for families and couples who want a bit of luxury without breaking the bank.

Price: ₽6 000 – ₽10 000 per night (≈ $68 – $115)

Highlights: Great pool, excellent location, modern design, family-friendly

5. The Sea Koh Samui (Bang Por)

If you’re looking for a peaceful escape with stunning sunset views, this resort on Bang Por Beach is a fantastic choice. Rooms are spacious and decorated in a contemporary Thai style, with large balconies overlooking the sea. The infinity pool is a highlight, and the on-site restaurant serves delicious seafood. It’s a bit remote (15 minutes from Fisherman’s Village), but that’s part of the charm. Ideal for couples and honeymooners.

Price: ₽7 000 – ₽12 000 per night (≈ $80 – $135)

Highlights: Infinity pool, sunset views, quiet beach, romantic atmosphere

6. Anantara Bophut Koh Samui Resort

Nestled on the quieter end of Bophut Beach, this resort offers a blend of Thai elegance and modern comfort. Rooms are spacious with teak wood accents, and many have direct pool access. The spa is excellent, and the Sunday brunch is legendary. It’s a short walk to Fisherman’s Village with its night market and chic bars. Great for couples and solo travelers who want a touch of luxury at a mid-range price.

Price: ₽8 000 – ₽14 000 per night (≈ $90 – $160)

Highlights: Pool access rooms, excellent spa, close to Fisherman’s Village, beautiful grounds

7. Samui Resotel Beach Resort (Lamai)

This beachfront resort in Lamai offers a classic tropical experience with thatched-roof bungalows and a large free-form pool. Rooms are comfortable and clean, with private terraces. The beach here is quieter than Chaweng, and the resort has a lively bar with regular fire shows. It’s a great option for families and groups of friends who want a central location with plenty of amenities.

Price: ₽5 500 – ₽9 000 per night (≈ $62 – $102)

Highlights: Beachfront, large pool, fire shows, good for groups

Luxury Hotels ($150+/night)

8. Four Seasons Resort Koh Samui

Perched on a hillside overlooking the Gulf of Thailand, the Four Seasons is the pinnacle of luxury on the island. Villas are massive, with private infinity pools, outdoor rain showers, and butler service. The resort has two restaurants, a world-class spa, and a private beach cove. It’s located on the northwest coast (Laem Yai Bay), offering total privacy and breathtaking sunsets. Perfect for honeymooners and those celebrating special occasions.

Price: ₽30 000 – ₽70 000+ per night (≈ $340 – $800+)

Features: Private infinity pools, butler service, private beach, Michelin-star dining, yoga pavilion

How to Choose the Right Area

Koh Samui’s beaches and neighborhoods each have a distinct personality, so picking the right area is key to enjoying your trip:

  • For beach lovers: If your main goal is to lounge on pristine sand and swim in calm waters, head to Chaweng (longest beach, most amenities) or Lamai (more relaxed, beautiful rock formations). For quieter beaches, Maenam and Bang Por offer serene escapes.
  • For sightseeing and culture: Bophut (Fisherman’s Village) is ideal—it’s close to the Big Buddha, Wat Plai Laem, and the night market. Hua Thanon (south) is near the Hin Ta and Hin Yai rocks and the Samui Aquarium.
  • For nightlife: Chaweng is the epicenter of parties, with clubs, bars, and live music. Lamai has a more laid-back but still lively scene, while Bophut offers chic cocktail bars and a more sophisticated vibe.

If you’re unsure, I’d recommend staying in Bophut or Lamai for a balanced mix of beach, culture, and nightlife. For pure luxury and seclusion, choose the northwest coast (Four Seasons area) or the hills of Lamai (Banyan Tree).

Airport Transfers

Samui International Airport (USM) is small but busy, located on the northeast coast near Chaweng and Bophut. Getting to your hotel is straightforward:

  • Taxi: Official taxi stands are outside arrivals. A ride to Chaweng costs around ₽1 200 – ₽1 800 (≈ $14 – $20), to Lamai ₽1 500 – ₽2 200 (≈ $17 – $25), and to Maenam or Bang Por ₽1 800 – ₽2 500 (≈ $20 – $28). Negotiate the fare before getting in.
  • Private transfer: For a hassle-free experience, pre-book a private transfer. I recommend checking Kiwitaxi for fixed prices and English-speaking drivers. A convenient transfer can be ₽2 000 – ₽3 500 (≈ $23 – $40) depending on your destination.
  • Rental car or scooter: If you’re comfortable driving, you can rent a car (₽1 500 – ₽2 500/day) or a scooter (₽300 – ₽500/day) from the airport. Note that traffic can be chaotic, and you’ll need an International Driving Permit.

Booking Tips

To get the best value for your stay in Koh Samui, keep these tips in mind:

  • Book early for peak season: December to February and July to August are the busiest times. Prices can double, so book at least 3-4 months ahead. Use Hotellook to compare rates across multiple platforms.
  • Consider the shoulder season: March to May and September to November offer lower prices and fewer crowds. You can often snag luxury rooms for mid-range prices.
  • Look for free cancellation: Many hotels on Booking.com offer free cancellation up to 24-48 hours before check-in. This gives you flexibility if your plans change.
  • Check for package deals: Sometimes booking a flight and hotel together through Aviasales can save you money, especially for longer stays.
  • Use a price comparison tool: You can ₽3 500 – ₽40 000+ per night (≈ $40 – $450+) to compare prices and find the best deal. I always filter by “guest rating” and “recent reviews” to avoid surprises.
  • Sign up for loyalty programs: If you’re a frequent traveler, consider booking directly with hotel chains (like Anantara or Banyan Tree) for perks like room upgrades or late checkout.

Pro tip: For digital nomads or long-term stays, consider renting a private villa through Airbnb—you’ll get more space and a kitchen for less than a hotel.

Koh Samui is a magical island that caters to every budget and style. Whether you’re sleeping in a rustic bungalow on Maenam or sipping champagne in a Four Seasons villa, the island’s warm hospitality and stunning landscapes will make your trip unforgettable. Happy travels!
