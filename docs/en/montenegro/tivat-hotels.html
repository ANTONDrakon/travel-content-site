If you're dreaming of a Montenegrin escape with turquoise waters, luxury yachts, and a laid-back Mediterranean vibe, Tivat is your spot. This charming coastal town has blossomed into a premier destination, thanks largely to the glittering Porto Montenegro marina and its world-class amenities. Whether you're a backpacker craving the Adriatic sun or a traveler seeking five-star indulgence, Tivat's hotel scene has something special for you.

The town is compact but diverse: the waterfront area buzzes with cafes and boutiques, while the surrounding hills offer quiet retreats with stunning bay views. Hotel prices in Tivat can vary wildly depending on the season—expect peak rates in July and August, and significant discounts in spring and fall.

Budget Hotels (under $50/night)

1. Hotel Astoria

A charming, family-run hotel in the heart of Tivat's old town. Rooms are simple but spotless, with authentic stone walls and wooden beams. The terrace offers a lovely view of the bay, and the staff treats you like family. It's a short walk to the main square and the waterfront.

Price: ₽4 500 (≈ $48) per night for a double room.

  • Pros: Central location, friendly atmosphere, free breakfast.
  • Cons: No pool, limited parking, basic amenities.

2. Guesthouse Villa Milica

Tucked away in the residential neighborhood of Donja Lastva, this guesthouse offers cozy rooms with kitchenettes. It's a 10-minute walk to the nearest beach and a short bus ride to the center. The garden is a peaceful spot for morning coffee.

Price: ₽3 700 (≈ $40) per night for a studio.

  • Pros: Quiet area, self-catering facilities, free parking.
  • Cons: Far from nightlife, basic furnishings, no breakfast.

3. Apartments Kovačević

These modern apartments sit on a hillside above Tivat, offering panoramic views of the bay. Each unit has a private balcony and a fully equipped kitchen. It's a 15-minute walk downhill to the center (the walk back up is steep!).

Price: ₽4 000 (≈ $43) per night for a one-bedroom apartment.

  • Pros: Stunning views, spacious, good value for groups.
  • Cons: Steep hill, no pool, limited dining options nearby.

Mid-Range Hotels ($50-150/night)

4. Hotel Pine

A well-established hotel in the center of Tivat, just steps from the marina. Rooms are clean and comfortable, with modern bathrooms. The on-site restaurant serves excellent Montenegrin cuisine, and the pool is a welcome retreat on hot days.

Price: ₽7 000 (≈ $75) per night for a double room.

Highlights: Central location, pool, good restaurant, friendly staff.

5. Hotel Helada

Located near the beach in the suburb of Belane, this hotel offers a relaxed atmosphere with direct access to a pebble beach. Rooms are spacious and many have sea views. The breakfast buffet is generous, and the bar serves cocktails by the water.

Price: ₽8 500 (≈ $92) per night for a sea-view double.

Highlights: Beachfront, sea views, great breakfast, free sunbeds.

6. Boutique Hotel La Roche

A stylish boutique hotel in the Porto Montenegro complex. Rooms are decorated with contemporary art and offer marina views. The rooftop terrace is perfect for sunset drinks, and the spa provides a range of treatments. It's ideal for couples.

Price: ₽12 000 (≈ $130) per night for a deluxe room.

Highlights: Prime location, rooftop bar, spa, chic design.

7. Hotel Palma

A family-friendly hotel near the center with a large outdoor pool and a playground. Rooms are simple but well-maintained, and the staff organizes activities like boat trips and wine tastings. It's a great choice for travelers with kids.

Price: ₽9 500 (≈ $103) per night for a family room.

Highlights: Family-oriented, pool, activities, good value.

Luxury Hotels ($150+/night)

8. Regent Porto Montenegro

The crown jewel of Tivat's luxury scene. This five-star hotel sits right on the marina, with rooms that blend Venetian elegance with modern comfort. Features include a stunning infinity pool, a world-class spa, and multiple gourmet restaurants. The service is impeccable.

Price: ₽23 000 (≈ $250) per night for a marina-view room.

Features: Infinity pool, spa, Michelin-starred dining, butler service, private beach club.

9. Hotel Mediteran

A luxury resort set in lush gardens on the outskirts of Tivat. Rooms are spacious and elegantly furnished, with private balconies overlooking the bay. The resort has three pools, a tennis court, and a wellness center. It's a peaceful escape from the marina buzz.

Price: ₽18 000 (≈ $195) per night for a suite.

Features: Multiple pools, tennis court, spa, garden setting, kids' club.

10. Villa Zelena

An exclusive boutique villa with just five suites, located on a hill with breathtaking views of the Bay of Kotor. Each suite is uniquely decorated, with antique furniture and modern amenities. The villa offers a private chef, a wine cellar, and a heated infinity pool.

Price: ₽28 000 (≈ $305) per night for a suite.

Features: Private chef, infinity pool, panoramic views, wine cellar, personalized service.

How to Choose the Right Area

Tivat is small but varied. Here's how to pick the best neighborhood based on your travel style:

For Beach Lovers

Stay near Belane beach or Donja Lastva. These areas offer pebble beaches, calm waters, and a relaxed vibe. Hotels like Hotel Helada and Guesthouse Villa Milica are excellent choices. You'll have easy access to the sea and beach bars, but you'll be a bit away from the main town center.

For Sightseeing Enthusiasts

Choose the old town or the Porto Montenegro area. You'll be steps away from the marina, the Naval Heritage Collection, and the Church of St. Rocco. Hotel Astoria and Regent Porto Montenegro put you right in the action. From here, you can easily walk to the ferry for day trips to Kotor or Herceg Novi.

For Nightlife Seekers

The Porto Montenegro waterfront is the epicenter of Tivat's nightlife. High-end bars and clubs line the marina, and the crowd is glamorous. Boutique Hotel La Roche and Regent Porto Montenegro are perfect for party-goers. If you prefer a quieter scene, the old town has cozy wine bars and live music venues.

Airport Transfers

Tivat Airport (TIV) is just 4 kilometers from the town center, making your arrival incredibly convenient. Most hotels are within a 10-15 minute drive. Taxis are readily available outside the terminal, with fixed rates to most hotels—expect to pay around ₽1 500 (≈ $16) for a standard ride.
