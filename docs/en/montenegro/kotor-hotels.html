Kotor, Montenegro, is one of those rare places where medieval stone walls meet the shimmering Adriatic Sea. I’ve spent countless hours wandering its cobbled streets, and I can tell you that choosing the right hotel here makes all the difference.

Whether you’re a backpacker on a shoestring or someone who wants to wake up to a private bay view, Kotor’s hotel scene has evolved beautifully for 2026. The infrastructure is solid: most hotels offer free Wi-Fi, air conditioning, and breakfast, while many mid-range and luxury properties now include rooftop pools, spa facilities, and electric car charging points.

Neighborhoods matter a lot. The Old Town (Stari Grad) is a UNESCO World Heritage site—compact, car-free, and packed with history. Staying inside the walls means you’re steps from St. Tryphon Cathedral and the Maritime Museum, but expect narrow lanes and limited parking. The Dobrota and Muo strips along the bay offer quieter beachfront stays with easy bus access to the Old Town (about 15 minutes on foot or a quick taxi ride).

Budget Hotels (under $50/night)

1. Hostel Kotor

A lively spot in the heart of the Old Town, Hostel Kotor is all about social vibes and affordability. Dorm beds start at ₽1 400 (≈ $15), while private doubles run around ₽4 200 (≈ $45). The staff organizes walking tours and pub crawls, and the communal kitchen is a bonus for self-catering. Pros: unbeatable location, friendly atmosphere, free walking tours. Cons: thin walls, noise from the courtyard until late, no parking.

2. Guesthouse Vela

Located in the quiet Dobrota neighborhood, Guesthouse Vela offers simple, clean rooms with bay views. A double room costs ₽3 700 (≈ $40). It’s a 20-minute walk to the Old Town, but there’s a bus stop right outside. Pros: peaceful setting, private balcony in some rooms, free street parking. Cons: basic furnishings, limited dining options nearby, no air conditioning in some rooms.

3. Apartments Šušanj

These family-run apartments sit on the hillside above Kotor, offering stunning panoramas for a steal. A studio apartment goes for ₽4 100 (≈ $45) per night. The owners are incredibly welcoming and often provide homemade rakija (local brandy) upon arrival. Pros: amazing views, fully equipped kitchenettes, free parking. Cons: steep walk to the Old Town (15-20 minutes downhill), no restaurant on-site, limited public transport.

Mid-Range Hotels ($50-150/night)

1. Hotel Marija

A charming boutique hotel just outside the Old Town walls, Hotel Marija combines traditional stone architecture with modern comforts. Double rooms start at ₽6 500 (≈ $70) and include a generous buffet breakfast. The terrace garden is a hidden gem for morning coffee. Highlights: free private parking (rare in Kotor), friendly multilingual staff, and a short 5-minute walk to the main square.

2. Palazzo Radomiri

This restored 18th-century palace in Dobrota is a masterpiece of heritage hospitality. Rooms feature original stone walls, wooden beams, and antique furniture, with prices from ₽9 200 (≈ $100) per night. The beach is just across the street, and the restaurant serves excellent local seafood. Highlights: unique historic atmosphere, direct beach access, excellent restaurant with bay views.

3. Hotel Hippocampus

Located right on the waterfront in the Old Town, Hotel Hippocampus offers modern rooms with a nautical theme. Doubles cost around ₽8 300 (≈ $90) and include a small but well-equipped gym. The rooftop bar is a popular spot for sunset cocktails. Highlights: prime Old Town location, rooftop terrace, soundproofed rooms for a quiet sleep.

4. Apartments Boka

These spacious apartments in Muo are perfect for families or longer stays. A one-bedroom apartment with a kitchenette and balcony starts at ₽7 400 (≈ $80) per night. The property has a small pool and sun loungers, plus a private dock for swimming. Highlights: full kitchen facilities, pool access, free kayak rental for guests.

Luxury Hotels ($150+/night)

1. Chedi Luštica Bay

Part of the exclusive Luštica Bay development, this five-star hotel is a 20-minute drive from Kotor Old Town. Rooms start at ₽18 400 (≈ $200) and feature minimalist design with floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking the marina. The spa offers hammam treatments and a hydrotherapy pool. Features: private beach club, multiple restaurants (including a Michelin-starred chef’s table), infinity pool, and a kids’ club.

2. Hotel Forza Mare

A small luxury boutique hotel in Dobrota with only 12 rooms, Forza Mare is all about personalized service. Doubles cost ₽20 700 (≈ $225) per night, including a gourmet breakfast. The property has a private pier, a saltwater pool, and a wine cellar with over 200 labels. Features: butler service, complimentary yacht transfers to nearby beaches, cooking classes with the chef.

3. Aman Sveti Stefan

While technically a 30-minute drive from Kotor, this legendary resort on the island of Sveti Stefan is the ultimate luxury escape. Villas start at ₽55 200 (≈ $600) per night, offering total privacy and world-class service. The property includes a private beach, a spa with traditional Montenegrin treatments, and a Michelin-starred restaurant. Features: dedicated villa hosts, private butler, helicopter transfers available, access to the island’s historic chapel and gardens.

How to Choose the Right Area

For Beach Lovers

Stay in Dobrota or Muo along the bay. These areas have small pebble beaches and easy access to swimming platforms. Hotels like Palazzo Radomiri and Apartments Boka are ideal. You’ll be a short bus ride from the Old Town but can enjoy quieter evenings by the water.

For Sightseeing Enthusiasts

Choose the Old Town itself. Being inside the walls means you can explore St. Tryphon Cathedral, the Maritime Museum, and the city walls without needing transport. Hotel Hippocampus or Hostel Kotor are great bases. Just be prepared for crowds during peak season (July-August).

For Nightlife Seekers

The Old Town has the most bars and clubs, but for a livelier scene, head to the waterfront promenade near the Sea Gate. Hotel Mariya is a good mid-range option here. If you want a party vibe, consider staying in nearby Tivat (15 minutes by taxi) where the Porto Montenegro marina has upscale nightclubs.

Airport Transfers

Tivat Airport (TIV) is the closest, just 10-15 minutes from Kotor by car. A taxi from the airport to your hotel costs around ₽1 800 (≈ $20).
