15 Best Things to Do in Tivat 2026

Tivat often gets overlooked by travelers rushing to Kotor or Budva, but that’s exactly why I love it. This charming Bay of Kotor town blends maritime elegance with laid-back Mediterranean vibes. In 2026, Tivat is more than just a stopover—it’s a destination with world-class marinas, historic parks, and hidden beaches. Whether you’re docking your yacht at Porto Montenegro or backpacking on a budget, these 15 attractions will make your trip unforgettable.

Why visit Tivat for its attractions? Unlike crowded Old Towns, Tivat offers a relaxed pace. You get the luxury of Porto Montenegro, the tranquility of Lustica Peninsula, and the history of a 15th-century summer house—all within a short walk or drive. Plus, the views of the bay from any hilltop are pure magic.

15 Best Attractions

1. Porto Montenegro

The epicenter of Tivat’s glamour. This luxury marina is lined with designer boutiques, waterfront cafes, and superyachts. Even if you’re not shopping, the promenade is perfect for a sunset stroll.

  • Ticket price: Free entry (₽0)
  • Hours: 24/7, but shops open 10:00–22:00
  • How to get there: Walk from Tivat city center (10 minutes) or take bus #1 from the bus station (₽50 / ≈ $0.55)
  • Tip: Visit at golden hour for the best yacht photos.

2. Tivat City Park (Park Tivatskih Topola)

A lush green oasis in the heart of town. The park features century-old trees, a small lake with swans, and a playground. Locals come here for morning coffee at the kiosk.

  • Ticket price: Free (₽0)
  • Hours: 24/7
  • How to get there: It’s right behind the main post office; a 5-minute walk from the bus station.
  • Tip: Buy a scoop of homemade ice cream from the kiosk (₽150 / ≈ $1.65).

3. Boka Tower (Clock Tower)

This 16th-century Venetian clock tower stands in the main square. It’s a simple but iconic landmark, often used as a meeting point. The surrounding square hosts a small market on weekends.

  • Ticket price: Free (₽0)
  • Hours: Always visible
  • How to get there: It’s in the center, next to the Tivat City Park.
  • Tip: Pair it with a visit to the nearby Church of St. Sava.

4. Church of St. Sava

A Serbian Orthodox church built in 1904. The interior is adorned with beautiful frescoes, and the bell tower offers panoramic views of the bay. It’s a peaceful spot for reflection.

  • Ticket price: Free (₽0) – donations welcome
  • Hours: 8:00–18:00
  • How to get there: Walk up the hill from the main square (10 minutes).
  • Tip: Climb the bell tower for a small donation (₽100 / ≈ $1.10).

5. Naval Heritage Collection

A museum inside Porto Montenegro dedicated to the region’s maritime history. You’ll see model ships, uniforms, and artifacts from the Yugoslav Navy. It’s small but well-curated.

  • Ticket price: ₽350 (≈ $3.85)
  • Hours: 10:00–18:00 (closed Mondays)
  • How to get there: Inside Porto Montenegro, near the Regent Hotel.
  • Tip: Combine with a visit to the nearby Maritime Museum in Kotor for a full naval history experience.

6. Plavi Horizonti Beach (Blue Horizons)

The most popular beach on the Lustica Peninsula. Soft white sand, crystal-clear water, and a lively bar scene. It gets crowded in July and August, so arrive early.

  • Ticket price: Free entry; sunbeds from ₽1 000 (≈ $11) per day
  • Hours: 24/7 (lifeguards 9:00–19:00)
  • How to get there: Take bus #5 from Tivat bus station to Lustica (₽150 / ≈ $1.65), then walk 15 minutes.
  • Tip: Bring water shoes—the seabed has some pebbles.

7. Lustica Peninsula Hiking Trails

For active travelers, the trails along the Lustica Peninsula offer stunning coastal views. The path from Plavi Horizonti to Mirište Beach is a 5-km hike with wild rosemary and olive trees.

  • Ticket price: Free (₽0)
  • Hours: Daylight hours
  • How to get there: Start from Plavi Horizonti beach or take a taxi to Radovici village (₽600 / ≈ $6.60).
  • Tip: Download a GPS map—trails are unmarked in some sections.

8. Mirište Beach

A quieter alternative to Plavi Horizonti, with a pebble shore and a small restaurant. The water is incredibly clear, perfect for snorkeling. It’s a hidden gem.

  • Ticket price: Free (₽0)
  • Hours: 24/7
  • How to get there: Hike from Plavi Horizonti (30 minutes) or take a taxi from Tivat (₽800 / ≈ $8.80).
  • Tip: The restaurant serves fresh grilled fish for around ₽1 500 (≈ $16.50).

9. Tivat Solila (Salt Pans)

A protected nature reserve on the outskirts of town. It’s a birdwatcher’s paradise—herons, flamingos, and ducks stop here during migration. The boardwalk is peaceful.

  • Ticket price: Free (₽0)
  • Hours: 24/7
  • How to get there: Walk from the bus station along the coast (20 minutes) or take bus #3 (₽50 / ≈ $0.55).
  • Tip: Bring binoculars for the best bird spotting.

10. Church of St. Rocco (Donja Lastva)

A tiny stone church in the fishing village of Donja Lastva, just south of Tivat. It’s a popular spot for wedding photos and offers a serene view of the bay. The surrounding village has a lovely promenade.

  • Ticket price: Free (₽0)
  • Hours: Always open
  • How to get there: Walk along the waterfront from Tivat (25 minutes) or take bus #2 (₽50 / ≈ $0.55).
  • Tip: Have lunch at one of the konobas (taverns) on the promenade—try the black risotto (₽1 200 / ≈ $13.20).

11. Porto Montenegro Regatta Course

If you’re visiting in October, the Porto Montenegro Regatta is a must-see. Watch sleek sailboats race on the bay. Even if there’s no regatta, the course is used for sailing lessons.

  • Ticket price: Free to watch (₽0)
  • Hours: Regatta days 10:00–17:00; sailing lessons available year-round
  • How to get there: View from Porto Montenegro promenade.
  • Tip: Book a sailing lesson with Porto Montenegro Sailing Club (₽4 500 / ≈ $49.50 per hour).

12. Gornja Lastva (Upper Lastva)

A traditional mountain village overlooking Tivat. The stone houses and narrow streets feel like stepping back in time. There’s a small church and a viewpoint with a bench.

  • Ticket price: Free (₽0)
  • Hours: 24/7
  • How to get there: Hike up from Donja Lastva (45 minutes) or take a taxi (₽500 / ≈ $5.50).
  • Tip: Pack a picnic—there are no restaurants.

13. Kalimanj Beach

A small, family-friendly beach near the city center. It has a concrete platform for sunbathing and a shallow swimming area for kids. It’s not as glamorous as Lustica beaches, but it’s convenient.

  • Ticket price: Free (₽0)
  • Hours: 24/7
  • How to get there: Walk from the main square (10 minutes east).
  • Tip: Grab a coffee at the nearby beach bar (₽200 / ≈ $2.20).

14. Tivat Local Market (Green Market)

Every morning, local farmers sell fresh produce, olives, cheese, and honey. It’s a great place to buy picnic supplies or souvenirs like homemade rakija. The market is behind the main square.

  • Ticket price: Free entry (₽0)
  • Hours: 7:00–14:00 daily
  • How to get there: A 3-minute walk from Boka Tower.
  • Tip: Try the local goat cheese (₽500 / ≈ $5.50 per 200g).

15. Lustica Bay Golf Course

An 18-hole championship golf course designed by Gary Player. It opened in 2022 and offers stunning views of the bay. Even if you don’t play, the clubhouse restaurant is worth a visit.

  • Ticket price: Green fee ₽6 500 (≈ $71.50); club rental ₽3 000 (≈ $33)
  • Hours: 7:00–19:00
  • How to get there: Take a taxi from Tivat (₽800 / ≈ $8.80) or the free shuttle from Porto Montenegro.
  • Tip: Book tee times in advance via their website.

1-Day, 2-Day, 3-Day Itineraries

1-Day Itinerary

Morning (9:00–12:00): Start at Porto Montenegro for a coffee and a stroll. Visit the Naval Heritage Collection (₽350). Then walk to Tivat City Park and Boka Tower.

Lunch (12:00–13:30): Eat at a konoba in Donja Lastva (₽1 200 for a meal).

Afternoon (13:30–17:00): Take bus #5 to Plavi Horizonti Beach for swimming and sunbathing.

Evening (17:00–20:00): Return to Porto Montenegro for sunset drinks. Dinner at a waterfront restaurant (₽2 500 / ≈ $27.50).

2-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Same as above, but skip the beach. Instead, hike to Gornja Lastva in the afternoon (2 hours round trip).

Day 2 (9:00–16:00): Take a boat tour to the Blue Cave and Mamula Island. Tip: You can book boat tours on GetYourGuide from ₽3 500 (≈ $38.50). Afternoon at Mirište Beach.

Evening (16:00–20:00): Visit Tivat Solila for birdwatching, then dinner at the market area.

3-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Same as 1-day itinerary.

Day 2: Same as 2-day itinerary Day 2.

Day 3 (9:00–17:00): Play golf at Lustica Bay (₽6 500) or hike the Lustica Peninsula trails. Afternoon at Kalimanj Beach. Evening: explore the local market for souvenirs.

Practical Tips

Transportation

Tivat is walkable, but for Lustica Peninsula, use bus #5 (₽150) or taxis (₽500–₽800). For Kotor or Budva, buses run every 30 minutes from the main bus station (₽200–₽300). You can book private transfers via Kiwitaxi for convenience. For eSIM data, Airalo offers Montenegro plans from ₽400 (≈ $4.40) for 1 GB.

Combo Tickets

There are no official combo tickets for Tivat attractions. However, some boat tours on GetYourGuide include multiple stops (Blue Cave, Mamula, and Lady of the Rocks) for one price (₽4 000 / ≈ $44). For accommodation, Booking.com often has deals with free breakfast included.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do attractions cost in Tivat?

Entrance fees vary: from free attractions to $20-30 for museums. Many venues have discount days.

Do I need a guide in Tivat?

A guide is recommended for historical sites and complex routes. Self-guided travel is possible with audio guides and maps.
