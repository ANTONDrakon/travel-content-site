FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the travel guides free?

Yes, all guides on the site are free. We earn through affiliate partnerships — this costs you nothing extra and doesn't affect prices.

How to book hotels cheaper?

Book 2-3 months ahead. Compare prices on multiple platforms. Low season prices are 30-50% lower. We recommend checking Aviasales and Ostrovok.

Do I need a visa?

It depends on the destination. Many countries offer visa-free entry: Turkey, Thailand, Egypt, Montenegro, Georgia. Check the guide for each country.

How to get travel insurance?

Insurance is essential for international trips. We recommend EKTA, Tripinsurance, or Cherehapa. Costs start from $2-5 per day.

How to get to the resort?

Each guide includes a "How to get there" section. We recommend searching flights on Aviasales and booking transfers through Kiwitaxi or GetTransfer.

Do I need an eSIM?

For international trips, eSIM is convenient. We recommend Airalo or Drimsim. Costs start from $3-5 per GB. Activate online, no need to find local SIM cards.

How to get help with planning?

Leave a request on the site or contact us. We'll help with destination, hotel, itinerary, visas, and insurance. Free of charge.

How do you make money?

We earn a small commission when you book through affiliate links. This costs you nothing extra and doesn't affect the price. We don't sell tours directly — we only recommend trusted services.
