Kazan, the vibrant capital of Tatarstan, is a city where East meets West in the most enchanting way. With its stunning Kremlin, lively streets along the Kazanka River, and a food scene that blends Russian and Tatar flavors, it's no wonder more travelers are adding this gem to their itineraries. Whether you're here for the rich history, the modern cultural scene, or just to soak in the unique atmosphere, finding the right place to stay makes all the difference.

Kazan's hotel infrastructure has grown impressively in recent years. You'll find everything from sleek business hotels near the city center to cozy boutique options in quieter neighborhoods. The Kremlin area (Bauman Street and surrounding blocks) is the heart of the action, with most major sights within walking distance. The newer "Kazan-City" district along the river offers modern high-rises and business hotels, while the historic Tatar settlement area gives you a more authentic, local vibe. Public transport is reliable, with the metro connecting key points, so even staying a bit further out is easy.

Prices in Kazan are generally more affordable than in Moscow or St. Petersburg, but they vary by season. Summer (June-August) and the New Year holidays see the highest rates, while spring and autumn offer good deals. On average, budget hotels start around ₽3 500 (≈ $35) per night, mid-range options run ₽5 000–₽12 000 (≈ $50–$120), and luxury properties can exceed ₽15 000 (≈ $150). Budget Hotels (under $50/night)

1. Hostel MARCO POLO Kazan

Located just a 10-minute walk from the Kremlin, this modern hostel offers both dormitory beds and private rooms. The common area is spacious and social, with a fully equipped kitchen for self-catering. It's clean, secure, and popular with backpackers and solo travelers.

Price: Dorm bed from ₽1 200 (≈ $12); private room from ₽3 000 (≈ $30).

  • Pros: Great location, friendly staff, free Wi-Fi, communal vibe.
  • Cons: Shared bathrooms in dorms, can be noisy on weekends.

2. Hotel Volga Kazan

A no-frills option right on Bauman Street, the main pedestrian thoroughfare. Rooms are basic but clean, with essential amenities like TV, fridge, and private bathroom. The hotel has a small café serving breakfast for an extra fee.

Price: Double room from ₽3 800 (≈ $38).

  • Pros: Unbeatable central location, affordable, 24-hour reception.
  • Cons: Outdated décor, thin walls, no elevator.

3. Ibis Kazan

Part of the reliable Ibis chain, this hotel is a 15-minute walk from the Kremlin and near the metro. Rooms are compact but functional, with soundproofing and blackout curtains. The on-site restaurant serves a decent buffet breakfast.

Price: Double room from ₽4 200 (≈ $42).

  • Pros: Consistent quality, good value, free Wi-Fi, pet-friendly.
  • Cons: Minimalist design, no frills, breakfast costs extra.

Mid-Range Hotels ($50-150/night)

4. Courtyard by Marriott Kazan Kremlin

This hotel sits right next to the Kremlin walls, offering stunning views of the towers and the Qolşärif Mosque. Rooms are modern and spacious, with large windows and comfortable beds. The fitness center and business lounge are pluses for work travelers.

Price: Double room from ₽6 500 (≈ $65).

Highlights: Prime location, rooftop terrace with Kremlin views, solid breakfast buffet.

5. DoubleTree by Hilton Kazan City

Located in the Kazan-City business district, this hotel is a 5-minute drive from the Kremlin. Rooms are sleek and contemporary, with floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking the river or city skyline. The hotel has a spa, indoor pool, and a stylish restaurant.

Price: Double room from ₽8 000 (≈ $80).

Highlights: Modern design, excellent spa facilities, free shuttle to city center.

6. Shushly Hotel

A charming boutique hotel in the historic Tatar settlement, just across the river from the Kremlin. The building is a restored 19th-century merchant house, with individually decorated rooms featuring traditional Tatar textiles. The on-site restaurant serves authentic local cuisine.

Price: Double room from ₽7 200 (≈ $72).

Highlights: Unique character, quiet neighborhood, excellent Tatar restaurant, free parking.

7. Kazan Palace by Tasigo

This stylish hotel blends Ottoman-inspired architecture with modern luxury. Rooms are spacious, with ornate decor and marble bathrooms. The spa includes a hammam and indoor pool, and the rooftop bar offers panoramic views. It's a 10-minute walk from the Kremlin.

Price: Double room from ₽10 500 (≈ $105).

Highlights: Luxurious ambiance, rooftop bar, spa and hammam, good for couples.

Luxury Hotels ($150+/night)

8. Ramada by Wyndham Kazan

A high-rise hotel in the Kazan-City district with 4-star amenities and 5-star service. Rooms are large and elegantly furnished, with city or river views. The hotel features a full-service spa, fitness center, and multiple dining options, including a Japanese restaurant.

Price: Double room from ₽14 000 (≈ $140).

Features: Sky lounge, indoor pool, business center, valet parking.

9. Hilton Garden Inn Kazan

Located near the Kazan Arena and the riverfront, this hotel offers modern comfort with a touch of sophistication. Rooms are soundproofed and come with ergonomic workstations. The hotel has a 24-hour convenience store and a restaurant serving international dishes.

Price: Double room from ₽16 000 (≈ $160).

Features: Free Wi-Fi, fitness center, on-site parking, close to sports venues.

10. Mirage Hotel Kazan

The only true 5-star hotel in Kazan, located right on Bauman Street. Rooms are opulent, with classic European decor, marble bathrooms, and butler service. The hotel has a grand ballroom, a luxury spa, and a restaurant with a Michelin-trained chef.

Price: Double room from ₽22 000 (≈ $220).

Features: Butler service, luxury spa, fine dining restaurant, concierge, limousine service.

How to Choose the Right Area

Kazan is compact but diverse, and your choice of neighborhood can shape your entire trip. Here's how to pick based on your priorities:

For Sightseeing: Kremlin Area & Bauman Street

Stay within a 15-minute walk of the Kazan Kremlin to be close to the main attractions: the Qolşärif Mosque, Annunciation Cathedral, and the Hermitage-Kazan Center. Bauman Street is a pedestrian zone full of shops, cafes, and street performers. Hotels like Courtyard by Marriott or Hotel Volga put you right in the action.

For Beach & Relaxation: Kazanka Riverfront

The Kazanka River embankment has parks, walking paths, and small beaches during summer. The Kazan-City district (near the river) offers modern hotels like DoubleTree or Ramada with river views. For a quieter stay, the Tatar settlement area across the river is more residential and peaceful.

For Nightlife: Bauman Street & Downtown

Kazan's nightlife is centered around Bauman Street and the surrounding blocks. Here you'll find bars, nightclubs, and live music venues. The Mirage Hotel and Kazan Palace are both within walking distance of the best spots. If you prefer a quieter evening, choose the Tatar settlement or Kazan-City area.

Booking Tips

Saving money on your Kazan hotel is easy with a few smart strategies:

  • Book early: For summer and New Year, book at least 2-3 months in advance. For other seasons, 3-4 weeks ahead is fine.
  • Use price comparison tools: Check Aviasales for flight+hotel combos, and Hotellook to compare rates across booking sites.
  • Consider package deals: Some platforms like Booking offer discounts for bundled flights and hotels.
  • Look for free cancellation: Many mid-range and luxury hotels offer free cancellation up to 24 hours before check-in. Use this flexibility to lock in a good rate and keep searching for better deals.
  • Check for hidden fees: Some budget hotels charge extra for breakfast, Wi-Fi (rare, but possible), or parking. Always read the fine print.
  • Use local booking sites: Russian platforms like Ostrovok.ru sometimes have lower rates for domestic travelers, but international sites are fine for most visitors.
