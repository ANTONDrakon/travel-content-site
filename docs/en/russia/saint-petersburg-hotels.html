Top 10 Hotels in Saint Petersburg 2026: Budget to Luxury

Saint Petersburg is a city of canals, imperial palaces, and vibrant cultural life — and finding the right hotel can make or break your trip. I’ve spent years exploring this magnificent Russian city, and I know that the perfect accommodation depends on your budget, travel style, and what you want to see. Whether you’re a backpacker on a shoestring, a family seeking comfort, or a couple craving five-star luxury, Saint Petersburg has something for you.

The city’s hotel infrastructure is well-developed, with options ranging from Soviet-era hostels to modern international chains and boutique properties. The most popular neighborhoods for tourists are the historic center (around Nevsky Prospekt and the Admiralteysky District), Vasilievsky Island for a quieter vibe, and the Petrograd Side for parks and river views. Prices in Saint Petersburg can vary widely depending on the season — expect peak rates from May to September and during the White Nights festival in June. Budget Hotels (under $50/night)

1. Friends Hostel on Nevsky

Located right on Nevsky Prospekt, this hostel offers clean, modern dormitories and private rooms. It’s a favorite among backpackers for its social atmosphere and free walking tours. Prices start at ₽1 500 (≈ $16) per night for a dorm bed and ₽3 500 (≈ $38) for a private room.

  • Pros: Central location, friendly staff, free breakfast.
  • Cons: Can be noisy at night, shared bathrooms in dorms.

2. Soul Kitchen Hostel

A cozy, artsy hostel near the Church of the Savior on Spilled Blood. It features a communal kitchen, a rooftop terrace, and regular cultural events. Dorm beds from ₽1 800 (≈ $20); private doubles from ₽4 000 (≈ $44).

  • Pros: Unique design, great location, free Wi-Fi.
  • Cons: Limited parking, no elevator.

3. Hotel Indigo St. Petersburg – Nevsky Prospekt

Despite the name, this is a budget-friendly boutique hotel with stylish rooms and a 24-hour front desk. It’s a bit off the main drag but still walkable to major sights. Rooms from ₽4 500 (≈ $49) per night.

  • Pros: Good value for a private room, quiet area.
  • Cons: Small rooms, basic amenities.

Mid-Range Hotels ($50-150/night)

4. W St. Petersburg

A trendy hotel on Voznesensky Prospekt with a rooftop bar and modern design. Rooms feature floor-to-ceiling windows and rainfall showers. Prices start at ₽6 000 (≈ $65) per night.

  • Highlights: Excellent service, central location, great for couples.

5. Radisson Royal Hotel St. Petersburg

This luxury chain hotel offers spacious rooms and a full-service spa, including a pool and sauna. It’s located near the Moskovsky Railway Station, making it convenient for train travelers. Rooms from ₽8 000 (≈ $87) per night.

  • Highlights: Great for business travelers, family-friendly, breakfast buffet included.

6. Hotel Astoria

A historic property near St. Isaac’s Cathedral, blending classic elegance with modern comfort. The hotel has a renowned restaurant and a fitness center. Prices from ₽10 000 (≈ $109) per night.

  • Highlights: Stunning architecture, walking distance to the Hermitage, attentive staff.

7. Sokos Hotel Palace Bridge

A Finnish chain hotel on Vasilievsky Island, offering stylish rooms and a rooftop bar with panoramic views of the Neva River. It’s a short walk to the Kunstkamera and the Spit of Vasilievsky Island. Rooms from ₽9 000 (≈ $98) per night.

  • Highlights: Great value for the area, modern amenities, free bike rentals.

Luxury Hotels ($150+/night)

8. Four Seasons Hotel Lion Palace St. Petersburg

Housed in a 19th-century palace, this five-star hotel offers opulent rooms with marble bathrooms and butler service. The spa features a hammam and a pool. Prices start at ₽25 000 (≈ $272) per night.

  • Features: Michelin-starred restaurant, concierge for private tours, central location near St. Isaac’s.

9. Belmond Grand Hotel Europe

A legendary hotel on Nevsky Prospekt, known for its Art Nouveau interiors and world-class service. It has hosted celebrities and royalty for over a century. Rooms from ₽30 000 (≈ $326) per night.

  • Features: Multiple fine dining restaurants, a luxurious spa, and a grand ballroom.

10. Kempinski Hotel Moika 22

Overlooking the Moika River, this hotel offers elegant rooms with river views and a rooftop terrace. It’s steps from the Hermitage and Palace Square. Prices from ₽28 000 (≈ $304) per night.

  • Features: Private boat tours, a cigar lounge, and a 24-hour fitness center.

How to Choose the Right Area

Your choice of neighborhood can transform your Saint Petersburg experience. Here’s a quick guide based on your travel purpose:

  • Beach lovers: Head to the Petrograd Side (near the Peter and Paul Fortress) or the Gulf of Finland beaches in Sestroretsk (a 30-minute train ride). Hotels like Sokos Hotel Palace Bridge offer easy access to the waterfront.
  • Sightseeing: Stay in the historic center around Nevsky Prospekt or Admiralteysky District. You’ll be walking distance to the Hermitage, Church of the Savior on Spilled Blood, and St. Isaac’s Cathedral. Try Hotel Astoria or Four Seasons for luxury, or Friends Hostel on a budget.
  • Nightlife: The area around Dumskaya Street and Rubinshteina Street is the epicenter of bars, clubs, and live music. Mid-range options like W St. Petersburg put you in the action. For a quieter night, stay on Vasilievsky Island but take a taxi to the party zone.

Booking Tips

To save money and secure the best room, follow these tips:

  • Book early: For summer visits (especially June’s White Nights), book at least 2–3 months in advance. Use Aviasales for flights and Hotellook for hotel comparisons.
  • Consider package deals: Some hotels offer discounts when you book tours through GetYourGuide or Kiwitaxi transfers.
  • Use price alerts: Set alerts on Booking or Agoda for your chosen dates — prices often drop 1–2 weeks before check-in.
  • Check for hidden fees: Some hotels charge extra for breakfast or Wi-Fi. Read the fine print before booking.
  • Stay connected: Buy a local SIM or use an eSIM from Airalo to avoid roaming charges.
