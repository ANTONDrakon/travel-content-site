How to Find Cheap Flights to Sortavala 2026

Sortavala, a charming town on the shores of Lake Ladoga in Russia's Republic of Karelia, is a hidden gem for nature lovers and history enthusiasts. Known for its stunning archipelago, rugged forests, and the nearby Valaam Monastery, it's a destination that feels worlds away from the hustle of city life. If you're planning a trip in 2026, finding affordable flights is the first step to making your dream adventure a reality.

The nearest major airport is Petrozavodsk Airport (PES), located about 260 kilometers from Sortavala. From there, you can take a scenic bus or train ride through Karelia's pine forests. In this guide, I'll share practical tips, estimated prices, and insider hacks to help you snag cheap flights to Sortavala, whether you're flying from Moscow, St. Petersburg, or international destinations.

Direct Flights

Direct flights to Petrozavodsk Airport (PES) are limited but convenient for travelers from major Russian cities. As of 2026, the following airlines operate direct routes:

  • Severstal Aircompany – Flights from Moscow (Sheremetyevo, SVO) to Petrozavodsk (PES). Estimated one-way price: ₽5 000 – ₽10 000 (≈ $55 – $110). Frequency: 2-3 times weekly.
  • RusLine – Seasonal flights from St. Petersburg (LED) to Petrozavodsk (PES). Estimated one-way price: ₽4 000 – ₽8 000 (≈ $44 – $88). Frequency: 1-2 times weekly during summer.
  • Pobeda – Budget flights from Moscow (Vnukovo, VKO) to Petrozavodsk (PES). Estimated one-way price: ₽3 000 – ₽6 500 (≈ $33 – $72). Frequency: Daily during peak season.

Note that direct flights are most common from May to September, aligning with Sortavala's peak tourist season. For other months, you may need to rely on connecting flights. I recommend checking Aviasales for the latest schedules and prices, as airlines sometimes add seasonal routes.

Connecting Flights

If direct flights are too expensive or unavailable, connecting flights offer more flexibility and often lower prices. The cheapest options typically involve a layover in Moscow or St. Petersburg. Here's what to expect:

  • From International Destinations: Fly into Moscow (SVO, DME, VKO) or St. Petersburg (LED), then connect to Petrozavodsk (PES). Example: A flight from Bangkok (BKK) to Petrozavodsk via Moscow can cost ₽25 000 – ₽40 000 (≈ $275 – $440) round-trip, with a layover of 3-6 hours. Total duration: 12-18 hours.
  • From Russian Cities: Connect through Moscow or St. Petersburg. For instance, a flight from Yekaterinburg (SVX) to Petrozavodsk via Moscow might cost ₽8 000 – ₽15 000 (≈ $88 – $165) one-way, with a 2-4 hour layover. Total duration: 6-10 hours.
  • Budget Airlines: Consider combining a budget flight to Moscow (e.g., with Pobeda or Smartavia) and then a separate ticket to Petrozavodsk. This can save money but requires careful timing. For example, a flight from Sochi (AER) to Moscow (VKO) for ₽3 500 (≈ $38), plus a Moscow-Petrozavodsk flight for ₽4 000 (≈ $44), totals ₽7 500 (≈ $82) – much cheaper than a direct flight.

Flights can be compared across dates using Aviasales or Hotellook to find the cheapest combinations. For example, flying on a Tuesday or Wednesday often reduces costs by 15-20%. I also suggest using Booking for accommodation near Petrozavodsk if you have an overnight layover – a budget hotel room costs around ₽2 500 (≈ $28) per night.

10 Hacks for Cheap Flights

Here are my top strategies for saving money on flights to Sortavala, with specific tips and services:

  1. Use Incognito Mode – Airlines and booking sites often track your searches and raise prices. Search for flights in private browsing mode on Aviasales to avoid this.
  2. Fly Mid-Week – Tuesdays and Wednesdays are typically cheapest. For example, a Moscow-Petrozavodsk flight on a Tuesday might cost ₽6 000 (≈ $66), versus ₽9 000 (≈ $99) on a Friday.
  3. Book Connecting Flights Separately – Sometimes booking two one-way tickets (e.g., Moscow to St. Petersburg with Pobeda, then St. Petersburg to Petrozavodsk with RusLine) is cheaper than a single booking. Check Aviasales for combinations.
  4. Set Price Alerts – Use Aviasales to monitor price drops. When a flight to Petrozavodsk drops below ₽5 000 (≈ $55), grab it immediately.
  5. Consider Nearby Airports – If Petrozavodsk is expensive, fly to Lappeenranta (Finland, LPP) or Helsinki (HEL), then take a bus to Sortavala (about 4-5 hours). A flight from Moscow to Helsinki can cost ₽10 000 (≈ $110), plus a bus for ₽2 000 (≈ $22).
  6. Use Budget Airlines – Pobeda and Smartavia often have sales. For instance, Pobeda's Moscow-Petrozavodsk route can drop to ₽2 500 (≈ $28) during promotions. Follow them on social media for flash deals.
  7. Book Through Aggregators – Hotellook compares flight and hotel packages, sometimes offering discounts. A flight+hotel deal for 3 nights in Sortavala might save 10-15%.
  8. Travel Light – Budget airlines charge for checked luggage. A carry-on only (up to 10 kg) saves ₽1 500 – ₽3 000 (≈ $17 – $33) per leg. Pack layers for Karelia's variable weather.
  9. Use Kiwitaxi for Transfers – After landing, a pre-booked transfer from Petrozavodsk to Sortavala via Kiwitaxi costs around ₽6 000 (≈ $66) for a car, but if you share with other travelers (e.g., via Facebook groups), it can be ₽2 000 (≈ $22) per person.
  10. Stay Connected with Airalo – To avoid roaming fees, buy an eSIM from Airalo for Russia. A 7-day plan with 1GB data costs ₽500 (≈ $5.50) – perfect for checking flight updates and maps.

When to Book

Timing is crucial for cheap flights. Based on 2026 trends:

  • Domestic Flights (within Russia): Book 4-6 weeks in advance for the best prices. For example, a Moscow-Petrozavodsk flight booked 5 weeks out might cost ₽7 000 (≈ $77), versus ₽12 000 (≈ $132) if booked 2 weeks before.
  • International Flights: Book 3-4 months ahead. A flight from Europe to Petrozavodsk via Moscow, booked 3 months early, could be ₽25 000 (≈ $275), but last-minute bookings can exceed ₽40 000 (≈ $440).
  • Peak Season (June-August): Book at least 3 months in advance. For July, I recommend booking by March to secure seats under ₽15 000 (≈ $165) round-trip.
  • Off-Peak (November-February): You can often book 2-3 weeks ahead and still find deals under ₽8 000 (≈ $88). However, flights are less frequent, so check Aviasales for availability.

Pro tip: Set a price alert on Aviasales for your route. When the price drops by 20% or more, book immediately – such deals often sell out within hours.

Flight Insurance

Even with careful planning, flights can be delayed or cancelled, especially in Karelia's unpredictable weather. Here's what to do:

  • Check Your Rights: Under Russian law, if your flight is delayed by 2+ hours, the airline must provide refreshments (₽300-500 ≈ $3-5). For delays over 8 hours, they must offer hotel accommodation (₽1 000-2 000 ≈ $11-22 per night). Always ask for written confirmation.
  • File a Compensation Claim: For delays over 3 hours, you may be entitled to compensation of 25% of the minimum wage per hour (approx. ₽500 ≈ $5.50 per hour, max ₽5 000 ≈ $55). Use Aviasales or Hotellook to track your claim.
  • Travel Insurance: I strongly recommend purchasing flight insurance through Booking or a dedicated provider. Policies covering trip interruption and delay cost around ₽1 500-3 000 (≈ $17-33) for a week-long trip. For example, if your flight is cancelled due to snow, insurance can reimburse up to ₽20 000 (≈ $220) for rebooking and accommodation.
  • What to Do at the Airport: If delayed, stay near the gate and listen for announcements. Download the airline's app for real-time updates. If the delay exceeds 4 hours, ask for a meal voucher – most airlines provide one worth ₽500-1 000 (≈ $5.50-11).

For compensation claims, you can also use Kiwitaxi to arrange alternative transport if needed, such as a taxi from Petrozavodsk to Sortavala for around ₽6 000 (≈ $66). Remember, patience is key – Karelia's winter storms can cause disruptions, but the stunning landscapes are worth the wait.
