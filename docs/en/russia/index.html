Information

Practical information

🛂 Visa

For Russian citizens — internal passport. Visa-free travel across the country.

💱 Currency

Ruble (RUB). Bank cards accepted everywhere. Yandex.Pay, SBP system.

🗣 Language

Russian. English spoken in tourist areas.

🚌 Transport

Flights (Aeroflot, S7, Pobeda), RJD trains (Sapsan, Lastochka), metro, buses, taxi (Yandex, Citymobil).

🛡 Safety

Standard precautions. Tourist areas are safe. Police assist tourists.

📶 Internet & SIM

Mobile internet (MTS, Beeline, MegaFon, Tele2). Wi-Fi in hotels, cafes, transport.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a visa for Russia?

No, Russian citizens need only an internal passport. Visa-free travel across the country.

What budget do I need?

Economy: from $150/week. Comfort: from $500. Premium: from $1,500.

When is the best time to visit?

Summer (Jun–Aug) for hiking and beaches. Winter (Dec–Feb) for ice skating and skiing.

How to get there?

Flights from any city. RJD trains. Car rental available.

Where to go with children?

Sochi — beaches and parks. Moscow — museums and water parks. Karelia — nature and lakes.

Is it safe to travel in Russia?

Yes, standard precautions apply. Tourist areas are safe.

What excursions are popular?

Red Square, Hermitage, Peterhof, Kizhi, Hot Key. Excursions from ₽500.
