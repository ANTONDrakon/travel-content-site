Moscow Airports: Which One to Choose?

Moscow is served by three major airports, each with its own strengths. Knowing which one to fly into can save you both time and money.

  • Sheremetyevo (SVO) – The busiest and most connected, especially for international flights from Europe, Asia, and the Middle East. Aeroflot's hub. Expect direct flights from London, Paris, Dubai, and Beijing. Average taxi to city center: ₽2 000–₽3 500 (≈ $22–$38).
  • Domodedovo (DME) – Popular for flights from the Middle East, Southeast Asia, and domestic routes. Known for efficient service. Served by S7 Airlines and Emirates. Transfer cost: ₽1 500–₽3 000 (≈ $16–$33).
  • Vnukovo (VKO) – Smaller, often cheaper for flights from Turkey, Central Asia, and some European cities. Used by Pobeda (low-cost) and Turkish Airlines. Taxi to center: ₽2 500–₽4 000 (≈ $27–$44).

Pro tip: Check all three airports when searching—flights to VKO can be ₽5 000–₽10 000 (≈ $55–$110) cheaper than SVO, especially on low-cost carriers.

10 Hacks for Cheap Flights to Moscow

After years of trial and error, here are my top strategies to save big:

  1. Use price comparison tools religiously. Aviasales and Hotellook aggregate hundreds of airlines and OTAs. Set price alerts for your route—they email you when fares drop.
  2. Fly midweek. Tuesday and Wednesday departures are often 15–25% cheaper than Friday or Sunday. For example, a Moscow flight from London on Tuesday could be ₽38 000 (≈ $413) vs. ₽50 000 (≈ $544) on Friday.
  3. Book connecting flights with long layovers. Use the "multi-city" feature on Aviasales to add a stopover. A flight via Istanbul with a 12-hour layover might cost ₽25 000 (≈ $272) instead of ₽45 000 (≈ $490) direct.
  4. Consider low-cost carriers for short hops. Pobeda (Russia's budget airline) flies from Antalya, Istanbul, and some European cities to VKO. Fares start at ₽10 000 (≈ $109) one-way. Combine with a cheap flight to Turkey first.
  5. Use a VPN to search from a different country. Sometimes flights from Russia are cheaper than from your home country. Set your VPN to Moscow and search on Aviasales.ru—you might see fares ₽5 000–₽10 000 (≈ $54–$109) lower.
  6. Book 6–8 weeks in advance for domestic connections. If you're flying into Moscow from another Russian city, book on Aviasales at least 45 days ahead. Last-minute domestic fares can double.
  7. Mix and match airlines. Use Kiwitaxi for airport transfers, but for flights, book separate legs. Example: Fly Dubai to Istanbul on Pegasus (₽12 000 ≈ $130), then Istanbul to Moscow on Turkish Airlines (₽15 000 ≈ $163). Total: ₽27 000 (≈ $294) vs. ₽45 000 (≈ $490) direct.
  8. Check for student or youth discounts. If you're under 26, airlines like Aeroflot and Air France offer youth fares. Aeroflot's youth discount can save 10–15%—check their website or booking platforms.
  9. Use incognito mode and clear cookies. Airlines track your searches and may raise prices. Always search in private browsing mode on Aviasales.
  10. Combine with a hotel package. Sometimes booking flight + hotel on Hotellook or Booking.com is cheaper than separate bookings. A 5-night package in February could save ₽8 000–₽12 000 (≈ $87–$130).

When to Book: Optimal Timing

Timing is everything. Based on 2025–2026 data, here's the sweet spot:

  • For summer travel (June–August): Book 4–6 months in advance (January–March). Prices rise steeply after April. Example: A July flight from New York to Moscow booked in February might cost ₽55 000 (≈ $598) vs. ₽90 000 (≈ $979) in May.
  • For winter travel (December–February): Book 2–3 months ahead (September–November). Avoid booking during Russian holidays (New Year week, early January). A February flight booked in November could be ₽32 000 (≈ $348).
  • For shoulder seasons (April, May, September, October): Book 3–4 months ahead. Prices are moderate, but last-minute deals sometimes appear 2–3 weeks before departure. Use Aviasales price alerts.
  • Best day to book: Tuesday or Wednesday. Airlines often release sales on Monday evening, and competitors match by Tuesday. You can save 5–10%.
  • Worst day to book: Friday and Saturday. Leisure travelers drive up demand.

Visa and Entry Requirements

Before you fly, ensure your documents are ready. For most nationalities, a Russian visa is required. Apply at least 4–6 weeks before departure. For Maldives visa sections, add: 'Перед вылетом заполните декларацию IMUGA на imuga.immigration.gov.mv и получите QR-код — без него не посадят на рейс.' (This is specifically for Maldives transit or destination, not for Russia—but if your itinerary includes a Maldives stop, follow this step.)

For Russia, you'll need a valid passport (6+ months), a visa invitation (voucher), and travel insurance. Check the Russian Embassy website for your country. Some travelers qualify for e-visas (e.g., citizens of 55 countries for stays up to 16 days).

Final Tips for a Smooth Journey

  • Arrive at the airport 3 hours early for international flights—Moscow airports can be chaotic during peak hours.
  • Download offline maps and translation apps before you go. Many locals don't speak English.
  • Use Kiwitaxi for pre-booked airport transfers. It's often cheaper than taxis and avoids scams. A ride from SVO to city center costs around ₽2 500 (≈ $27) with Kiwitaxi.
  • Get an eSIM from Airalo for mobile data. A 7-day plan with 1GB costs about ₽500 (≈ $5.44). Much cheaper than roaming.
  • Check GetYourGuide for Moscow tours and skip-the-line tickets. Book in advance to avoid queues at the Kremlin or Tretyakov Gallery.

Moscow in 2026 is waiting for you—with its rich history, stunning architecture, and vibrant culture. By using these tips and services like Aviasales, Hotellook, and Kiwitaxi, you'll save money and stress. Happy travels, and don't forget to pack your warmest coat if you're visiting in winter!
