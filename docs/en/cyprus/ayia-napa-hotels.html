Top 10 Hotels in Ayia Napa 2026: Budget to Luxury

Ayia Napa has transformed from a quiet fishing village into Cyprus’s most dynamic resort destination, and by 2026, its hotel scene is more diverse than ever. Whether you’re dreaming of sprawling beachfront resorts, charming boutique stays, or wallet-friendly hostels, this guide covers ten handpicked properties across every price range.

I’ve personally explored these neighborhoods—from the buzzing bars of the main strip to the serene coves near Cape Greco—and I’ll break down what each area really feels like, so you can pick the perfect base for your trip.

Hotel infrastructure here is well-developed: most properties offer pools, on-site dining, and easy access to Nissi Beach or the town center. The main tourist area clusters around the harbor and Ayia Napa Square, while quieter neighborhoods like Pernera and Kapparis offer a more relaxed vibe.

Prices vary dramatically by season—expect peak summer rates (June–August) to be 40–60% higher than in May or September. For a quick overview, hotel prices in Ayia Napa can be ₽8 000–₽12 000 (≈ $85–130) per night in mid-range during high season, but you’ll find great deals if you book early or travel in shoulder months.

Budget Hotels (under $50/night)

1. Adams Beach Hotel (Budget Wing)

The budget wing of this large resort offers simple, clean rooms with shared facilities. It’s a 10-minute walk from Nissi Beach and has a basic pool. The main hotel’s restaurants and bars are accessible at extra cost.

Price: ₽3 500–₽4 500 (≈ $37–48) per night

  • Pros: Unbeatable location near the best beach; access to resort amenities; friendly staff.
  • Cons: Rooms are dated; no private bathroom; can be noisy in summer.

2. Tofinis Hotel

A family-run gem in the town center, Tofinis offers basic but spotless rooms with air conditioning and private bathrooms. The rooftop terrace has partial sea views, and the bar serves cheap local wine.

Price: ₽2 800–₽4 000 (≈ $30–43) per night

  • Pros: Central location near shops and clubs; very affordable; includes breakfast.
  • Cons: Thin walls; no pool; limited parking.

3. Nissi Beach Hostel

Perfect for solo travelers and backpackers, this hostel offers dorm beds and private rooms just 200 meters from Nissi Beach. It has a communal kitchen, free Wi-Fi, and a lively common area.

Price: ₽1 400–₽2 800 (≈ $15–30) per night for dorms; private rooms from ₽3 200 (≈ $34)

  • Pros: Social atmosphere; cheap beer; walking distance to beach and clubs.
  • Cons: Basic facilities; noise from parties; shared bathrooms.

Mid-Range Hotels ($50-150/night)

4. Nissi Beach Resort

This iconic resort sits directly on Nissi Beach, with a sprawling pool complex, three restaurants, and a spa. Rooms are modern with balconies overlooking the Mediterranean. The beach bar is a hotspot for afternoon cocktails.

Price: ₽7 000–₽11 000 (≈ $75–118) per night

  • Highlights: Private beach access; kids’ club; live music evenings.

5. Grecian Park Hotel

Perched on a cliff near Cape Greco, this hotel offers panoramic sea views and a peaceful setting. It has a large outdoor pool, tennis courts, and a spa. The rooms are elegantly furnished with marble bathrooms.

Price: ₽6 500–₽10 000 (≈ $70–107) per night

  • Highlights: Stunning sunset views; quiet location; free shuttle to town.

6. Pavlo Napa Beach Hotel

A stylish boutique hotel in Pernera, just a short walk from the sandy beach. Rooms are contemporary with bright colors and smart TVs. The rooftop bar serves creative cocktails and has a small plunge pool.

Price: ₽5 500–₽9 000 (≈ $59–96) per night

  • Highlights: Trendy design; adults-only section; excellent breakfast buffet.

7. Anastasia Hotel

Located near the center of Ayia Napa, this hotel offers a mix of standard and suite rooms. It has a large pool with a swim-up bar, a gym, and a spa. The staff organize daily activities like yoga and water polo.

Price: ₽6 000–₽10 500 (≈ $64–112) per night

  • Highlights: Central location; family-friendly; all-inclusive option available.

Luxury Hotels ($150+/night)

8. Alion Beach Hotel

This five-star resort in Ayia Thekla offers a private sandy beach, two infinity pools, and a world-class spa. Suites have sea views and private balconies with jacuzzis. The fine-dining restaurant serves Mediterranean cuisine with a twist.

Price: ₽14 000–₽23 000 (≈ $150–247) per night

  • Features: Butler service; beachfront cabanas; Michelin-starred chef residency in summer.

9. Napa Mermaid Design Hotel & Spa

A sleek adults-only hotel near the harbor, known for its minimalist design and infinity pool overlooking the sea. Rooms have floor-to-ceiling windows and rainfall showers. The spa offers hammam and hot stone therapies.

Price: ₽16 000–₽28 000 (≈ $172–301) per night

  • Features: Rooftop bar with panoramic views; free yacht excursions; personalized concierge.

10. Amanti Hotel

Boutique luxury meets bohemian chic at this hillside hotel. Each suite is uniquely decorated with local art and antiques. The property has a saltwater pool, a private cinema, and a farm-to-table restaurant.

Price: ₽18 000–₽30 000 (≈ $194–323) per night

  • Features: Private plunge pools in select suites; cooking classes; complimentary champagne on arrival.

How to Choose the Right Area

Your ideal neighborhood depends on what you want from your trip. Here’s a quick breakdown:

  • For beach lovers: Stay near Nissi Beach or Ayia Thekla. Nissi Beach Resort and Alion Beach Hotel give you direct sand access. Prices here are higher, but you’ll avoid the town crowds.
  • For sightseeing: The town center or harbor area is best. Hotels like Anastasia Hotel or Tofinis Hotel put you close to the Ayia Napa Monastery, Thalassa Museum, and boat tours to Cape Greco. You’ll walk everywhere.
  • For nightlife: The main strip around Ayia Napa Square is where the clubs and bars are. Budget options like Nissi Beach Hostel are ideal—you can stumble back after 4 a.m. Be prepared for noise until dawn.

Airport Transfers

Larnaca International Airport (LCA) is the main gateway, about 45–60 minutes from Ayia Napa by car. You have several options to reach your hotel:

  • Taxi: Fixed rates from the airport cost around ₽4 500–₽6 000 (≈ $48–64) one way. Book at the counter in arrivals.
  • Bus: The Kapnos Airport Shuttle runs every 30 minutes from Larnaca to Ayia Napa central station. A ticket is about ₽700 (≈ $7.50). From there, take a local bus or walk to your hotel.
  • Rental car: Agencies like Sixt and Hertz have desks at the airport. Daily rates start at ₽2 800 (≈ $30). Driving is easy on the A3 highway.
  • Private transfer: For comfort and reliability, a convenient transfer can be ₽3 500–₽5 000 (≈ $37–54) via Kiwitaxi, with a driver meeting you at arrivals and taking you directly to your hotel door.

Booking Tips

To get the best value for your Ayia Napa stay, follow these tips:

  • Book early: For summer 2026, reserve by March to secure lower rates. Hotels like Adams Beach and Pavlo Napa often offer early-bird discounts of 20–30%.
  • Compare platforms: Use Aviasales for flights and Hotellook or Booking for hotels. For mid-range options, Agoda sometimes has exclusive deals. You can ₽8 000–₽12 000 (≈ $85–130) per night in mid-range to compare prices across these sites quickly.
  • Consider all-inclusive: Resorts like Anastasia Hotel offer meal packages that save money if you plan to eat and drink on-site. Compare with half-board options.
  • Travel in shoulder season: May and September have warm weather (25–30°C) and lower prices. Luxury hotels like Amanti can drop to ₽12 000 (≈ $129) per night.
  • Use local agents: Cypriot travel agencies sometimes have unpublished rates. Check GetYourGuide for tours that include hotel pickups.
  • Check cancellation policies: Flexible rates cost 10–15% more but let you change dates if plans shift. Most hotels on Booking offer free cancellation up to 48 hours before.
  • Book airport transfers in advance: Kiwitaxi and local taxi apps let you pre-book for a fixed price, avoiding surge pricing. For eSIM data, Airalo offers Cyprus packages from ₽350 (≈ $3.75) for 1 GB.

Remember, prices are estimates based on typical market rates for 2026—always double-check with the provider before booking. Happy travels!
