15 Best Things to Do in Ayia Napa 2026

Welcome to Ayia Napa — a sun-drenched Mediterranean gem that’s so much more than its party reputation. I’ve spent countless hours exploring this Cypriot paradise, and I’m thrilled to share the absolute best things to do here in 2026.

From pristine beaches and ancient ruins to thrilling water parks and serene nature trails, Ayia Napa offers a rich tapestry of experiences for every type of traveler. Whether you’re a solo backpacker, a couple seeking romance, or a family craving adventure, this guide will help you craft an unforgettable trip. Let’s dive in!

15 Best Attractions in Ayia Napa

1. Nissi Beach

Nissi Beach is the crown jewel of Ayia Napa’s coastline — a stunning stretch of golden sand lapped by crystal-clear turquoise waters. It’s famous for its shallow lagoon, which makes it perfect for swimming and paddleboarding. In 2026, the beach remains a lively hub with beach bars, water sports, and sunbed rentals. Arrive early to snag a prime spot, as it gets packed by midday.

  • Ticket Price: Free entry; sunbeds from ₽1 500 (≈ $16) per set
  • Hours: Always open; water sports operate 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM
  • How to Get There: 15-minute walk from Ayia Napa center; bus 101 from the square
  • Tip: Visit on a weekday for fewer crowds and better chances of spotting sea turtles offshore.

2. Cape Greco National Forest Park

This protected nature reserve at the southeastern tip of Cyprus is a haven for hikers and photographers. The park features dramatic sea cliffs, hidden sea caves, and the iconic “Love Bridge” — a natural rock arch. In spring 2026, wildflowers carpet the trails, adding bursts of color. Don’t miss the panoramic views from the Kamara Tou Koraka viewpoint.

  • Ticket Price: Free entry
  • Hours: Open 24/7; visitor center 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM (April–October)
  • How to Get There: 10-minute drive from Ayia Napa; bus 102 stops at the park entrance
  • Tip: Bring sturdy shoes and a flashlight to explore the sea caves at low tide.

3. Ayia Napa Monastery

Nestled in the heart of the town, this 16th-century Venetian monastery is a peaceful oasis amid the buzz. Its limestone walls and central courtyard, shaded by a sprawling sycamore fig tree, offer a glimpse into Cyprus’s religious history. The monastery now houses a small museum with icons and artifacts. It’s a must-visit for history buffs and a serene spot for reflection.

  • Ticket Price: Free entry
  • Hours: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM (closed Sundays)
  • How to Get There: Central location, a 5-minute walk from the main square
  • Tip: Combine your visit with a stroll through the adjacent Farmers’ Market on Saturdays.

4. WaterWorld Themed Waterpark

One of Europe’s largest waterparks, WaterWorld is a splash-tastic family favorite with over 30 slides and attractions. The Ancient Greek theme — complete with a replica Trojan Horse — adds a unique twist. Highlights include the “Poseidon’s Wave” pool and the adrenaline-pumping “Drop to Atlantis” slide. In 2026, new shaded lounging areas have been added for comfort.

  • Ticket Price: ₽5 500 (≈ $60) for adults; ₽3 500 (≈ $38) for children (online discount available)
  • Hours: 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM (May–October)
  • How to Get There: 5-minute drive from Ayia Napa; free shuttle from major hotels
  • Tip: Book tickets online via GetYourGuide to skip queues and save 15%.

5. Ayia Napa Sculpture Park

This open-air gallery along the coastal path features over 20 contemporary sculptures by Cypriot and international artists. The pieces blend seamlessly with the Mediterranean landscape, creating a thought-provoking stroll. It’s especially magical at sunset when the light casts dramatic shadows. A perfect free activity for art lovers and Instagrammers.

  • Ticket Price: Free
  • Hours: Always open
  • How to Get There: Along the coastal path from Ayia Napa harbor; 10-minute walk
  • Tip: Visit early morning for the best light and fewer people.

6. Thalassa Municipal Museum

This modern museum dives deep into the marine life and maritime history of the region. Exhibits include a full-scale replica of a 4th-century BC Greek ship, interactive touchscreens, and a stunning aquarium with local fish species. It’s educational and engaging for all ages. The rooftop café offers lovely sea views.

  • Ticket Price: ₽700 (≈ $7.50) for adults; ₽350 (≈ $3.75) for children
  • Hours: 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM (Tuesday–Sunday)
  • How to Get There: On the main road to Nissi Beach; bus 101 stops nearby
  • Tip: Check for free guided tours in English on Wednesdays.

7. Konnos Beach

A quieter alternative to Nissi, Konnos Beach is a sheltered cove with soft sand and calm, shallow waters. It’s ideal for families with young children or couples seeking tranquility. The surrounding pine-clad hills make it a scenic spot for a picnic. In 2026, a new eco-friendly beach bar serves local wines and meze.

  • Ticket Price: Free; sunbeds from ₽1 200 (≈ $13)
  • Hours: Always open; lifeguards on duty 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM
  • How to Get There: 10-minute drive from Ayia Napa; bus 102 stops 500m away
  • Tip: Rent a kayak from the beach shack to explore the nearby sea caves.

8. Ayia Napa Harbour

The bustling harbor is the heart of Ayia Napa’s nightlife and fishing culture. By day, you can watch colorful boats bob in the marina, grab fresh seafood at tavernas, or join a glass-bottom boat tour. By night, it transforms into a vibrant strip of bars and clubs. Don’t miss the sunset views from the lighthouse.

  • Ticket Price: Free
  • Hours: Always open; restaurants 11:00 AM – midnight
  • How to Get There: 10-minute walk from the center; follow the signs to “Harbour”
  • Tip: Book a sunset sailing tour via GetYourGuide for around ₽4 500 (≈ $49).

9. Love Bridge (Konnos)

This natural rock formation near Konnos Beach is a beloved photo spot and a symbol of romance. Legend says couples who kiss under the arch will stay together forever. The clear waters below are perfect for snorkeling, with colorful fish darting among the rocks. Arrive early to avoid the selfie crowds.

  • Ticket Price: Free
  • Hours: Always open
  • How to Get There: Walk 15 minutes from Konnos Beach along the coastal path
  • Tip: Wear water shoes — the rocks can be slippery and sharp.

10. Ayia Napa Caves (Sea Caves)

A short drive from Cape Greco, these dramatic sea caves are carved by centuries of waves. You can explore them by foot, kayak, or boat. The largest cave has a natural skylight that creates a stunning light show inside. It’s a favorite spot for cliff jumping (at your own risk).

  • Ticket Price: Free
  • Hours: Always open
  • How to Get There: 10-minute drive from Ayia Napa; bus 102 to “Sea Caves” stop
  • Tip: Bring a flashlight and go at low tide for easier access.

11. Protaras Ocean Aquarium

While technically in nearby Protaras, this aquarium is a 15-minute drive and well worth it. It features over 1,000 marine creatures, including sharks, rays, and sea turtles. The highlight is the walk-through tunnel where you’re surrounded by swimming fish. In 2026, a new penguin exhibit has opened.

  • Ticket Price: ₽2 500 (≈ $27) for adults; ₽1 500 (≈ $16) for children
  • Hours: 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM (daily)
  • How to Get There: Bus 101 from Ayia Napa to Protaras; 15-minute walk
  • Tip: Buy tickets online via Booking.com attractions to save 10%.

12. Makronissos Beach

This long, sandy beach is a favorite for windsurfing and kiteboarding thanks to its consistent winds. It’s less crowded than Nissi, with a laid-back vibe and a few beachfront tavernas. The nearby Makronissos Tombs — ancient rock-cut graves — add a historical twist to your beach day.

  • Ticket Price: Free; sunbeds from ₽1 000 (≈ $11)
  • Hours: Always open
  • How to Get There: 10-minute drive from Ayia Napa; bus 102 stops nearby
  • Tip: Rent windsurfing gear from the beach shack for around ₽3 000 (≈ $33) per hour.

13. Dinosaur Beach

A quirky, family-friendly attraction, Dinosaur Beach features life-sized dinosaur models set against a beach backdrop. Kids love the interactive exhibits and fossil-digging area. It’s a bit touristy but great for a fun afternoon. In 2026, a new zip line has been added over the beach.

  • Ticket Price: ₽1 500 (≈ $16) for adults; ₽1 000 (≈ $11) for children
  • Hours: 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM (April–October)
  • How to Get There: 5-minute drive from Ayia Napa; free parking
  • Tip: Combine with a visit to nearby Nissi Beach for a full day out.

14. Ayia Napa Farmers’ Market

Held every Saturday near the monastery, this market is a feast for the senses. You’ll find fresh local produce, halloumi cheese, olive oil, honey, and handmade crafts. It’s a great place to pick up souvenirs and sample Cypriot delicacies. The atmosphere is lively with live music and food stalls.

  • Ticket Price: Free entry
  • Hours: 8:00 AM – 2:00 PM (Saturdays only)
  • How to Get There: Central location, next to the monastery
  • Tip: Bring cash — many vendors don’t accept cards.

15. Parko Paliatso Luna Park

This amusement park in the heart of Ayia Napa offers rides for all ages, from gentle carousels to thrilling roller coasters. It’s especially popular with families and teens. In 2026, a new virtual reality ride has been added. The park is open late, making it a great evening activity.

  • Ticket Price: Free entry; ride tokens from ₽300 (≈ $3.25) each
  • Hours: 6:00 PM – midnight (April–October)
  • How to Get There: On Nissi Avenue, a 10-minute walk from the center
  • Tip: Buy a wristband for ₽2 000 (≈ $22) for unlimited rides during off-peak hours.

1-Day, 2-Day, and 3-Day Itineraries

1-Day Itinerary: Best of Ayia Napa

Start your day at Nissi Beach for a morning swim (9:00 AM – 11:00 AM). Head to the Ayia Napa Monastery for a cultural break (11:30 AM – 12:30 PM). Lunch at a harbor taverna (12:30 PM – 1:30 PM). Afternoon at Cape Greco for hiking and sea caves (2:00 PM – 5:00 PM). End with sunset at Love Bridge (5:30 PM – 6:30 PM). Dinner and drinks at the harbor (7:00 PM onward).

2-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Follow the 1-day itinerary above. Day 2: Morning at WaterWorld Waterpark (10:00 AM – 3:00 PM). Afternoon at Ayia Napa Sculpture Park and Thalassa Museum (3:30 PM – 5:30 PM). Evening at Parko Paliatso Luna Park (6:00 PM – 9:00 PM).

3-Day Itinerary

Day 1 & 2: As above. Day 3: Morning at Konnos Beach and Love Bridge (9:00 AM – 12:00 PM). Afternoon at Protaras Ocean Aquarium and Makronissos Beach (1:00 PM – 5:00 PM). Evening at the Farmers’ Market (if Saturday) or a sunset boat tour (6:00 PM – 8:00 PM).
