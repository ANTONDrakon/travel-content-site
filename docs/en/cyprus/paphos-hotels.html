Budget Hotels (under $50/night)

Traveling on a shoestring? These budget-friendly hotels offer clean, comfortable rooms without breaking the bank. They're perfect for backpackers, solo travelers, or anyone who'd rather spend their money on experiences than accommodation.

1. Pyramos Hotel

Located in the heart of Kato Paphos, Pyramos Hotel is a charming, family-run property just a 10-minute walk from the harbor and archaeological park. Rooms are simple but spotless, with air conditioning, private bathrooms, and small balconies. The rooftop terrace offers lovely sea views, and there's a small pool for cooling off. Prices start at ₽3 200 (≈ $35) per night in low season, rising to ₽4 600 (≈ $50) in peak summer.

  • Pros: Great location, friendly staff, free Wi-Fi, budget-friendly.
  • Cons: No restaurant, basic amenities, can be noisy from street traffic.

2. Axiothea Hotel

A short stroll from the Paphos Castle and waterfront, Axiothea Hotel offers excellent value for money. The rooms are a bit dated but well-maintained, with en-suite bathrooms and minifridges. A continental breakfast is included, and the staff goes out of their way to help with local tips. Nightly rates range from ₽2 800 (≈ $30) in winter to ₽4 200 (≈ $45) in summer.

  • Pros: Central location, free breakfast, helpful staff, quiet at night.
  • Cons: No pool, limited parking, older furniture.

3. Kiniras Hotel & Apts

This no-frills hotel in the residential area of Kato Paphos is ideal for longer stays, offering both hotel rooms and self-catering apartments. It's a 15-minute walk to the beach and 20 minutes to the harbor. The property has a small pool, a bar, and a restaurant serving Cypriot dishes. Prices start at ₽3 700 (≈ $40) per night for a room, or ₽4 600 (≈ $50) for an apartment with a kitchenette.

  • Pros: Kitchenette options, pool, good for families, free parking.
  • Cons: Farther from attractions, basic decor, limited breakfast.

Mid-Range Hotels ($50-150/night)

Mid-range hotels in Paphos offer a fantastic balance of comfort, amenities, and location. You'll find well-appointed rooms, pools, on-site dining, and often sea views—perfect for couples, families, or anyone wanting a bit more without the luxury price tag.

4. Leonardo Cypria Bay

Set on the beachfront in Kato Paphos, Leonardo Cypria Bay is a popular choice for its prime location and excellent facilities. Rooms are modern and spacious, with balconies overlooking the Mediterranean. The hotel features two outdoor pools, a spa, and several restaurants serving international and Cypriot cuisine. Nightly rates range from ₽6 500 (≈ $70) in low season to ₽11 000 (≈ $120) in peak summer.

  • Highlights: Direct beach access, large pools, kids' club, great for families.

5. Amavi Hotel

This adults-only hotel in the heart of Kato Paphos is a sleek, modern retreat with a focus on relaxation. Rooms are minimalist yet luxurious, with rainfall showers and smart TVs. The rooftop pool and bar offer stunning sunset views, and the spa is a highlight. Prices start at ₽7 400 (≈ $80) per night, climbing to ₽12 000 (≈ $130) in high season.

  • Highlights: Adults-only, rooftop infinity pool, excellent spa, romantic vibe.

6. Constantinou Bros Athena Beach Hotel

Located in the quieter area of Kato Paphos, this beachfront hotel offers a relaxed atmosphere with plenty of activities. Rooms are bright and airy, with private balconies. The property has multiple pools, a tennis court, and a kids' club. Nightly rates range from ₽5 500 (≈ $60) in winter to ₽10 000 (≈ $110) in summer.

  • Highlights: Beachfront location, good for families, all-inclusive option, friendly service.

7. Elysium Hotel

While technically a luxury property, Elysium Hotel occasionally offers mid-range rates in low season, making it a steal for those who book early. This five-star resort in Kato Paphos is set in lush gardens with direct beach access. Rooms are opulent, with marble bathrooms and sea views. The hotel boasts a huge pool complex, a spa, and several restaurants. Expect to pay ₽11 000 (≈ $120) per night in shoulder season, rising to ₽18 500 (≈ $200) in peak.

  • Highlights: World-class service, stunning grounds, great for couples, spa facilities.

Luxury Hotels ($150+/night)

For those seeking indulgence, Paphos has a range of luxury hotels that deliver unforgettable experiences. Think private beaches, Michelin-star dining, and impeccable service. These properties are perfect for honeymoons, anniversaries, or simply treating yourself.

8. Annabelle Hotel

A legendary property in Kato Paphos, Annabelle Hotel is a five-star resort with a timeless elegance. Rooms are spacious and beautifully appointed, with many offering direct sea views. The hotel features a stunning lagoon-style pool, a thalassotherapy spa, and multiple dining options including a Greek taverna. Nightly rates start at ₽14 000 (≈ $150) in low season and go up to ₽22 000 (≈ $240) in summer.

  • Features: Lagoon pool, spa, beachfront location, award-winning gardens, excellent dining.

9. Almyra Hotel

Part of the Thanos Hotels group, Almyra is a chic, modern resort with a focus on design and wellness. The minimalist rooms feature floor-to-ceiling windows, and the property has a private beach, a large pool, and a kids' club. The spa is top-notch, and the restaurant "Oceans" serves fresh seafood with sea views. Prices range from ₽16 500 (≈ $180) to ₽25 000 (≈ $270) per night.

  • Features: Modern design, private beach, kids' club, spa, beachfront dining.

10. The Ivi Mare – Designed for Adults

This adults-only luxury hotel in Kato Paphos is all about sophistication and relaxation. Rooms are contemporary and spacious, with private terraces and hydrotherapy pools. The infinity pool overlooks the sea, and the spa offers a range of treatments. The on-site restaurant serves gourmet Mediterranean cuisine. Nightly rates start at ₽18 000 (≈ $195) and can exceed ₽28 000 (≈ $300) in peak season.

  • Features: Adults-only, infinity pool, hydrotherapy pools, gourmet dining, serene atmosphere.

How to Choose the Right Area

Paphos is a compact city, but its neighborhoods have distinct personalities. Here's how to pick the best area based on your travel style:

For Beach Lovers

Head to Coral Bay, about 10 km north of the city center. This area has the best sandy beaches in Paphos, with calm waters and plenty of sunbeds. Hotels here are mostly mid-range to luxury, with easy access to water sports and beach bars. Coral Bay is quieter than Kato Paphos, perfect for families or couples seeking relaxation.

For Sightseeing Enthusiasts

Stay in Kato Paphos (lower town), near the harbor and archaeological park. You'll be walking distance to the Paphos Castle, the Tombs of the Kings, and the Paphos Mosaics. This area is bustling with restaurants, shops, and nightlife, but can be noisy. Budget and mid-range hotels dominate here, with a few luxury options.

For Nightlife and Dining

The Kato Paphos harbor area is the epicenter of nightlife, with bars, clubs, and tavernas open late. If you want to be in the action, choose a hotel near the harbor or along the main strip. For a quieter evening, stay in the residential areas just a 10-minute walk away—you'll still have easy access to the party scene.

Airport Transfers

Paphos International Airport (PFO) is just 15 km from the city center, making transfers quick and easy. The cheapest option is the public bus (route 612), which runs every 30 minutes and costs ₽200 (≈ $2) per person. Taxis are widely available, with fixed fares of around ₽2 800 (≈ $30) to Kato Paphos.
