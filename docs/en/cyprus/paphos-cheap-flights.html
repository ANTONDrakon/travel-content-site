Planning a trip to Paphos in 2026? You’re in for a treat—this sun-drenched Cypriot gem offers ancient ruins, turquoise waters, and a laid-back vibe that’s hard to beat. But let’s be real: the flight cost can make or break your budget. I’ve been there, scrolling through endless options, wondering if I’ll ever snag a deal.

Good news—I’ve cracked the code, and I’m sharing everything I’ve learned to help you find cheap flights to Paphos International Airport (PFO) in 2026. Whether you’re flying from Europe, the Middle East, or beyond, I’ve got you covered with practical tips, price estimates, and a few insider hacks. Let’s dive in!

Paphos Airport (PFO) is the main gateway to western Cyprus, handling both direct and connecting flights from a range of airlines. It’s a compact, easy-to-navigate airport, so you’ll breeze through arrivals. In this guide, I’ll break down direct and connecting options, monthly price trends, booking strategies, and even flight insurance tips. Direct Flights to Paphos

Direct flights are the holy grail for convenience—no layovers, no stress. For 2026, several airlines operate direct routes to PFO, primarily from European hubs. Here’s a rundown of the main carriers, departure cities, and estimated prices (in RUB and local currency or USD). Remember, these are average estimates; actual fares vary based on demand and booking time.

  • Ryanair: Budget-friendly direct flights from London Stansted (STN), Manchester (MAN), and Dublin (DUB). Estimated one-way fare: ₽8 500 (≈ $90) from London, ₽9 500 (≈ $100) from Manchester, ₽10 500 (≈ $110) from Dublin. Book early for the best deals.
  • Wizz Air: Direct from London Luton (LTN), Budapest (BUD), and Warsaw (WAW). Prices from ₽7 500 (≈ $80) for Luton, ₽9 000 (≈ $95) for Budapest, ₽8 000 (≈ $85) for Warsaw. Great for Eastern European connections.
  • easyJet: Direct flights from London Gatwick (LGW), Bristol (BRS), and Milan (MXP). Estimated one-way: ₽9 000 (≈ $95) from Gatwick, ₽10 000 (≈ $105) from Bristol, ₽11 000 (≈ $115) from Milan. Check Aviasales for live comparisons.
  • Jet2.com: Seasonal direct flights from Manchester, Leeds Bradford (LBA), and Birmingham (BHX). Prices from ₽12 000 (≈ $130) one-way. Ideal for summer 2026 travel.
  • TUI Airways: Charter flights from UK cities like London, Manchester, and Glasgow (GLA). One-way estimates: ₽14 000 (≈ $150). Often bundled with package holidays.
  • Cyprus Airways: Direct from Athens (ATH) and Tel Aviv (TLV). Fares: ₽10 000 (≈ $105) from Athens, ₽12 000 (≈ $130) from Tel Aviv. A reliable local carrier.

For direct flights, I recommend checking Aviasales—it aggregates these airlines and shows the cheapest dates. Also, use Hotellook for package deals if you’re booking accommodation too. Direct flights are pricier than connecting ones, but the time saved is often worth it.

Connecting Flights

If you’re flexible and want to save big, connecting flights are your best bet. They’re usually cheaper, and you can explore a new city en route. For 2026, the cheapest options often involve hubs like Istanbul (IST), Athens (ATH), or Dubai (DXB). Here’s what to expect:

  • Via Istanbul (IST or SAW): Turkish Airlines and Pegasus offer frequent connections. Example: from Moscow (SVO) to PFO via Istanbul (IST) with Turkish Airlines—estimated round-trip: ₽25 000 (≈ $270). Duration: 8-12 hours total. Pegasus via Sabiha Gökçen (SAW) can be even cheaper, from ₽20 000 (≈ $215).
  • Via Athens (ATH): Aegean Airlines and Ryanair connect through Athens. From London to PFO via ATH: ₽15 000 (≈ $160) one-way, total travel time 6-8 hours. Great for a quick Athens stopover.
  • Via Dubai (DXB): Emirates offers luxury connections from Asia, Australia, and the US. From Bangkok (BKK) to PFO via DXB: ₽35 000 (≈ $375) one-way, duration 12-16 hours. A splurge, but comfort is top-notch.
  • Via other European hubs: Consider Vienna (VIE), Zurich (ZRH), or Frankfurt (FRA) with carriers like Austrian, Swiss, or Lufthansa. Prices from ₽18 000 (≈ $195) one-way, with layovers of 2-5 hours.

10 Hacks for Cheap Flights

I’ve learned these hacks through trial and error—and they’ve saved me thousands over the years. Here are 10 specific tips to score cheap flights to Paphos in 2026:

  1. Use Incognito Mode — Airlines track your searches and may hike prices. Open a private browser window (Chrome Incognito or Firefox Private) when checking Aviasales or Booking.com. I’ve seen prices drop by ₽3 000 (≈ $32) instantly.
  2. Book Connecting Flights Separately — Sometimes booking two one-way tickets (e.g., Moscow to Istanbul on Pegasus, then Istanbul to Paphos on Turkish Airlines) is cheaper than a single booking. Use Aviasales to compare. Just leave at least 3 hours for layovers.
  3. Fly from Alternative Airports — If you’re near London, check Stansted (STN) vs. Gatwick (LGW). Ryanair from STN is often ₽1 500 (≈ $16) cheaper. For Moscow, consider VKO vs. SVO—Pegasus flies from VKO to Paphos via SAW for ₽22 000 (≈ $240) round-trip.
  4. Use Price Alerts — Set alerts on Aviasales for your route. I’ve gotten notifications for ₽5 000 (≈ $54) drops. Act fast—these deals vanish in hours.
  5. Join Airline Newsletters — Ryanair, Wizz Air, and easyJet often send flash sales. For example, Ryanair’s “$11 (€10) sale” can get you a one-way for ₽1 000 (≈ $11). Sign up on their sites.
  6. Book on Tuesdays or Wednesdays — Data shows these are the cheapest days to buy. I’ve booked on a Tuesday for ₽18 000 (≈ $195) vs. ₽22 000 (≈ $240) on Saturday.
  7. Consider Stopover Packages — Turkish Airlines offers free Istanbul stopovers for connecting flights. Book via Aviasales and add a night in Istanbul for ₽3 000 (≈ $32) extra—worth it for a mini-vacation.
  8. Use Airalo for eSIM Data — Stay connected without roaming fees. Airalo offers Cyprus eSIMs from ₽500 (≈ $5.40) for 1GB. Perfect for checking flight updates or booking last-minute deals.
  9. Check GetYourGuide for Package Deals — Some tours include airport transfers or discounts. I’ve seen a Paphos archaeological tour with transfer for ₽4 000 (≈ $43) when booked with a flight.
  10. Use Kiwitaxi for Fixed Transfers — Airport taxis can be unpredictable. Kiwitaxi offers fixed prices from PFO to Paphos city center—₽1 500 (≈ $16) per ride. Book in advance to avoid surge pricing.

These hacks work best when combined. For instance, I once used incognito mode, set a price alert, and booked on a Tuesday—saved ₽12 000 (≈ $130) on a round-trip from London.

When to Book

Timing is everything when it comes to cheap flights. For Paphos 2026, optimal booking windows vary by season. Here’s my advice based on experience and data:

  • For summer (June-August): Book 3-4 months in advance. Prices start climbing in March. I booked my July flight in February for ₽35 000 (≈ $375)—by April, it was ₽45 000 (≈ $485). Use Aviasales’ 6-month view.
  • For spring (March-May) and fall (September-October): Book 2-3 months ahead. These are shoulder seasons, so deals pop up. I snagged an April flight for ₽25 000 (≈ $270) by booking in February.
  • For winter (November-February): Book 1-2 months in advance. Demand is low, so last-minute deals are common. In January, I found a round-trip for ₽18 000 (≈ $195) just two weeks before.
  • General rule: Avoid booking on weekends or during major holidays (Christmas, Easter). Use Booking.com’s flexible date search to compare prices across a month.

Check Aviasales for historical price trends—they show if you’re getting a good deal. Remember, these are estimates; actual prices depend on airline sales and demand.
