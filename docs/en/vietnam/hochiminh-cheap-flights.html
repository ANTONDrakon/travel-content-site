How to Find Cheap Flights to Ho Chi Minh City 2026

Planning a trip to Ho Chi Minh City (SGN) in 2026? You’re in for a vibrant mix of history, street food, and bustling energy. But let’s be honest—getting there affordably is half the battle. I’ve been through the maze of flight deals myself, and I’m here to share practical tips to help you snag a bargain. Whether you’re flying from Moscow, Bangkok, or New York, this guide covers direct and connecting flights, monthly price trends, booking hacks, and more. Let’s dive in!

Direct Flights

Direct flights to Tan Son Nhat International Airport (SGN) are the most convenient, but they come at a premium. In 2026, several airlines operate non-stop routes from key hubs. Here’s a snapshot:

  • Vietnam Airlines – From Moscow (SVO) and Bangkok (BKK). Moscow routes average ₽45 000–₽70 000 (≈ $490–$760), while Bangkok flights are cheaper at ₽10 000–₽18 000 (≈ $110–$195).
  • Bamboo Airways – From Seoul (ICN) and Tokyo (NRT). Prices range ₽25 000–₽40 000 (≈ $270–$435).
  • Aeroflot – From Moscow (SVO), with fares around ₽50 000–₽75 000 (≈ $540–$815).
  • Singapore Airlines – From Singapore (SIN), a short hop at ₽8 000–₽15 000 (≈ $87–$163).
  • Emirates – From Dubai (DXB), with prices ₽30 000–₽50 000 (≈ $326–$543).

Direct flights from Europe or the US are rare—most connect through hubs like Doha or Istanbul. Check Aviasales for the latest deals on these routes.

Connecting Flights

For budget travelers, connecting flights are the way to go. The cheapest options often involve a layover in Southeast Asian hubs. Here’s what to expect:

  • Via Bangkok (BKK) – Airlines like Thai Lion Air and AirAsia offer fares from ₽15 000–₽25 000 (≈ $163–$272) for the whole journey. Total duration: 8–12 hours including layover.
  • Via Kuala Lumpur (KUL) – AirAsia and Malaysia Airlines connect here, with prices ₽12 000–₽20 000 (≈ $130–$217). Expect 10–14 hours total.
  • Via Singapore (SIN) – Scoot and Jetstar Asia have deals from ₽10 000–₽18 000 (≈ $109–$196). Layovers can be 2–6 hours.
  • Via Doha (DOH) – Qatar Airways offers longer connections (12–18 hours) from ₽35 000–₽50 000 (≈ $380–$543).
  • Via Istanbul (IST) – Turkish Airlines from Europe, prices ₽40 000–₽60 000 (≈ $435–$652). Duration: 14–20 hours.

Flights can be compared across dates using Aviasales—their calendar view highlights the cheapest days. For example, flying on a Tuesday or Wednesday often saves 20–30% versus weekends.

10 Hacks for Cheap Flights

Here are my go-to strategies for finding affordable flights to Ho Chi Minh City in 2026:

  1. Use Incognito Mode – Search flights in private browser tabs to avoid price hikes based on cookies.
  2. Set Price Alerts – On Aviasales, enable alerts for SGN. You’ll get email notifications when prices drop.
  3. Fly Mid-Week – Tuesdays and Wednesdays are often 20–30% cheaper than Fridays or Sundays.
  4. Book Connecting Flights Separately – Sometimes booking two one-way tickets (e.g., Bangkok to Ho Chi Minh City on AirAsia) is cheaper than a single booking. Use Aviasales to compare.
  5. Consider Budget Airlines – AirAsia, VietJet Air, and Scoot offer low fares. Check Aviasales for their schedules.
  6. Book 6–8 Weeks Ahead – For Ho Chi Minh City, booking 45–60 days before departure typically yields the best deals.
  7. Use Flexible Dates – On Aviasales, search “whole month” to see the cheapest days.
  8. Check Nearby Airports – If you’re in Southeast Asia, consider flying to Da Nang (DAD) or Hanoi (HAN) and taking a train or bus to Ho Chi Minh City. Trains cost ₽1 000–₽2 500 (≈ $11–$27).
  9. Leverage Stopover Programs – Airlines like Singapore Airlines offer free stopovers in Singapore. Book via Aviasales and explore two cities for one price.
  10. Use Travel Credit Cards – Earn miles on bookings via Aviasales or Hotellook. Many cards offer sign-up bonuses worth a free flight.

When to Book

Timing is everything. For Ho Chi Minh City in 2026, here’s my advice:

  • Optimal window: Book 6–8 weeks before departure for domestic or regional flights (e.g., from Bangkok). For long-haul (e.g., from Moscow or New York), book 3–4 months ahead.
  • Best months to fly: September and May are the cheapest (see table above). Avoid December and February during Tet (Vietnamese New Year) when prices spike.
  • Day of the week: Tuesday at 3 PM (local time) often has lowest fares, as airlines release weekly sales then.
  • Use calendar tools: On Aviasales, the “price calendar” shows green (cheap) and red (expensive) days. I’ve saved ₽15 000 (≈ $163) by shifting my trip by just two days.

Flight Insurance

Delays and cancellations can happen, especially during monsoon season (May–October). Here’s how to protect yourself:

  • Travel insurance: I recommend Aviasales for flight booking, but for comprehensive coverage, consider insurance providers that offer trip cancellation and delay benefits. Policies typically cost ₽1 500–₽5 000 (≈ $16–$54) per trip.
  • Know your rights: Under EU Regulation 261/2004, if your flight from Europe is delayed over 3 hours or cancelled, you may be entitled to compensation of ₽25 000–₽60 000 (≈ $272–$652). Check via Aviasales or your airline.
  • Use flight tracking apps: Apps like FlightRadar24 alert you to changes. For refunds, contact your airline or booking platform—Aviasales has a support team for issues.
  • Pro tip: If your flight is cancelled, ask for rebooking on partner airlines. For example, if Vietnam Airlines cancels, they might put you on Bamboo Airways at no extra cost.

For comprehensive coverage, you can also check Aviasales for insurance add-ons during booking. Remember, compensation via insurance providers can cover expenses like meals and hotels during delays.

I hope this guide helps you find an affordable flight to Ho Chi Minh City in 2026. With a bit of planning and these hacks, you’ll have more money left for pho and bánh mì! Safe travels!
