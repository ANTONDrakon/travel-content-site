How to Find Cheap Flights to Da Nang 2026

Updated: July 2026 · Expert guide · ⏱ 5 min read

If you're dreaming of golden beaches, ancient temples, and the vibrant food scene of central Vietnam, Da Nang should be high on your list. I've been exploring this coastal gem for years, and one thing I know for sure: getting there doesn't have to break the bank. With a bit of planning and the right tools, you can snag a flight to Da Nang International Airport (DAD) for a fraction of what you'd expect. Let me walk you through everything you need to know for 2026—from direct routes to sneaky savings hacks.

Da Nang's airport (DAD) is well-connected, with airlines like Vietnam Airlines, VietJet Air, Bamboo Airways, and budget carriers like AirAsia and Scoot serving the route. Whether you're flying from Moscow, Bangkok, or London, there's a way to make it affordable.

Direct Flights

Direct flights to Da Nang are a game-changer if you value time and convenience. In 2026, several airlines operate non-stop services, especially from Asian hubs. Here's a breakdown of what to expect:

Airlines and Departure Cities

  • Vietnam Airlines – Direct from Moscow (SVO) to Da Nang: ₽45 000–₽70 000 (≈ $490–$760). Also from Hanoi, Ho Chi Minh City, and Seoul.
  • VietJet Air – Budget direct from Bangkok (BKK), Kuala Lumpur (KUL), and Singapore (SIN): ₽8 000–₽15 000 (≈ $87–$163).
  • Bamboo Airways – Direct from Taipei (TPE) and Tokyo (NRT): ₽25 000–₽40 000 (≈ $270–$435).
  • Scoot – Direct from Singapore (SIN): ₽10 000–₽18 000 (≈ $109–$196).
  • AirAsia – Direct from Kuala Lumpur (KUL) and Bangkok (DMK): ₽7 000–₽14 000 (≈ $76–$152).

These prices are estimates based on typical 2026 market rates—always check current deals on Aviasales or Hotellook for real-time comparisons.

Connecting Flights

If direct flights are out of budget or not available from your city, connecting flights are your best friend. They're often significantly cheaper, especially if you're flying from Europe, the US, or Australia. Here's what I've found works best:

Cheapest Options and Key Transit Hubs

  • Via Bangkok (BKK) – Flights from Moscow, London, or New York to Bangkok (₽25 000–₽40 000 / ≈ $270–$435), then connect to Da Nang with AirAsia or VietJet (₽5 000–₽10 000 / ≈ $54–$109). Total: ₽30 000–₽50 000 (≈ $326–$543). Duration: 12–16 hours.
  • Via Kuala Lumpur (KUL) – Similar pricing, with AirAsia or Malaysia Airlines. Total: ₽28 000–₽45 000 (≈ $304–$489). Duration: 14–18 hours.
  • Via Singapore (SIN) – Scoot or Jetstar Asia. Total: ₽30 000–₽48 000 (≈ $326–$522). Duration: 13–17 hours.
  • Via Hanoi (HAN) or Ho Chi Minh City (SGN) – Great if you want to explore another Vietnamese city. Domestic flights are cheap: ₽3 000–₽6 000 (≈ $33–$65). Total from Europe: ₽35 000–₽55 000 (≈ $380–$598). Duration: 15–20 hours.

Flights can be compared across dates using Aviasales—just set a flexible calendar to see the cheapest days. For example, flying on a Tuesday or Wednesday often saves 20–30%.

10 Hacks for Cheap Flights

Over the years, I've picked up a few tricks that save hundreds of dollars. Here are my top 10 hacks for finding cheap flights to Da Nang in 2026:

  1. Use Incognito Mode – Airlines and booking sites track your searches. Open a private browser window to avoid price hikes. I always do this on Aviasales.
  2. Set Price Alerts – On Aviasales, you can set alerts for your route. You'll get an email when prices drop. I've saved ₽10 000+ this way.
  3. Fly Midweek – Tuesdays and Wednesdays are cheapest. For example, a Moscow–Da Nang flight on a Tuesday can be ₽5 000–₽8 000 (≈ $54–$87) cheaper than on a Friday.
  4. Book Connecting Flights Separately – Sometimes buying two one-way tickets (e.g., Moscow to Bangkok on Aviasales, then Bangkok to Da Nang on AirAsia) is cheaper than a single itinerary. Just leave 3–4 hours between connections.
  5. Check Budget Airlines Directly – For short hops, go to AirAsia or VietJet's own site. But for long-haul, use Aviasales to compare all options.
  6. Use Stopover Programs – Some airlines offer free stopovers. For instance, fly with Vietnam Airlines via Hanoi and get a free night in a hotel. Check on Hotellook for accommodation deals.
  7. Book 6–8 Weeks Ahead – For Da Nang, the sweet spot is 45–60 days before departure. Last-minute deals are rare for this route.
  8. Consider Nearby Airports – Fly into Hue (HUI) or Chu Lai (VCL) if they're cheaper. Then take a bus or train to Da Nang (1–2 hours, ₽500–₽1 000 / ≈ $5–$11). Use Kiwitaxi for transfers.
  9. Use a VPN – Sometimes flights from a different country are cheaper. Set your VPN to Thailand or Malaysia and compare prices on Aviasales.
  10. Bundle with Hotels – On Hotellook, you can find flight+hotel packages that save 10–15%. For example, a week in Da Nang with a 4-star hotel might cost ₽50 000–₽70 000 (≈ $543–$761) total.

When to Book

Timing is everything. Based on my analysis of 2026 trends, here's the optimal booking window:

  • For peak season (December–February, July–August): Book 3–4 months ahead. Prices rise 20–30% closer to departure.
  • For shoulder season (March–April, October–November): Book 2–3 months ahead. You'll find good deals.
  • For low season (May–June, September): Book 1–2 months ahead. Last-minute sales sometimes appear, but don't risk it.

I recommend starting your search on Aviasales at least 6 months before your trip. Use their calendar view to spot the cheapest dates. And remember: Tuesday afternoons (your local time) often have the lowest prices due to airline pricing algorithms.

Final Tips for Your Trip

Once you've booked your flight, consider these extras:

  • Visa for Vietnam – Most nationalities need a visa. Apply online via the official e-visa portal (₽1 500–₽2 500 / ≈ $16–$27). Check requirements on Aviasales travel guides.
  • SIM Card or eSIM – Stay connected with Airalo. A 7-day data plan costs ₽500–₽1 000 (≈ $5–$11).
  • Airport Transfers – Book a private car on Kiwitaxi from Da Nang Airport to your hotel (₽1 200–₽2 500 / ≈ $13–$27).
  • Activities – Explore Marble Mountains, Hoi An, and Ba Na Hills. Book tours on GetYourGuide for ₽1 500–₽4 000 (≈ $16–$43) per activity.

Da Nang is a magical place—I still remember my first sunset at My Khe Beach. With these tips, you'll have more money to spend on delicious bánh mì and fresh seafood. Happy travels, and see you in Vietnam!
