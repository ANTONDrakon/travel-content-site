Top 10 Hotels in Phu Quoc 2026: Budget to Luxury

Phu Quoc, Vietnam's largest island, has transformed from a quiet fishing outpost into one of Southeast Asia's top beach destinations. With its white-sand beaches, emerald waters, and lush national park, it offers something for every traveler—whether you're a backpacker on a tight budget or a honeymooner seeking five-star indulgence.

The island's hotel scene has exploded in recent years, with everything from rustic bungalows to world-class resorts lining the coast. In this guide, I'll walk you through ten of the best hotels across three price tiers, share insights on neighborhoods, and give you practical tips for booking in 2026.

Phu Quoc is divided into several distinct areas. The west coast, especially around Duong Dong town and Long Beach, is the most developed, with restaurants, night markets, and easy access to the airport. The north coast, near Bai Dai (Long Beach) and the new Grand World complex, is quieter and home to many luxury resorts.

The south, around An Thoi, offers a more local vibe and is close to the cable car to Hon Thom Island. Prices vary widely—a basic guesthouse can cost as little as ₽4 500 (≈ $50) per night, while a villa at a top-tier resort can reach ₽45 000 (≈ $500) or more. Budget Hotels (under $50/night)

1. Sea Bungalow Phu Quoc

Located on the southern end of Long Beach, this family-run property offers simple but clean bungalows surrounded by tropical gardens. It's just a two-minute walk to the beach, and the owners are incredibly warm—they'll help you rent a motorbike or arrange snorkeling trips. Rooms come with air conditioning, a private bathroom, and a small terrace. The on-site restaurant serves affordable Vietnamese dishes like pho and fresh seafood.

Price: ₽4 000–₽5 500 (≈ $45–$60) per night

Pros: Friendly staff, great value, close to beach. Cons: Basic amenities, no pool, limited English spoken.

2. Tamarind Bungalow

Set in a quiet area near Duong Dong, Tamarind Bungalow is a collection of rustic wooden huts with thatched roofs. It's a bit older but well-maintained, and the garden is lush with fruit trees. The beach is a short walk away, and you're close to the night market for cheap seafood dinners. Rooms are basic—think mosquito nets and cold-water showers—but the vibe is genuinely local.

Price: ₽3 200–₽4 500 (≈ $35–$50) per night

Pros: Authentic experience, cheap, good location. Cons: No hot water, basic facilities, can be noisy at times.

3. Phu Quoc Backpacker Hostel

If you're traveling solo or on a shoestring, this hostel in Duong Dong offers dorm beds and private rooms. It's social, with a common area, free breakfast, and organized tours. The private rooms are simple but clean, with air conditioning and a shared bathroom. It's a five-minute walk to the beach and the night market.

Price: Dorms from ₽900 (≈ $10); private rooms from ₽2 700 (≈ $30) per night

Pros: Social atmosphere, cheap, organized tours. Cons: Shared bathrooms, not for couples or families.

Mid-Range Hotels ($50-150/night)

4. The Ocean Bay Phu Quoc

This boutique hotel on Long Beach offers a perfect balance of comfort and value. Rooms are modern with ocean views, a private balcony, and a rain shower. The infinity pool overlooks the sea, and the restaurant serves excellent breakfast buffets. It's within walking distance of restaurants and the Duong Dong market, making it ideal for independent travelers.

Price: ₽6 800–₽10 000 (≈ $75–$110) per night

Highlights: Great pool, beachfront location, good breakfast.

5. Mango Bay Phu Quoc

An eco-friendly resort on the northern coast, Mango Bay focuses on sustainability without sacrificing comfort. The bungalows are made from natural materials, and the property has a private beach, a spa, and a yoga pavilion. It's a bit remote, which adds to the peaceful atmosphere, but you'll need a scooter or taxi to explore. The restaurant uses local ingredients and hosts cooking classes.

Price: ₽7 200–₽11 000 (≈ $80–$120) per night

Highlights: Eco-friendly, private beach, cooking classes.

6. Chen Sea Resort & Spa

Located on the west coast near Duong Dong, Chen Sea offers spacious villas with private pools and garden views. The resort has a large lagoon-style pool, a spa with traditional treatments, and a restaurant serving both Vietnamese and international dishes. It's a bit further from the main tourist strip, but free shuttle buses run to the night market. The service is attentive and professional.

Price: ₽9 000–₽13 500 (≈ $100–$150) per night

Highlights: Private pool villas, spa, free shuttle.

7. Amarin Resort Phu Quoc

A modern resort on Long Beach, Amarin features clean, contemporary rooms with sea views and a rooftop pool. The location is excellent—right next to restaurants, bars, and the beach. The staff are helpful with tour bookings and motorbike rentals. It's a popular choice for couples and small families who want a comfortable base without breaking the bank.

Price: ₽6 300–₽9 500 (≈ $70–$105) per night

Highlights: Rooftop pool, central location, modern rooms.

Luxury Hotels ($150+/night)

8. JW Marriott Phu Quoc Emerald Bay

Arguably the most iconic hotel on the island, this JW Marriott property on the southern tip is a masterpiece of design. Set on a private beach, it features whimsical architecture inspired by a fictional university, with each building representing a different faculty. Rooms are opulent with marble bathrooms and ocean views. The resort has multiple pools, a world-class spa, and several restaurants, including the famous Pink Pearl. It's a destination in itself.

Price: ₽18 000–₽40 000 (≈ $200–$450) per night

Features: Private beach, multiple pools, fine dining, spa.

9. InterContinental Phu Quoc Long Beach Resort

Located on the west coast, this five-star resort offers a blend of luxury and family-friendly amenities. Rooms are spacious with balconies overlooking the sea or gardens. The resort has a large infinity pool, a kids' club, and a spa with a hammam. The dining options range from casual seafood to Michelin-starred Japanese at Sora. It's a short taxi ride to Duong Dong town.

Price: ₽13 500–₽27 000 (≈ $150–$300) per night

Features: Infinity pool, kids' club, multiple restaurants.

10. Salinda Resort Phu Quoc

A boutique luxury resort on the northern coast, Salinda is known for its intimate atmosphere and personalized service. The villas have private pools and outdoor showers, set within a lush tropical garden. The resort has a stunning beachfront infinity pool, a spa, and a restaurant serving farm-to-table cuisine. It's ideal for couples seeking a romantic escape away from the crowds.

Price: ₽16 000–₽32 000 (≈ $180–$350) per night

Features: Private pool villas, romantic setting, farm-to-table dining.

How to Choose the Right Area

The best area for you depends on your travel style. Here's a quick breakdown:

  • For beach lovers: Long Beach (west coast) offers the longest stretch of sand and easy access to resorts like The Ocean Bay and Amarin. The north coast, especially near Bai Dai, has quieter beaches and luxury resorts like Salinda.
  • For sightseeing and activities: Duong Dong town is the hub for tours, night markets, and restaurants. Stay here if you want to explore the island's national park, fish sauce factories, and pearl farms. Budget hotels like Tamarind Bungalow are convenient.
  • For nightlife: The area around Duong Dong's night market and the Grand World complex in the north has bars, live music, and late-night street food. Mid-range hotels near the market offer easy access to the action.
  • For total relaxation: The southern tip, near An Thoi and the JW Marriott, is secluded and perfect for a resort-focused stay. You'll need taxis to go anywhere else, but the peace is worth it.

Airport Transfers

Phu Quoc International Airport (PQC) is located in the south of the island, about 10-15 minutes from Duong Dong and 30-40 minutes from the northern resorts. Taxis are available outside the arrivals hall, but prices can vary—expect to pay ₽900–₽1 800 (≈ $10–$20) depending on your hotel's location.

For a hassle-free experience, you can book a private transfer in advance. A convenient transfer can be заказать трансфер в Фукуоке на Kiwitaxi, where you'll be met at the gate with a sign and driven directly to your hotel. Prices start at around ₽1 400 (≈ $15) for a standard car.

Alternatively, many hotels offer free or paid airport shuttles—check when booking. If you're staying in Duong Dong, you can also take a local bus (₽270, ≈ $3) that runs from the airport to the town center, though it's not as comfortable.

Booking Tips

To get the best deals on Phu Quoc hotels in 2026, follow these tips:

  • Book early for peak season: November to March is the dry season, and prices can double. Reserve at least 3-4 months in advance, especially for luxury resorts.
  • Use comparison sites: You can сравнить цены на отели Фукуока на Hotellook to see rates across Booking, Agoda, and other platforms. Agoda often has the best deals for Asia, but it's worth checking multiple sites.
  • Consider package deals: Some booking sites offer flight+hotel bundles through Aviasales or similar services, which can save money on both.
  • Check for hidden fees: Some resorts charge extra for breakfast, resort fees, or airport transfers. Read the fine print before confirming.
  • Book with free cancellation: Phu Quoc's weather can be unpredictable, and plans change. Choose flexible rates when possible.
  • Use loyalty points: If you're a member of hotel loyalty programs or credit card rewards, check if you can redeem points for free nights at properties like the InterContinental or JW Marriott.
