Da Nang, Vietnam’s coastal gem, has exploded in popularity over the past few years, and for good reason. With its stunning My Khe Beach, vibrant food scene, and easy access to UNESCO sites like Hoi An and the Marble Mountains, it’s a destination that offers something for every type of traveler. The hotel scene here is incredibly diverse, from sleek high-rises overlooking the East Sea to charming boutique stays tucked away in the city’s quieter alleys. In 2026, the city continues to refine its hospitality, with new properties opening and established ones upping their game. Whether you’re a backpacker on a shoestring or a couple seeking a splurge-worthy escape, Da Nang’s neighborhoods—from the bustling My An district to the central Hai Chau area—offer distinct vibes and price points.

When it comes to costs, Da Nang remains a fantastic value compared to other Asian beach destinations. A basic but clean room can start as low as ₽1 800 (≈ $20) per night, while a luxury suite with ocean views might set you back ₽14 000 (≈ $150) or more. The sweet spot for most travelers is the mid-range, where you’ll find excellent service, pools, and breakfast buffets for around ₽5 500–₽9 000 (≈ $60–$100). Naturally, hotel prices in Da Nang can be

Budget Hotels (under $50/night)

If you’re traveling on a tight budget or just want to save your dong for street food and excursions, these three hotels deliver comfort without breaking the bank. Expect clean rooms, friendly staff, and often a rooftop pool or breakfast included.

1. The Memory Hostel & Hotel

Located in the heart of the My An Beach area, The Memory Hostel & Hotel is a hybrid property that offers both private rooms and dorm beds. The vibe is young and social, with a common area where backpackers swap stories over free coffee. Private rooms are simple but spotless, with air conditioning and a small desk. The highlight? A small rooftop pool that offers a refreshing dip after a day at the beach, which is just a three-minute walk away.

Prices: Private rooms from ₽2 300 (≈ $25) per night; dorm beds from ₽550 (≈ $6).

  • Pros: Great location near the beach, friendly staff, free breakfast (basic but filling).
  • Cons: Can be noisy at night due to the social atmosphere; limited privacy in dorms.

This family-run hotel sits on a quiet street off Vo Nguyen Giap, the main beach road. It’s a no-frills option that focuses on cleanliness and comfort. 3. Danang Backpackers Hostel

A classic for solo travelers, this hostel in the My Khe area offers a lively atmosphere with organized pub crawls and family dinners. Private rooms are basic but come with en-suite bathrooms and air conditioning. The dorm beds have privacy curtains and personal reading lights, a nice touch for the price. The hostel also rents out motorbikes and arranges trips to Ba Na Hills.

Prices: Private rooms from ₽2 300 (≈ $25) per night; dorm beds from ₽460 (≈ $5).

  • Pros: Social vibe, great for meeting people, free walking tours.
  • Cons: Not suitable for couples seeking romance; dorms can get hot despite AC.

Mid-Range Hotels ($50-150/night)

The mid-range category is where Da Nang truly shines. You’ll get excellent value—think beachfront pools, stylish decor, and attentive service—for a fraction of what you’d pay in Thailand or Bali. These four hotels are my top picks for 2026.

4. Brilliant Hotel Da Nang

Overlooking the Han River, Brilliant Hotel is a modern property with a touch of elegance. The rooms are spacious, with floor-to-ceiling windows offering views of the Dragon Bridge or the river. The rooftop infinity pool is a showstopper, especially at sunset when the city lights begin to twinkle. Breakfast is a lavish buffet with both Vietnamese and Western options, including made-to-order pho and fresh tropical fruit.

Prices: Standard rooms from ₽5 500 (≈ $60) per night.

  • Highlights: Rooftop pool with river views, central location near the Dragon Bridge, excellent breakfast.

5. Mandila Beach Hotel

Just a two-minute walk from My Khe Beach, Mandila Beach Hotel is a boutique property that feels like a small resort. The rooms are decorated in a coastal-chic style with wooden accents and soft blues. Many rooms have private balconies where you can hear the waves. The hotel also has a small spa offering Vietnamese massages, and the pool is surrounded by lush greenery.

Prices: Deluxe rooms from ₽6 000 (≈ $65) per night.

  • Highlights: Beach proximity, peaceful garden pool, spa services at reasonable prices.

6. Grandvrio City Da Nang

Formerly known as the Grand Mercure, this hotel in the city center is a reliable choice for business and leisure travelers alike. The rooms are large and well-appointed, with separate bathtubs and rain showers. The hotel’s standout feature is its outdoor pool, which is surrounded by sun loungers and a bar. It’s also close to the Cham Museum and the Han Market, making it ideal for sightseeing.

Prices: Superior rooms from ₽6 500 (≈ $70) per night.

  • Highlights: Great location for exploring the city, spacious rooms, excellent value for the amenities.

7. Olalani Resort & Condotel

Located a bit south of the main beach area, Olalani offers a more resort-like experience at mid-range prices. The property has multiple pools, a private beach area, and spacious suites with kitchenettes—perfect for families or longer stays. The on-site restaurant serves excellent Vietnamese seafood, and the staff are known for their warm hospitality.

Prices: One-bedroom suites from ₽8 000 (≈ $85) per night.

  • Highlights: Multiple pools, private beach access, family-friendly suites with kitchenettes.

Luxury Hotels ($150+/night)

For those seeking indulgence, Da Nang’s luxury hotels offer world-class service, stunning architecture, and unforgettable experiences. These three properties are the crème de la crème in 2026.

8. InterContinental Danang Sun Peninsula Resort

Perched on the Son Tra Peninsula, this iconic resort is a destination in itself. Designed by architect Bill Bensley, it features a dramatic tiered layout with rooms and villas cascading down a lush hillside to a private beach. The rooms are opulent, with dark wood, marble bathrooms, and panoramic ocean views. The resort also boasts multiple restaurants, including the Michelin-starred La Maison 1888, and the world-class HARNN Heritage Spa.

Prices: Classic rooms from ₽28 000 (≈ $300) per night; villas from ₽65 000 (≈ $700).

  • Features: Private beach, cable car to the lobby, exceptional dining, infinity pools, butler service.

9. Fusion Suites Da Nang Beach

Fusion Suites is all about wellness and relaxation. Located directly on My Khe Beach, this all-suite hotel offers spacious rooms with ocean views and a unique "all-spa inclusive" concept—meaning you get two complimentary spa treatments per day per room. The rooftop infinity pool is a highlight, and the restaurant focuses on healthy, locally sourced cuisine. It’s a favorite among couples and digital nomads looking for a rejuvenating stay.

Prices: One-bedroom suites from ₽18 000 (≈ $195) per night.

  • Features: Complimentary daily spa treatments, rooftop pool, beachfront location, healthy dining options.

10. Pullman Da Nang Beach Resort

The Pullman offers a perfect blend of modern luxury and beachside relaxation. The resort features sleek, contemporary rooms with floor-to-ceiling windows, multiple swimming pools (including a lagoon-style pool), and a long stretch of private beach. The on-site dining includes both international buffets and a Vietnamese specialty restaurant. The Pullman is also known for its excellent kids’ club, making it a top choice for families.

Prices: Deluxe ocean-view rooms from ₽16 000 (≈ $175) per night.

  • Features: Multiple pools, kids’ club, private beach, fitness center, and a lively bar scene.

How to Choose the Right Area

Da Nang is a spread-out city, and your choice of neighborhood can make or break your trip. Here’s a quick guide based on your travel style:

For Beach Lovers

Stay in the My An / My Khe Beach area (east of the city center). This is where you’ll find the best access to the famous sandy shores, beachfront cafes, and seafood restaurants. Budget hotels like The Memory Hostel and mid-range options like Mandila Beach Hotel are concentrated here. You’ll be close to the water but a bit farther from the main city attractions.

For Sightseeing & Culture

Choose the Hai Chau district (city center) or the Son Tra Peninsula. Hai Chau is home to the Dragon Bridge, Han Market, the Cham Museum, and the Han River waterfront. Hotels like Grandvrio City Da Nang put you in the middle of the action. The Son Tra Peninsula, where the InterContinental sits, is quieter but offers access to the Linh Ung Pagoda and monkey spotting.

For Nightlife & Dining

Head to the An Thuong area (a sub-neighborhood of My An). This is a grid of streets packed with bars, international restaurants, and live music venues. It’s a bit noisier but incredibly vibrant. Many budget and mid-range hotels are within walking distance of the nightlife here.
