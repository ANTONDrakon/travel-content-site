Top 10 Hotels in Nha Trang 2026: Budget to Luxury

Nha Trang, Vietnam’s stunning coastal gem, has evolved into a world-class destination with a hotel scene as diverse as its turquoise bays and golden beaches. From the lively, backpacker-friendly streets of the city center to the serene, resort-lined shores of the northern beaches, the city offers accommodation for every traveler. Whether you’re craving a cheap dorm bed for ₽1 800 (≈ $20) or a lavish overwater villa for ₽45 000 (≈ $500), Nha Trang delivers.

The infrastructure is excellent: most hotels are within walking distance of the beach, and the main drag, Tran Phu Street, is packed with restaurants, bars, and spas. Prices here are generally lower than in Da Nang or Phu Quoc, making it a fantastic value destination. You can expect to find hotel prices in Nha Trang ranging from ₽2 700 (≈ $30) for a basic guesthouse to ₽90 000 (≈ $1000) for a five-star suite.

Budget Hotels (under $50/night)

1. Mojzo Inn Boutique

This stylish hostel-hotel hybrid is a favorite among solo travelers and young couples. Located just a block from the beach, Mojzo Inn offers clean, air-conditioned dorm beds and private rooms with a modern, minimalist design. The rooftop bar is a social hub, and free breakfast (simple but satisfying) is included. Prices: Dorm bed from ₽900 (≈ $10); private room from ₽2 700 (≈ $30). Pros: Excellent social vibe, great location, free Wi-Fi. Cons: Dorms can get noisy at night, no pool.

2. Nha Trang Backpackers Hostel

A true backpacker institution, this hostel is perfect for budget travelers who want to meet people. It’s slightly further from the beach (a 10-minute walk) but close to the night market and bars. Rooms are basic but clean, and the staff organize daily pub crawls and tours. Prices: Dorm bed from ₽720 (≈ $8); private room from ₽1 800 (≈ $20). Pros: Extremely cheap, fun atmosphere, tour desk. Cons: Basic amenities, no pool, can be loud.

3. Hotel Yen Quang

A no-frills, family-run hotel in the city center, ideal for travelers on a strict budget. Rooms are small but spotless, with air conditioning and hot water. The staff are incredibly friendly and helpful. It’s a 5-minute walk to the beach and surrounded by local eateries. Prices: Private room from ₽1 350 (≈ $15). Pros: Great value, very clean, excellent location. Cons: No breakfast, no pool, thin walls.

Mid-Range Hotels ($50-150/night)

4. Liberty Central Nha Trang

A sleek, modern hotel on Tran Phu Street with direct beach access. The rooftop infinity pool offers stunning ocean views, and the rooms are spacious with floor-to-ceiling windows. The breakfast buffet is extensive, with both Western and Vietnamese options. Prices: ₽9 000–18 000 (≈ $100–200). Highlights: Rooftop pool, beachfront location, excellent breakfast.

5. Rosaka Boutique Hotel

This boutique property is a hidden gem, blending French colonial charm with Vietnamese hospitality. Rooms are individually decorated with vintage furniture, and the small plunge pool is a relaxing escape. It’s located in a quiet alley near the city center, a 7-minute walk from the beach. Prices: ₽6 300–13 500 (≈ $70–150). Highlights: Unique design, peaceful atmosphere, personalized service.

6. Novotel Nha Trang

A reliable, family-friendly hotel with a large outdoor pool and a kids’ club. Rooms are comfortable and modern, and the hotel is right on the beach. The on-site restaurant serves good international food, and the spa offers affordable treatments. Prices: ₽9 000–16 200 (≈ $100–180). Highlights: Great for families, large pool, direct beach access.

7. Queen Ann Nha Trang

This hotel offers excellent value for money with its spacious suites and panoramic sea views. The rooftop pool is a highlight, and the location is central, close to restaurants and bars. The staff are known for their warm hospitality. Prices: ₽7 200–13 500 (≈ $80–150). Highlights: Large rooms, rooftop pool, central location.

Luxury Hotels ($150+/night)

8. Six Senses Ninh Van Bay

An exclusive resort accessible only by boat, offering overwater and hillside villas with private pools. This is the ultimate romantic escape, with world-class dining, a stunning spa, and pristine nature. Prices: ₽45 000–135 000 (≈ $500–1,500). Features: Overwater villas, private butler service, eco-friendly design.

9. Amiana Resort Nha Trang

A luxurious beachfront resort with a massive infinity pool, a private beach, and a top-notch spa. The rooms are elegant and spacious, and the resort offers a free shuttle to the city center. It’s perfect for couples and families seeking tranquility. Prices: ₽22 500–54 000 (≈ $250–600). Features: Large pool, private beach, excellent spa.

10. Mia Resort Nha Trang

This boutique resort on the northern beach offers a serene, adults-only vibe (with a family section). The villas have private pools and outdoor showers, and the restaurant serves fantastic Vietnamese cuisine. The staff go above and beyond to make your stay special. Prices: ₽27 000–63 000 (≈ $300–700). Features: Private pools, adults-only area, exceptional service.

How to Choose the Right Area

Nha Trang’s neighborhoods offer distinct experiences. For beach lovers, the central Tran Phu area is ideal — it’s packed with hotels, bars, and restaurants, and you’re steps from the sand. For sightseeing, the northern beaches (like Bai Dai) are quieter and closer to attractions like the Po Nagar Cham Towers and Hon Chong Promontory.

For nightlife, stay near the city center (around Nguyen Thien Thuat Street) where bars and clubs stay open late. If you want a mix, the southern end of Tran Phu offers a quieter beach vibe with easy access to the city.

Airport Transfers

Cam Ranh International Airport (CXR) is about 35 km south of Nha Trang city center. The easiest way to get to your hotel is by taxi or private transfer. A standard taxi costs around ₽1 350–2 250 (≈ $15–25), but you can also book a private car in advance.

A convenient transfer can be booked via Kiwitaxi for ₽1 800 (≈ $20), which includes meet-and-greet service and a comfortable sedan. Alternatively, local buses run from the airport to the city center for ₽270 (≈ $3), but they are less comfortable and drop you at a central bus station, not your hotel.
