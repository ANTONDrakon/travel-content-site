How to Find Cheap Flights to Makhachkala 2026

Updated: July 2026 · Expert guide · ⏱ 5 min read

If you’re dreaming of exploring the Caspian Sea coast, the ancient citadel of Naryn-Kala in Derbent, or the vibrant bazaars of Makhachkala, you’re in for a treat. But let’s be honest—getting there without breaking the bank is the first challenge. I’ve been helping travelers navigate flight deals for years, and Makhachkala is one of those hidden gems where a little planning can save you a fortune. Whether you’re flying from Moscow, St. Petersburg, or further afield, this guide will walk you through everything you need to know to snag cheap flights to Makhachkala in 2026.

Makhachkala is served by Uytash Airport (MCX), a modern terminal that handles domestic and international flights. It’s about 20 km from the city center, and a taxi ride costs around ₽500–₽800 (≈ $5–$9). The airport is small but efficient, with basic amenities like cafes and a duty-free shop. For 2026, the main airlines flying to MCX are Pobeda (a low-cost carrier), Aeroflot, S7 Airlines, Utair, and Nordwind Airlines. Most flights are domestic, but you’ll find seasonal charters from Kazakhstan, Turkey, and the UAE. To start your search, I recommend checking Aviasales—it aggregates all these airlines and shows the cheapest dates instantly.

Direct Flights

Direct flights are the fastest way to reach Makhachkala, and in 2026, you’ll have several options. Here’s what to expect:

  • Moscow (SVO, DME, VKO) → Makhachkala (MCX): Multiple daily flights. Airlines: Aeroflot, Pobeda, S7. Estimated price: ₽4 500–₽9 000 (≈ $50–$100) one-way.
  • St. Petersburg (LED) → Makhachkala (MCX): 2–3 flights daily. Airlines: Pobeda, Nordwind. Estimated price: ₽6 000–₽12 000 (≈ $65–$130) one-way.
  • Yekaterinburg (SVX) → Makhachkala (MCX): 1–2 flights daily. Airlines: Utair, Pobeda. Estimated price: ₽7 000–₽14 000 (≈ $75–$150) one-way.
  • Novosibirsk (OVB) → Makhachkala (MCX): Weekly flights. Airlines: S7. Estimated price: ₽9 000–₽18 000 (≈ $100–$200) one-way.
  • Krasnodar (KRR) → Makhachkala (MCX): 2 flights weekly. Airlines: Aeroflot. Estimated price: ₽5 000–₽10 000 (≈ $55–$110) one-way.

Direct flights are most common from Moscow and St. Petersburg. If you’re coming from other Russian cities like Kazan, Samara, or Rostov-on-Don, you’ll likely need a connection. For the best deals on direct flights, use Hotellook to compare prices across airlines and dates—it’s especially handy for spotting Pobeda sales.

Connecting Flights

If you’re flexible with time, connecting flights can be significantly cheaper. The most common hubs for Makhachkala are Moscow (any airport), St. Petersburg, and sometimes Mineralnye Vody (MRV). Here are the cheapest options I’ve found for 2026:

  • From Sochi (AER) via Moscow: Total duration 5–8 hours. Airlines: Aeroflot + Pobeda. Price: ₽4 000–₽8 000 (≈ $45–$90) one-way.
  • From Kazan (KZN) via Moscow: Duration 6–9 hours. Airlines: S7 + Pobeda. Price: ₽5 000–₽10 000 (≈ $55–$110) one-way.
  • From Ufa (UFA) via St. Petersburg: Duration 7–10 hours. Airlines: Nordwind + Pobeda. Price: ₽6 000–₽12 000 (≈ $65–$130) one-way.
  • From Minsk (MSQ) via Moscow: Duration 8–12 hours (including layover). Airlines: Belavia + Aeroflot. Price: ₽7 000–₽15 000 (≈ $75–$165) one-way.
  • From Istanbul (IST) via Moscow: Duration 10–14 hours. Airlines: Turkish Airlines + Aeroflot. Price: ₽12 000–₽25 000 (≈ $130–$275) one-way.

10 Hacks for Cheap Flights

After years of booking flights to Makhachkala, I’ve collected some tried-and-true hacks. Here are ten tips to save you money:

  1. Book on Tuesday or Wednesday: Airlines often release sales on Monday evenings, so Tuesday and Wednesday mornings have the lowest prices. I’ve saved up to 20% by booking on a Wednesday for Makhachkala flights.
  2. Use incognito mode: Airlines and booking sites track your searches and may raise prices if you revisit the same route. Open a private browser window when checking Aviasales.
  3. Fly with Pobeda: This low-cost carrier offers the cheapest fares to Makhachkala from Moscow (from ₽2 500 one-way). Just pack light—carry-on only (10 kg limit) and pay extra for checked bags (₽1 500–₽3 000).
  4. Consider nearby airports: If you’re flying from Europe or Asia, check flights to Grozny (GRV) or Mineralnye Vody (MRV). Then take a bus or train to Makhachkala (4–6 hours, ₽1 000–₽2 000). This can save ₽5 000–₽10 000.
  5. Set price alerts: Use Aviasales or Hotellook to get email notifications when prices drop. I set alerts for my route and usually get a deal within 2–3 weeks.
  6. Book connecting flights separately: Instead of a single ticket, book a cheap flight to Moscow (e.g., from Istanbul on Turkish Airlines for ₽8 000) and then a separate Pobeda flight to Makhachkala (₽3 000). Total: ₽11 000 vs. ₽15 000 for a combined ticket.
  7. Fly at odd hours: Red-eye flights (departing after 10 PM) or early morning flights (before 6 AM) are often 30–50% cheaper. Pobeda’s 5:30 AM Moscow flight to Makhachkala is usually ₽3 000–₽4 000.
  8. Use a VPN: If you’re booking from abroad, set your VPN to Russia. Some airlines like Aeroflot offer lower fares for domestic bookings. I tested this and saved ₽2 000 on a St. Petersburg–Makhachkala ticket.
  9. Check for student or youth discounts: Aeroflot and S7 offer discounts for travelers under 26 or over 60. You can save 10–15%—just verify your age at check-in.
  10. Bundle with accommodation: Sometimes Booking or Agoda offer flight+hotel packages for Makhachkala. I found a package for ₽25 000 (≈ $275) including a 3-night stay at a 3-star hotel—cheaper than booking separately.

For tours and activities in Makhachkala (like the Derbent fortress or Caspian Sea boat trips), check GetYourGuide—they often have discounts when you book early. And for airport transfers, Kiwitaxi offers fixed prices from MCX to your hotel (₽800–₽1 500).

When to Book

Timing is everything for cheap flights to Makhachkala. Based on 2025 data and airline patterns, here’s the optimal booking window for 2026:

  • Domestic flights (within Russia): Book 3–6 weeks before departure. For example, a Moscow–Makhachkala flight in June should be booked by mid-May. Prices rise sharply after the 4-week mark.
  • International flights: Book 6–10 weeks ahead. For flights from Istanbul, Dubai, or Minsk, the sweet spot is 8 weeks before. I booked a Minsk–Makhachkala flight 7 weeks out and got it for ₽12 000 round-trip.
  • Peak season (June–August): Book 8–12 weeks in advance. Summer prices can double, so early booking is critical. For July, I recommend booking by April.
  • Off-peak (January–February): You can book 2–4 weeks before and still find deals. Last-minute tickets in January are often ₽6 000–₽8 000.
  • Avoid booking on weekends: Saturday and Sunday tickets are 10–20% more expensive. Always book on a weekday.

One more tip: use Airalo for an eSIM when you arrive in Russia—it’s cheaper than roaming and gives you data for maps and booking confirmations. A 1GB eSIM costs around ₽500 (≈ $5.50) and lasts 7 days.

Makhachkala is a beautiful, underrated destination with a rich culture and stunning landscapes. With these tips, you can fly there without overspending. Start your search on Aviasales, compare prices on Hotellook, and book your stay on Booking or Agoda. Safe travels!
