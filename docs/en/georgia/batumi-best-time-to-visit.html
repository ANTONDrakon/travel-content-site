Best Time to Visit Batumi 2026: Seasons & Weather

Planning a trip to Batumi in 2026? You’re in for a treat. This charming Black Sea port city in Georgia offers a unique blend of subtropical beach vibes, lush greenery, and vibrant urban energy. I’ve spent time exploring Batumi’s seasons, and I can tell you that the best time to visit really depends on what you’re after—whether it’s sunbathing, sightseeing, or celebrating local festivals. Let me walk you through the weather, crowds, and prices month by month, so you can choose your perfect window.

Batumi enjoys a humid subtropical climate, with mild winters and warm, humid summers. The city is sheltered by the Caucasus Mountains, which trap moisture and create a lush, green environment year-round. Rainfall is frequent, especially in autumn and winter, but sunny days are plentiful from late spring through early autumn. The Black Sea waters are inviting from June to October, making it a popular beach destination. Here’s a detailed breakdown to help you decide.

High Season

When: June through August, plus the first half of September.

This is Batumi’s busiest period. The weather is hot and sunny, with average highs around 26°C and sea temperatures perfect for swimming (22-26°C). The city comes alive with beach parties, open-air cafes, and a bustling promenade. Prices for accommodation and flights spike significantly—expect to pay around ₽8 000–₽15 000 (≈ $85–$160) per night for a mid-range hotel, and even more for beachfront options.

During high season, it’s wise to book hotels early via Hotellook or Booking.com to secure your spot. I’ve found that booking at least 2-3 months in advance can save you up to 30% compared to last-minute rates. If you’re flexible, consider visiting in late June or early September—crowds are thinner, and prices are slightly lower.

Low Season

When: November through March.

Low season in Batumi is quiet and affordable. The weather is cool and damp, with temperatures ranging from 6°C to 12°C, and frequent rain (up to 160 mm in December). Many beachside cafes and attractions close, but the city still has charm. Accommodation prices drop dramatically—you can find a good hotel for ₽2 500–₽5 000 (≈ $27–$54) per night.

Pros: No crowds, lower prices, and a more authentic local experience. You can explore the botanical garden and old town without jostling for space. Cons: Limited swimming (water temps are 8-11°C), and some outdoor activities may be restricted. If you’re on a tight budget or love cozy, rainy days, this is your time.

Best Time for Beach Vacation

For a classic beach holiday, aim for June to September. The sea is warmest in August (26°C) and July (25°C), with air temperatures around 26°C. June and September are slightly cooler but still comfortable for swimming (22-24°C).

I recommend July and August for sunbathing and water sports, but if you prefer fewer crowds, late June or early September are ideal. The beaches—like Batumi Beach and the quieter Kvariati—are well-maintained, with plenty of sun loungers and umbrellas. Expect to pay around ₽500–₽1 000 (≈ $5–$11) per day for a lounger set. Don’t forget sunscreen—the UV index is high!

Best Time for Sightseeing

The most comfortable months for exploring Batumi’s attractions—like the Alphabet Tower, Piazza Square, and the Botanical Garden—are April, May, September, and October. Temperatures range from 14°C to 22°C, with moderate rainfall and plenty of sunny days.

Spring (April-May) brings blooming flowers and lush greenery, while autumn (September-October) offers mild weather and golden light for photos. Prices are moderate, and you’ll avoid the summer heat. In May, you can enjoy the Batumi Boulevard without sweating, and in October, the autumn foliage in the botanical garden is stunning. For tours and activities, check GetYourGuide for local guides.

Rainy Season

Batumi doesn’t have a distinct rainy season, but precipitation is highest from October to February. December is the wettest month (160 mm), followed by January (150 mm). Rain is often heavy but intermittent—you might get a downpour in the morning and clear skies by afternoon.

Is it worth visiting during this time? Yes, if you enjoy cozy, atmospheric weather and lower prices. The city’s indoor attractions—like the Batumi Art Museum and the Dolphinarium—are still open. Just pack a waterproof jacket and umbrella. For those who don’t mind a bit of drizzle, it’s a peaceful escape.

Events & Festivals

Batumi hosts several vibrant events throughout the year. Here are the major ones by month:

  • May: Batumi International Jazz Festival (mid-May) – world-class musicians perform in the city’s squares and clubs. Free concerts are common.
  • June: Batumi Sea Festival (early June) – celebrates the start of the beach season with parades, live music, and fireworks.
  • July: Black Sea Jazz Festival (mid-July) – a major event with international artists, held at the Batumi Stadium. Tickets cost around ₽2 000–₽5 000 (≈ $22–$54).
  • August: Batumi Summer Festival (all month) – includes art exhibitions, theater performances, and street food fairs.
  • September: Batumi Wine Festival (mid-September) – sample Georgian wines and local cuisine. Entry is free, but tastings cost extra.
  • October: Batumi International Theater Festival (late October) – features performances from Georgian and international troupes.
  • December: New Year’s Eve celebrations – the city lights up with decorations and fireworks. Hotels can be busy, so book early.

Conclusion

So, what’s the best time to visit Batumi in 2026? For a classic beach holiday, go July to August—but expect higher prices and crowds. For sightseeing and comfortable weather, April-May or September-October are perfect. If you’re on a budget and don’t mind rain, November-March offers great deals.

My personal pick is late May to early June or September. You get warm enough weather for swimming (sea temps around 22°C), fewer tourists, and moderate prices. For flights, you can check Aviasales to find the best deals. Typical round-trip flights from Moscow to Batumi in high season cost around ₽25 000–₽40 000 (≈ $270–$430), while low season prices drop to ₽15 000–₽25 000 (≈ $160–$270). For transfers, consider Kiwitaxi for airport pickups. And don’t forget an eSIM from Airalo to stay connected—prices start at ₽500 (≈ $5) for a week of data.

Whichever season you choose, Batumi’s unique blend of nature, culture, and hospitality will leave you with wonderful memories. Safe travels!
