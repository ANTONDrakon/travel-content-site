Planning a trip to Batumi in 2026? You're in for a treat — this Black Sea gem has turquoise beaches, lush mountains, and a charming old town. But getting there on a budget? That's the real challenge, and I'm here to help. As someone who's spent years tracking flight deals and writing destination guides, I can tell you: finding cheap flights to Batumi is all about timing, flexibility, and knowing where to look. In this guide, I'll walk you through everything you need to know — from direct and connecting flights to monthly price trends and insider hacks. Let's dive in!

Batumi is served by Batumi International Airport (BUS), a modern hub about 7 kilometers south of the city center. It's smaller than Tbilisi's airport, but it's growing fast, with new airlines and routes popping up each year. For 2026, expect a mix of direct and connecting flights from major cities in Europe, the Middle East, and beyond. All prices below are approximate estimates based on typical market rates — not exact quotes from specific providers, so always double-check when booking.

Direct Flights

Direct flights to Batumi are limited but growing. As of 2026, the main airlines operating direct routes include:

  • Turkish Airlines — from Istanbul (IST) to Batumi. Flights are daily, with average prices starting at ₽12 000 (≈ $130) one-way. Check Aviasales for the best deals.
  • Pegasus Airlines — from Istanbul (SAW) to Batumi, often cheaper than Turkish Airlines, with one-way fares from ₽9 000 (≈ $97). Use Hotellook to compare prices.
  • Belavia — from Minsk (MSQ) to Batumi, seasonal flights (summer 2026). One-way from ₽15 000 (≈ $162).
  • FlyOne — from Chișinău (RMO) to Batumi, seasonal. One-way from ₽11 000 (≈ $119).
  • Ukraine International Airlines — from Kyiv (KBP) to Batumi, if routes resume by 2026. Estimated one-way from ₽10 000 (≈ $108).

Departure cities for direct flights are limited: Istanbul is the most common hub, with Minsk, Chișinău, and Kyiv offering seasonal options. If you're flying from the US, UK, or Asia, you'll likely need a connecting flight — but don't worry, I've got you covered below.

Connecting Flights

Connecting flights are where the real savings happen. The cheapest options usually involve a layover in Istanbul, Moscow, or Yerevan. Here are the best routes for 2026:

  • Via Istanbul (IST or SAW) — From most European, Asian, or American cities, fly to Istanbul (Turkish Airlines, Pegasus, or low-cost carriers), then connect to Batumi. Total duration: 6-12 hours depending on layover. Prices from ₽20 000 (≈ $216) one-way. Use Aviasales to search for combo deals.
  • Via Moscow (SVO, DME, or VKO) — From cities like London, Berlin, or Dubai, fly to Moscow (Aeroflot, S7, or Pobeda), then to Batumi. Duration: 8-15 hours. Prices from ₽18 000 (≈ $194) one-way. Check Hotellook for price comparisons across dates.
  • Via Yerevan (EVN) — From Middle Eastern or European hubs, fly to Yerevan (Armenia), then take a short 1-hour flight to Batumi (often with FlyOne or Armenia Airways). Duration: 5-8 hours. Prices from ₽15 000 (≈ $162) one-way.
  • Via Tbilisi (TBS) — Fly to Tbilisi, then take a domestic flight or bus to Batumi (3-4 hours by bus). Flights to Tbilisi are cheaper, with prices from ₽25 000 (≈ $270) round-trip from Europe. Add a bus ticket for ₽1 000 (≈ $11).

10 Hacks for Cheap Flights

Here are my tried-and-tested hacks to save money on flights to Batumi in 2026. I've used these myself, and they've saved me hundreds of dollars.

  1. Book 6-8 weeks in advance — For Batumi, the sweet spot is 6-8 weeks before departure. Last-minute deals are rare, and early birds get the best prices. Set alerts on Aviasales or Hotellook.
  2. Use incognito mode — Airlines and booking sites track your searches. Clear cookies or use incognito mode to avoid price hikes. I always do this before checking Aviasales.
  3. Fly on Tuesdays or Wednesdays — Midweek flights are 15-20% cheaper than weekends. For Batumi, Tuesday departures from Istanbul are often the cheapest. Check Agoda for hotel+flight bundles on those days.
  4. Consider nearby airports — Fly to Tbilisi (TBS) or Kutaisi (KUT) and take a bus or train to Batumi. Kutaisi is served by Wizz Air from Europe, with one-way fares from ₽5 000 (≈ $54). Then a bus to Batumi costs ₽1 500 (≈ $16). Use Booking for accommodation in Tbilisi if you need an overnight stop.
  5. Use connecting flights with long layovers — Book a flight with a 12-24 hour layover in Istanbul or Yerevan and explore the city for free. Turkish Airlines offers free hotel stays for layovers over 6 hours. Check GetYourGuide for tours during your layover.
  6. Sign up for airline newsletters — Turkish Airlines, Pegasus, and FlyOne often send flash sales. I once got a flight to Batumi for ₽8 000 (≈ $86) via a Pegasus email deal. Don't skip this.
  7. Use a VPN — Sometimes, booking from a different country (e.g., Turkey or Georgia) shows lower prices. Set your VPN to Turkey and check Aviasales — I've seen 20% discounts.
  8. Book one-way tickets separately — For connecting flights, booking two one-way tickets (e.g., London to Istanbul, then Istanbul to Batumi) can be cheaper than a round-trip. Use Hotellook to compare both options.
  9. Check budget airlines for domestic legs — If flying to Tbilisi first, use Georgian low-cost carriers (e.g., Vanilla Sky or Air Batumi) for the final leg. One-way from Tbilisi to Batumi starts at ₽3 000 (≈ $32). Use Kiwitaxi for airport transfers if needed.
  10. Use eSIM for connectivity — Stay online during your trip with Airalo. Their eSIM plans start at ₽500 (≈ $5) for 1GB in Georgia. This helps you track flight changes and book last-minute deals without roaming fees.

When to Book

Timing is everything for cheap flights to Batumi. Based on 2025 data and trends for 2026, here's my advice:

  • For summer travel (June-August): Book 3-4 months in advance. Prices start rising in March, so lock in by February. I recommend checking Aviasales in January for early bird deals.
  • For spring/autumn (March-May, September-October): Book 6-8 weeks ahead. These are shoulder seasons, so flexibility helps. Use Hotellook to compare dates — a shift of 2 days can save you ₽5 000 (≈ $54).
  • For winter (November-February): Book 4-6 weeks ahead. Prices are lowest, but avoid December holidays (Christmas, New Year) when rates spike. January and February are best — I've seen flights from Moscow for ₽12 000 (≈ $130) round-trip.
  • Best day to book: Tuesday or Wednesday. Airlines often release sales on Monday, and by Tuesday, prices adjust. Check Aviasales on Tuesday mornings for the best deals.
  • Last-minute tips: If you're flexible, check flights 2-3 weeks before departure. Sometimes, unsold seats drop to ₽20 000 (≈ $216) round-trip from Istanbul. Use GetYourGuide for last-minute tours once you arrive.
