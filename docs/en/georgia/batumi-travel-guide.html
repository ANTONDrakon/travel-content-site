Batumi is Georgia’s Black Sea gem—a city where Soviet-era charm meets futuristic skyscrapers, and the scent of sea salt mingles with the aroma of fresh khachapuri. This vibrant port city has transformed into a must-visit destination, offering a unique blend of subtropical beaches, lush botanical gardens, and a buzzing nightlife scene. Unlike the ancient stone streets of Tbilisi, Batumi feels like a breath of fresh air—literally, with its palm-lined boulevards and a 7-kilometer seaside promenade perfect for lazy strolls or bike rides.

Why is 2026 the year to visit? Because Batumi is still an underrated gem, offering incredible value for money compared to other Black Sea resorts. You’ll find modern casinos, a lively Old Town with 19th-century architecture, and the iconic Alphabet Tower spinning slowly against the skyline. The city is a cultural melting pot, where Georgian hospitality meets a relaxed, resort-like vibe. Whether you’re sipping local wine at a beachfront café or hiking the nearby Mtirala National Park, Batumi surprises at every turn.

For Russian travelers, Batumi feels like a home away from home—no language barrier (Russian is widely spoken), visa-free access for up to a year, and direct flights make it effortless. The city is safe, walkable, and packed with Instagram-worthy spots. If you’re craving a seaside escape with a twist of European flair, Batumi is your answer.

How to Get There

Batumi International Airport (BUS) receives flights from major hubs like Moscow, Istanbul, and Tel Aviv. From Moscow, direct flights take about 3 hours, with average prices ranging from ₽8 000 to ₽20 000 (≈ $85–$215) for a round trip in economy. You can also fly via Tbilisi and take a 5-hour marshrutka (minibus) for about ₽1 500 (≈ $16). Batumi’s neighborhoods cater to every style. Top 15 Attractions

  1. Batumi Boulevard — A 7-km seaside promenade with bike rentals, cafes, and the famous Dancing Fountains. Free entry. Open 24/7.
  2. Alphabet Tower — A 130-meter tower shaped like DNA, with an observation deck. Ticket: ₽1 000 (≈ $11). Hours: 10:00–22:00.
  3. Batumi Botanical Garden — 108 hectares of exotic plants overlooking the sea. Ticket: ₽800 (≈ $9). Hours: 09:00–20:00.
  4. Argo Cable Car — A 2.5-km ride up to Anuria Hill for panoramic views. Round trip: ₽1 200 (≈ $13). Hours: 10:00–23:00.
  5. Batumi Dolphinarium — Daily shows with bottlenose dolphins. Ticket: ₽1 500 (≈ $16). Hours: 12:00, 15:00, 18:00.
  6. Europe Square — A cobblestone square with the Medea statue and a stunning clock tower. Free. Open 24/7.
  7. Nephew’s House (Chacha Tower) — A quirky tower that pours chacha (Georgian brandy) every hour. Free to watch. Hours: 10:00–22:00.
  8. Batumi Archaeological Museum — Artifacts from ancient Colchis. Ticket: ₽400 (≈ $4). Hours: 10:00–18:00 (closed Mon).
  9. 6 May Park — A serene park with a lake, paddle boats, and the dolphinarium. Free entry. Open 24/7.
  10. Mtirala National Park — A rainforest with hiking trails and waterfalls. Entry: ₽200 (≈ $2). Hours: 09:00–18:00.
  11. Gonio Fortress — A Roman-era fort 15 km south of Batumi. Ticket: ₽300 (≈ $3). Hours: 09:00–19:00.
  12. Batumi Piazza — A beautiful square with mosaic floors and live music. Free. Open 24/7.
  13. Colchis Fountain — A golden fountain with statues of mythical figures. Free. Open 24/7.
  14. Batumi Sea Port — Watch ferries and cruise ships depart. Free. Accessible 24/7.
  15. Batumi State Music Center — A modern concert hall with nightly performances. Tickets from ₽1 000 (≈ $11). Hours vary.

Getting Around

Batumi is walkable, but for longer trips, use the city buses (₽0.80 per ride, ≈ $0.01). Taxis are cheap: ₽150–300 (≈ $2–$3) within the city. Rent a bike from the Boulevard for ₽200/hour (≈ $2). For airport transfers, pre-book on Kiwitaxi for a flat rate of ₽1 500 (≈ $16). Car rentals start at ₽2 500/day (≈ $27) from local agencies.

Where to Eat

Start with Adjarian khachapuri (boat-shaped bread with egg and cheese) at Retro Batumi — ₽600–900 (≈ $6–$10). For street food, try churchkhela (nut candy) from vendors for ₽100 (≈ $1). Seafood lovers flock to Sanremo on the Boulevard for grilled fish — mains ₽1 000–2 500 (≈ $11–$27). Budget eaters love KFC-style spots with shawarma for ₽300 (≈ $3). For a splurge, Barbarestan offers traditional Georgian dishes in a cozy setting — dinner for two: ₽4 000 (≈ $43).

Best Time to Visit

Batumi has a humid subtropical climate. June–September is peak season—temperatures hit 30°C (86°F), and the sea is warm (25°C/77°F). Prices double during this time. May and October are sweet spots—pleasant weather (20–25°C/68–77°F) with fewer crowds. Winter (December–February) is mild (5–10°C/41–50°F) but rainy — great for budget travelers, with hotel prices dropping by 50%. Avoid August if you hate crowds; July is the driest month.

Visa & Documents

Russian citizens can enter Georgia visa-free for up to 1 year. You need a valid passport (must be valid for at least 6 months from entry). No registration is required for stays under 90 days. For the Maldives visa section, note: Перед вылетом заполните декларацию IMUGA на imuga.immigration.gov.mv и получите QR-код — без него не посадят на рейс. For Batumi, no such form is needed—just your passport.

10 Travel Tips

Don't forget travel insurance!

get travel insurance
  • Cash is king — Many places don’t accept cards. Carry Georgian lari (GEL). ATMs are abundant.
  • Learn basic Georgian — “Gamarjoba” (hello) and “Madloba” (thank you) go a long way.
  • Beware of taxi scams — Always negotiate the fare before getting in, or use Bolt app.
  • Try the local wine — Batumi is near wine regions like Kakheti. Visit a wine bar for a tasting.
  • Pack for rain — Even in summer, sudden showers are common. Bring an umbrella.
  • Respect dress codes — Cover shoulders and knees when entering churches (e.g., Batumi Cathedral).
  • Buy a SIM card — Magti or Silknet offer 30GB for ₽500 (≈ $5). For eSIM, use Airalo.
  • Bargain at markets — At the Batumi Bazaar, haggling is expected for souvenirs.
  • Check the sunset time — The best views are from the Boulevard at golden hour (around 19:00 in summer).
  • Book tours in advance — For Mtirala or Gonio, book via GetYourGuide to avoid crowds.
