15 Best Things to Do in Batumi 2026

If you’re dreaming of a seaside escape that mixes modern glamour with old-world charm, Batumi is your spot. This Black Sea gem in Georgia has exploded onto the travel scene, and for good reason—its subtropical climate, buzzing boulevards, and quirky architecture make it unlike any other city in the Caucasus. I’ve wandered its streets countless times, and every visit reveals something new. Whether you’re here for the casinos, the cable cars, or just to dip your toes in the sea, Batumi delivers. Let me walk you through the 15 best things to do in 2026, plus itineraries, tours, and insider tips to help you plan like a pro.

15 Best Attractions

1. Batumi Boulevard

This 7-km seaside promenade is the city’s beating heart. Lined with palm trees, bike paths, and quirky sculptures, it’s perfect for morning jogs or sunset strolls. You’ll find fountains, cafes, and the famous moving “Love” statue (Ali and Nino) that merges and separates nightly. Free entry. Open 24/7. Reach it from the city center on foot (5 minutes from Europe Square) or by bus #10. Tip: Rent a bike (₽300/hour, ≈ $3.50) to cover more ground.

2. Alphabetic Tower

This 130-meter-high spiral tower celebrates the Georgian alphabet and script. Take the glass elevator to the observation deck for panoramic views of the coast and mountains. Inside, there’s a restaurant and a small museum. Tickets: ₽1 200 (≈ $14). Hours: 10:00–22:00 daily. Located on the boulevard, a 10-minute walk from the port. Tip: Go at sunset for golden-hour photos.

3. Batumi Cable Car (Argo)

Glide 2.5 km up to the Anuria Plateau in a glass cabin for jaw-dropping views of the city and sea. At the top, there’s a cafe, a Ferris wheel, and hiking trails. Tickets: ₽1 500 (≈ $17) round-trip. Hours: 10:00–22:00 (until 23:00 in summer). The lower station is at Gogebashvili Street, near the boulevard. Tip: Visit on a clear day—clouds can obscure the view.

4. Batumi Piazza

A gorgeous Italian-style square with mosaic floors, colorful facades, and live music in the evenings. It’s surrounded by restaurants and bars—perfect for a glass of Georgian wine. Free entry. Open 24/7. Located in the Old Town, a 5-minute walk from Europe Square. Tip: Try the local khachapuri (cheese bread) at a nearby cafe—₽400 (≈ $4.50).

5. Batumi Botanical Garden

Spread over 108 hectares on the Green Cape, this garden showcases plants from around the world—from Japanese cherry trees to Australian eucalyptus. It’s a peaceful escape from the city buzz. Tickets: ₽600 (≈ $7). Hours: 09:00–18:00 (until 20:00 in summer). Take bus #15 from the city center (30 minutes). Tip: Pack a picnic; there are few food options inside.

6. Europe Square

The historic heart of Batumi, framed by the 19th-century Clock Tower and the Medea statue (with the Golden Fleece). It’s a lively spot for people-watching and street performances. Free entry. Open 24/7. A 2-minute walk from the boulevard. Tip: Come at night when the buildings are lit up.

7. Dolphinarium

Watch bottlenose dolphins perform acrobatics in a modern indoor pool. Shows include music, lights, and playful interactions. Tickets: ₽1 000 (≈ $12) for adults, ₽600 (≈ $7) for children. Show times: 11:00, 15:00, 19:00 (check schedule). Located at the end of the boulevard, near the port. Tip: Book online via Booking.com or local kiosks to avoid queues.

8. Batumi Sea Port

This working port is also a hub for boat tours and sunset cruises. Walk along the pier to see cargo ships and yachts, or hop on a ferry to the nearby resort of Kobuleti. Free to walk around. Boat tours start at ₽1 500 (≈ $17) per person. Open 24/7. Reach it from the boulevard (10 minutes on foot). Tip: Take a 1-hour evening cruise for the best skyline views.

9. 6 May Park

A lush urban park with a lake, swan boats, and a small zoo. It’s a family favorite for paddle boating and picnicking. Free entry to the park; zoo tickets: ₽300 (≈ $3.50). Hours: 08:00–22:00. Located on Chavchavadze Street, a 15-minute walk from the center. Tip: Rent a pedal boat (₽500/hour, ≈ $6) for a fun break.

10. Argo Cable Car Upper Station

At the top of the cable car, you’ll find a Ferris wheel, a rope park, and a panoramic restaurant. The views stretch from the sea to the Lesser Caucasus. Free to walk around; Ferris wheel: ₽500 (≈ $6). Hours: same as cable car. Tip: Try the local lemonade (₽200, ≈ $2.30) at the cafe.

11. Batumi Archaeological Museum

Home to artifacts from ancient Colchis, including gold jewelry and Greek pottery. It’s a compact but rich museum that tells the story of the region’s Greek and Roman past. Tickets: ₽400 (≈ $4.50). Hours: 10:00–18:00 (closed Mondays). Located on Chavchavadze Street, a 10-minute walk from Europe Square. Tip: Combine with a visit to the nearby Piazza for lunch.

12. Batumi Piazza Fountain Show

Every evening, the Piazza’s central fountain comes alive with a synchronized light-and-music show. It’s free and lasts about 15 minutes. Free entry. Shows at 20:00, 21:00, 22:00 (seasonal). Tip: Grab a seat at a cafe 15 minutes early for the best view.

13. Batumi Mosque (Orta Jame)

This 19th-century mosque with a striking blue facade and minaret is a peaceful spot for reflection. Non-Muslims are welcome outside prayer times. Free entry. Hours: 09:00–17:00 (except prayer times). Located on Gamsakhurdia Street, a 5-minute walk from the boulevard. Tip: Dress modestly (cover shoulders and knees).

14. Batumi State Music Center

This modern concert hall hosts classical concerts, jazz performances, and Georgian folk shows. The architecture itself is a photo op. Tickets: ₽800–₽2 000 (≈ $9–$23). Hours: box office 10:00–19:00; shows usually at 19:00. Located on the boulevard, near the Alphabetic Tower. Tip: Check the schedule on GetYourGuide for upcoming events.

15. Batumi Nightlife (Casinos & Clubs)

Batumi is famous for its casinos—think grand entrances, free drinks, and high stakes. For a quieter night, try the rooftop bars along the boulevard. Free entry to most casinos (18+). Casinos open 24/7. Tip: Stick to the boulevard area for safe, lively nightlife.

1-Day, 2-Day, 3-Day Itineraries

1-Day Itinerary

Start at Europe Square (30 minutes), then walk to the Boulevard for a morning stroll. Visit the Alphabetic Tower (1 hour). Lunch at the Piazza (1 hour). Afternoon: ride the Cable Car (2 hours). Evening: dinner on the boulevard and the Fountain Show (1 hour). Total walking: ~6 km.

2-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Same as above, plus the Botanical Garden (3 hours) in the afternoon. Day 2: Morning at 6 May Park (2 hours), then the Dolphinarium (1.5 hours). Afternoon: Archaeological Museum (1.5 hours) and Mosque (30 minutes). Evening: casino or rooftop bar. Total walking: ~8 km/day.

3-Day Itinerary

Day 1 & 2: Same as 2-day plan. Day 3: Morning at Batumi Sea Port for a boat tour (2 hours). Afternoon: explore the Music Center (1 hour) and relax on the beach. Evening: dinner at a seaside restaurant and a night at the casino. Total walking: ~5 km/day.

Free Attractions

  • Europe Square – Iconic statues and architecture.
  • Batumi Piazza – Mosaic floors and live music.
  • 6 May Park – Lake, gardens, and zoo (free entrance to park).
  • Batumi Sea Port – Stroll the pier and watch ships.
  • Batumi Fountain Show – Evening light show.

Practical Tips

Getting Around Batumi

The city is walkable—most attractions are within 30 minutes on foot. For longer trips, use city buses (₽200/trip, ≈ $2.30) or taxis (₽300–₽500, ≈ $3.50–$6). Use Kiwitaxi for airport transfers (₽1 200, ≈ $14). For day trips, rent a car from Aviasales (from ₽2 000/day, ≈ $23).

Combo Tickets & Discounts

Buy a Batumi City Pass (₽3 000, ≈ $35) for entry to the Alphabetic Tower, Cable Car, Dolphinarium, and Botanical Garden. It’s valid for 48 hours and saves about 20% compared to individual tickets. Get it at the tourist office on the boulevard or via Hotellook.

Where to Stay

Book hotels on Booking.com or Agoda. Mid-range options near the boulevard start at ₽4 000/night (≈ $46). For luxury, try the Sheraton Batumi (from ₽12 000/night, ≈ $140).

SIM Card & Connectivity

Get an eSIM from Airalo (from ₽500, ≈ $6) for data. Local SIMs (Magti, Beeline) are available at the airport for ₽400 (≈ $4.50).

Best Time to Visit

May–September for beach weather (25–30°C). July–August is peak season—book early. Winter (December–February) is quiet and cheaper, but many attractions close early.

Money-Saving Tips

  • Eat at local khachapuri stalls (₽300–₽500, ≈ $3.50–$6) instead of tourist restaurants.
  • Walk everywhere—it’s flat and safe.
  • Visit free attractions first (they’re the best!).
  • Use public buses instead of taxis.

Batumi surprised me—I came for a weekend and stayed a week. Its blend of Soviet grit and modern glitz, plus the warm Black Sea, makes it addictive. Whether you’re riding the cable car at sunset or sipping wine in the Piazza, you’ll understand why this city is rising fast. Pack your walking shoes and an open mind—Batumi 2026 is ready for you.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do attractions cost in Batumi?

Entrance fees vary: from free attractions to $20-30 for museums. Many venues have discount days.

Do I need a guide in Batumi?

A guide is recommended for historical sites and complex routes. Self-guided travel is possible with audio guides and maps.
