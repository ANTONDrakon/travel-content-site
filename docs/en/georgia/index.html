Information

Practical information

🛂 Visa

Visa-free entry up to 1 year for Russian citizens.

💱 Currency

Georgian Lari (GEL). $1 ≈ 2.7 GEL. USD accepted in Tbilisi.

🗣 Language

Georgian. English spoken in Tbilisi, often Russian.

🚌 Transport

Minibuses, buses, trains. Car rental from $25/day.

🛡 Safety

Very safe. Low crime rate.

📶 Internet & SIM

Wi-Fi everywhere. Local SIM (Magti, Beeline) — from $3 with 10 GB.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a visa for Georgia?

No. Visa-free entry up to 1 year for Russian citizens.

When is the best time to visit Georgia?

Summer: for mountains. Autumn: for wine. Winter: for skiing.

How much does a trip to Georgia cost?

Budget: from $400/week. Comfort: from $800.

What to see in Georgia?

Tbilisi, Mtskheta, Kazbegi, Svaneti, Batumi, Kakheti.

Is Georgia safe?

Yes, very safe. One of the safest countries.

Can I travel by car?

Yes, excellent roads. Rental from $25/day.
