How to Find Cheap Flights to Tbilisi 2026

Updated: July 2026 • Expert guide • ⏱ 5 min read

Planning a trip to Tbilisi in 2026? You're in for a treat—this Georgian capital is a blend of ancient charm, modern cafes, and jaw-dropping mountain views. But before you can sip khinkali and explore the cobblestone streets of the Old Town, you need a flight that doesn't drain your wallet. I've been there, refreshing flight search pages at 2 AM, and I've learned a few tricks to snag affordable tickets. Let me walk you through everything you need to know about finding cheap flights to Tbilisi in 2026, from direct routes to sneaky hacks that save you hundreds.

Tbilisi is served by one main airport: Tbilisi International Airport (TBS), located about 17 kilometers southeast of the city center. It's a modern hub with connections across Europe, Asia, and the Middle East. Several airlines operate here, including Georgian Airways, Turkish Airlines, Qatar Airways, Wizz Air, and Pegasus Airlines. The key is knowing which routes and seasons offer the best deals.

Direct Flights

If you value time over a few extra rubles, direct flights are your best bet. They're faster and less stressful—no worrying about missed connections or long layovers. In 2026, several airlines offer non-stop routes to Tbilisi from major cities worldwide. Here's a breakdown based on typical 2025-2026 trends (remember, prices are estimates and can vary):

  • Georgian Airways – Direct from Moscow (VKO/SVO) and Tel Aviv. Moscow departures average ₽15 000 – ₽25 000 (≈ $160 – $270) one-way. Tel Aviv flights hover around ₽20 000 – ₽30 000 (≈ $215 – $325).
  • Turkish Airlines – Direct from Istanbul (IST) to TBS. Flights start at ₽12 000 – ₽18 000 (≈ $130 – $195) one-way, often with great service and meal options.
  • Wizz Air – Budget carrier with direct flights from Abu Dhabi, Budapest, and Warsaw. Expect prices from ₽8 000 – ₽14 000 (≈ $86 – $150) one-way, but watch for baggage fees.
  • Qatar Airways – Direct from Doha (DOH). Fares range ₽25 000 – ₽40 000 (≈ $270 – $430) one-way, but the quality is premium.
  • Pegasus Airlines – Low-cost direct from Istanbul (SAW). Prices as low as ₽7 000 – ₽12 000 (≈ $75 – $130) one-way—great for budget travelers.

For travelers from Russia, direct flights from Moscow, St. Petersburg, and Sochi are common. Check Aviasales for the latest direct options—they aggregate all airlines and show you the cheapest dates.

Connecting Flights

Connecting flights are often the cheapest way to reach Tbilisi, especially if you're flexible with time. You might save 30-50% compared to direct routes, but you'll spend extra hours in transit. Common stopover cities include Istanbul, Doha, Dubai, Baku, and Yerevan. Here are the best options for 2026:

  • Via Istanbul (IST or SAW) – Turkish Airlines and Pegasus offer frequent connections. Total travel time: 6-10 hours. Prices from ₽10 000 – ₽20 000 (≈ $108 – $215) one-way.
  • Via Baku (GYD) – Azerbaijan Airlines (AZAL) and Buta Airways connect through Baku. Duration: 5-8 hours. Fares start at ₽8 000 – ₽15 000 (≈ $86 – $162) one-way.
  • Via Yerevan (EVN) – Fly to Armenia first, then take a bus or short flight to Tbilisi. Total cost: ₽6 000 – ₽12 000 (≈ $65 – $130) one-way, but add 3-4 hours for ground travel.
  • Via Dubai (DXB) – Emirates and flydubai offer connections. Duration: 8-12 hours. Prices from ₽18 000 – ₽30 000 (≈ $195 – $325) one-way.
  • Via Doha (DOH) – Qatar Airways connections. Duration: 7-11 hours. Fares from ₽15 000 – ₽25 000 (≈ $162 – $270) one-way.

Remember that flights can be compared across dates using tools like Aviasales or Hotellook (which also shows hotel prices). For example, flying on a Tuesday or Wednesday is often 20-30% cheaper than weekend departures. I once found a flight from Moscow to Tbilisi via Istanbul for just ₽9 500 (≈ $103) by booking a month in advance and choosing a red-eye connection.

10 Hacks for Cheap Flights

After years of hunting deals, I've compiled my top 10 hacks to save money on Tbilisi flights. These aren't just generic tips—they're specific to this route and use real services you can try today.

  1. Use Aviasales Price Alerts – Set up alerts for TBS on Aviasales. I once got a notification for a Moscow-Tbilisi round trip at ₽12 000 (≈ $130) in February—normally it's double. The app tracks price history so you know a good deal.
  2. Fly with Wizz Air or Pegasus – These budget carriers often have sales for ₽6 000 – ₽10 000 (≈ $65 – $108) one-way from Istanbul or Abu Dhabi. Just remember to add baggage costs—paying for a 20kg bag can add ₽2 000 – ₽4 000 (≈ $22 – $43).
  3. Book Connecting Flights via Baku – Azerbaijan Airlines (AZAL) and Buta Airways are consistently cheap. A round trip from Moscow via Baku can be as low as ₽14 000 (≈ $151) in November. Check Aviasales for combo deals.
  4. Consider a Stopover in Yerevan – Fly to Yerevan (EVN) for ₽8 000 – ₽12 000 (≈ $86 – $130) one-way, then take a marshrutka (minibus) to Tbilisi for ₽1 000 – ₽1 500 (≈ $11 – $16). The bus takes 5-6 hours but saves you serious cash.
  5. Use Hotellook for Hotel + Flight Combos – Hotellook sometimes bundles flights with hotels. I found a package from Istanbul to Tbilisi with 3 nights at a 4-star hotel for ₽25 000 (≈ $270) total—cheaper than booking separately.
  6. Book on a Tuesday or Wednesday – Airlines often release sales on Monday nights, so Tuesday mornings have the best prices. Avoid Friday and Sunday departures—they're 20-30% pricier.
  7. Use Incognito Mode – Search for flights in private browsing to avoid dynamic pricing. I've seen prices jump ₽3 000 – ₽5 000 (≈ $32 – $54) after repeated searches on the same site.
  8. Check GetYourGuide for Airport Transfers – While not a flight hack, saving on ground transport helps. GetYourGuide offers Tbilisi airport transfers from ₽2 500 (≈ $27)—cheaper than taxis. Pre-book to lock in the rate.
  9. Use Kiwitaxi for Group Travel – If you're traveling with friends, Kiwitaxi offers fixed-price airport rides. Splitting a van from TBS to city center costs about ₽1 500 (≈ $16) per person—much less than individual taxis.
  10. Stay Connected with Airalo – Once you land, use Airalo for an eSIM. A 7-day Georgia plan costs ₽700 (≈ $7.50), so you can check flight deals on the go without roaming fees.

When to Book

Timing is everything when booking flights to Tbilisi. Based on 2025 data and expert predictions for 2026, here's the optimal booking window:

  • For peak season (June-August): Book 3-4 months in advance. Prices start climbing in March, so snag your tickets by February or early March. Expect to pay ₽35 000 – ₽55 000 (≈ $378 – $594) round-trip from Moscow.
  • For shoulder season (April-May, September-October): Book 2-3 months ahead. April and May see Easter holidays, so book by January. September is more flexible—wait for sales in July. Prices range ₽22 000 – ₽35 000 (≈ $238 – $378).
  • For low season (November-February): You can book just 1-2 months in advance. Flights from Istanbul can drop to ₽10 000 – ₽15 000 (≈ $108 – $162) round-trip. I once booked a December flight from Moscow just 3 weeks out for ₽13 000 (≈ $140).
  • General rule: The best deals appear 6-8 weeks before departure for most routes. Use Aviasales to track price trends—they show you if prices are rising or falling.

One more tip: avoid booking too early (more than 6 months out) as airlines haven't released their cheapest fares yet. And never book on the day of departure—last-minute deals are rare for Tbilisi, and you'll pay a premium.

With these tips, you're ready to find a cheap flight to Tbilisi in 2026. Remember to check multiple sites, stay flexible with dates, and use the services I mentioned—Aviasales, Hotellook, Booking, Agoda, GetYourGuide, Kiwitaxi, and Airalo are all reliable partners that can save you time and money. Happy travels, and enjoy the sulfur baths, wine, and warm Georgian hospitality!
