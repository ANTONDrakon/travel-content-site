Direct Flights

Direct flights to Colombo (CMB) are your fastest bet, but they’re not available from every city. In 2026, expect these airlines to offer non-stop service, with prices varying by season and demand.

Airlines and Departure Cities

  • Emirates – From Dubai (DXB), daily flights. Approximate round-trip: ₽45 000–₽70 000 ($490–$760).
  • Qatar Airways – From Doha (DOH), multiple daily flights. Approximate round-trip: ₽50 000–₽75 000 ($540–$810).
  • Singapore Airlines – From Singapore (SIN), daily flights. Approximate round-trip: ₽55 000–₽85 000 ($600–$920).
  • Air India – From Delhi (DEL) and Mumbai (BOM), daily flights. Approximate round-trip: ₽30 000–₽50 000 ($330–$540).
  • Malaysia Airlines – From Kuala Lumpur (KUL), daily flights. Approximate round-trip: ₽40 000–₽65 000 ($430–$700).
  • Turkish Airlines – From Istanbul (IST), daily flights. Approximate round-trip: ₽60 000–₽90 000 ($650–$970).
  • SriLankan Airlines – From London (LHR), Paris (CDG), Frankfurt (FRA), and other European hubs. Approximate round-trip: ₽70 000–₽110 000 ($760–$1190).

Direct flights are convenient, but they’re often pricier than connecting options. If you’re flexible, connecting flights can slash your costs significantly.

10 Hacks for Cheap Flights

I’ve gathered these tips from years of travel and feedback from readers. Use them to score the best deals to Unawatuna in 2026.

1. Use Incognito Mode or Clear Cookies

Airlines and booking sites sometimes track your searches and raise prices if they see repeated interest. Open a private browser window or clear your cookies before searching on Aviasales.

2. Book on Tuesday or Wednesday

Studies show that midweek flights are often cheaper—up to 15% less. Set a price alert on Hotellook to catch these drops.

3. Fly into Colombo, Then Take a Bus

Instead of flying directly to a nearby airport, land at CMB and take a local bus or train to Unawatuna (₽300–₽500, about $3–$5). It’s an adventure in itself!

4. Use a VPN to Change Your Location

If you’re booking from Europe, try setting your VPN to a country like India or Thailand. Prices can be 10–20% lower due to local market rates.

5. Look for Error Fares

Sign up for newsletters from Aviasales or other deal sites. Error fares (accidental low prices) pop up occasionally—grab them fast!

6. Book Connecting Flights Separately

Sometimes booking two one-way flights (e.g., London to Dubai on one airline, Dubai to Colombo on another) is cheaper than a round-trip. Use Hotellook to compare.

7. Travel Light with a Carry-On

Budget airlines like Air India or SriLankan Airlines charge extra for checked bags. Pack a 10kg carry-on to avoid fees (₽2 000–₽5 000, $22–$54 saved).

8. Use a Cashback or Rewards Credit Card

If you have a travel card with points, use it to book. Some cards offer 2–5% cashback on flight purchases.

9. Book a Multi-City Itinerary

If you’re visiting other destinations in Asia, book a multi-city flight (e.g., London–Colombo–Bangkok–London). It can be cheaper than a round-trip to Colombo alone.

10. Check Nearby Airports

Sometimes flying into Mattala Rajapaksa International Airport (HRI), about 3 hours from Unawatuna, is cheaper. Compare on Aviasales to see if it’s worth the drive.

When to Book

Timing is everything. For Unawatuna flights in 2026, aim to book 2–4 months in advance for the best rates. Here’s a breakdown:

  • Peak season (December–March): Book 4–6 months ahead. Prices rise sharply as the dates get closer.
  • Shoulder season (April, November): Book 2–3 months ahead. You’ll find decent deals, but not as low as low season.
  • Low season (May–October): Book 1–2 months ahead. Last-minute deals are common, but don’t wait too long—flights can still sell out on weekends.

For example, if you’re planning a trip in January 2026, start searching in September 2025. Use Hotellook to set price alerts—they’ll email you when fares drop.

Visa Requirements for Sri Lanka

Don’t forget: most travelers need an ETA (Electronic Travel Authorization) to enter Sri Lanka. Apply online at eta.gov.lk at least 2 weeks before your flight. The fee is about ₽2 500–₽3 500 ($27–$38) depending on your nationality. For Maldives visa sections, add: 'Перед вылетом заполните декларацию IMUGA на imuga.immigration.gov.mv и получите QR-код — без него не посадят на рейс.'

Getting from Colombo to Unawatuna

Once you land at CMB, you have a few options. A private taxi via Kiwitaxi costs about ₽3 000–₽5 000 ($33–$54) for a 2-hour ride. Alternatively, take the local bus (₽150–₽300, $1.60–$3.30) or a train (₽200–₽400, $2.20–$4.30) from Colombo Fort to Galle, then a tuk-tuk to Unawatuna. For a SIM card, grab a local eSIM from Airalo before you go—it’s cheaper than buying at the airport.
