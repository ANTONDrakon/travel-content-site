Why Travelers Choose Sri Lanka

🏖

Beaches Year-Round

South coast — golden sand, west coast — surfing. Water +28–30°C all year.

🏛

UNESCO Heritage

8 sites: Sigiriya, Polonnaruwa, Dambulla temples. 2500 years of history.

🌿

Tea & Nature

Tea plantations in Nuwara Eliya. Reserves with leopards, elephants, and whales.

💱

Budget-Friendly

Hotels from $25/night. Dinner from $3. One of Asia's most affordable destinations.

🐘

Safari

Yala and Udawalawe national parks. Elephants, leopards, crocodiles.

🧘

Yoga & Spa

Coastal retreats. Ayurvedic spas. Temple meditation.

Best Time to Visit Sri Lanka

☀️

West Coast

December–April

Peak season. Clear skies, +30°C. Perfect for beach.

🌊

East Coast

May–September

Dry season on east. Surfing in Arugam Bay.

🌧

Monsoons

October–November

Rain on south and west. Lowest prices.

Practical Information

🛂 Visa

Electronic visa (ETA) for Russian citizens. Online application, costs $50.

💱 Currency

Sri Lankan Rupee (LKR). $1 ≈ 300 LKR. USD accepted in hotels.

🗣 Language

Sinhala, Tamil. English spoken in tourist areas.

🚌 Transport

Buses (cheapest), tuk-tuks, trains. Car with driver from $30/day.

🛡 Safety

Generally safe. Beware of scams in Colombo and on beaches.

📶 Internet & SIM

Wi-Fi in hotels. Local SIM (Dialog, Mobitel) — from $5 with 10 GB.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a visa for Sri Lanka?

Yes, an electronic visa (ETA). Applied online within 24 hours, costs $50.

When is the best time to visit Sri Lanka?

West coast: December–April. East coast: May–September.

How much does a trip to Sri Lanka cost?

Budget: from $500/week. Comfort: from $1000. Including flights.

Is Sri Lanka safe?

Yes, generally safe. Follow standard precautions.

What to see in Sri Lanka?

Sigiriya, Dambulla temples, tea plantations, national parks, beaches.

Do I need a tour operator?

No, you can travel independently. But a tour operator simplifies logistics.
