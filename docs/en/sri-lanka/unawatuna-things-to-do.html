15 Best Things to Do in Unawatuna 2026

Unawatuna is a crescent-shaped beach town on Sri Lanka’s southern coast that feels like a tropical secret whispered among travelers. When I first stepped onto its golden sand, the gentle lapping of turquoise waves and the scent of spicy street food drew me in completely. Beyond the beach, this vibrant village offers a treasure trove of attractions — from ancient temples and wildlife encounters to adrenaline-pumping water sports. In 2026, Unawatuna is more accessible and exciting than ever, with new eco-lodges, improved roads, and a growing food scene. Whether you’re a solo backpacker, a family with kids, or a couple seeking romance, this guide will help you discover the best things to do here. Let’s dive into the top 15 attractions that make Unawatuna unforgettable.

15 Best Attractions

1. Unawatuna Beach

The heart of the town, this palm-fringed bay is perfect for swimming, sunbathing, and sipping fresh coconut water. The calm waters are ideal for beginners learning to snorkel. Ticket price: Free. Hours: Always open. How to get there: A 5-minute walk from any hotel in the center. Tip: Visit at sunrise (around 5:30 AM) to avoid crowds and watch fishermen haul in their nets.

2. Jungle Beach

A hidden cove about 20 minutes’ walk from Unawatuna Beach, accessed via a winding jungle path. The shallow, crystal-clear water is great for snorkeling among colorful fish. Ticket price: Free. Hours: Always open. How to get there: From the main beach, follow the signs up the hill past the Japanese Peace Pagoda. Tip: Bring water shoes — the path can be slippery after rain, and the rocky shore is tough on bare feet.

3. Japanese Peace Pagoda

Perched on a hilltop, this gleaming white stupa offers panoramic views of the coastline and jungle. Built by Japanese Buddhists, it’s a serene spot for meditation and photos. Ticket price: Free. Hours: 6:00 AM – 6:00 PM. How to get there: A 15-minute uphill walk from Unawatuna Beach. Tip: Go at sunset for golden light and fewer tourists — the monkeys here are cheeky, so keep your snacks hidden.

4. Rumassala Sanctuary

A lush hill with hiking trails, ancient trees, and a legendary connection to the Ramayana epic — locals believe it’s a piece of the Himalayas dropped by Hanuman. The sanctuary is a biodiversity hotspot with endemic birds and butterflies. Ticket price: Free (donation suggested at the entrance). Hours: 7:00 AM – 6:00 PM. How to get there: Walk 10 minutes from Unawatuna Beach to the trailhead near the Peace Pagoda. Tip: Hire a local guide (₽1 500 ≈ $16) to spot rare wildlife and hear the mythic stories.

5. Galle Fort

A UNESCO World Heritage site just 20 minutes away, this 17th-century Dutch fort is a living museum with cobblestone streets, boutique shops, and colonial architecture. Walk the ramparts for ocean views. Ticket price: Free to enter; museum entry ₽750 ≈ $8. Hours: 24/7 for the fort; museums 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM. How to get there: Take a tuk-tuk (₽500 ≈ $5) or bus (₽50 ≈ $0.50) from Unawatuna. Tip: Visit on a Sunday for the Galle Fort Walking Tour (₽1 200 ≈ $13) — it’s led by passionate local historians.

6. Sea Turtle Hatchery

Located in nearby Habaraduwa (10 minutes by tuk-tuk), this conservation center protects turtle eggs and releases hatchlings into the sea. You can see baby turtles in tanks and learn about their life cycle. Ticket price: ₽1 500 ≈ $16. Hours: 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM. How to get there: Tuk-tuk from Unawatuna (₽600 ≈ $6). Tip: Call ahead to check if hatchlings are being released — timing your visit for a release is magical.

7. Unawatuna Coral Reef

Just offshore, this vibrant reef is teeming with parrotfish, clownfish, and sea turtles. Snorkeling here is a must — the water is warm and clear year-round. Ticket price: Free (bring your own gear); guided snorkeling tour ₽2 500 ≈ $27. Hours: Best from 9:00 AM – 2:00 PM when the tide is low. How to get there: Swim from the beach or rent a kayak (₽1 000 ≈ $11 per hour). Tip: Avoid touching the coral — it’s fragile and protected by local laws.

8. Yatagala Raja Maha Viharaya

A 2,000-year-old Buddhist temple carved into a massive rock, featuring ancient murals and a giant reclining Buddha. The peaceful atmosphere is a stark contrast to the beach buzz. Ticket price: Free (donation welcome). Hours: 6:00 AM – 6:00 PM. How to get there: 10-minute tuk-tuk ride (₽400 ≈ $4) from Unawatuna. Tip: Dress modestly (cover shoulders and knees) and remove shoes before entering the shrine.

9. Whale Watching in Mirissa

From November to April, Mirissa (30 minutes away) is the best spot in Sri Lanka to see blue whales, sperm whales, and dolphins. Boats depart early morning for a 3–4 hour excursion. Ticket price: ₽6 000 ≈ $65 per person (includes breakfast and snacks). Hours: Departs at 6:30 AM. How to get there: Book a tour through your hotel or GetYourGuide — they include pickup from Unawatuna. Tip: Take motion sickness pills an hour before departure — the sea can get choppy.

10. Handunugoda Tea Estate

A boutique tea plantation near Galle where you can see how white tea (the rarest type) is made. The guided tour ends with a tasting of six different teas. Ticket price: ₽1 200 ≈ $13 (includes tour and tasting). Hours: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM. How to get there: 15-minute tuk-tuk ride (₽500 ≈ $5). Tip: Buy a box of Virgin White Tea (₽2 500 ≈ $27) — it’s a unique souvenir that doesn’t oxidize.

11. Unawatuna Night Market

Every Friday evening, the beachfront transforms into a lively market with stalls selling handmade jewelry, sarongs, street food, and live music. It’s a social hub for locals and travelers. Ticket price: Free entry. Hours: 5:00 PM – 10:00 PM. How to get there: Walk from anywhere in town. Tip: Try the kottu roti (₽400 ≈ $4) — a spicy chopped flatbread stir-fry that’s a Sri Lankan classic.

12. Madu River Safari

A boat ride through mangrove forests and narrow canals, where you’ll spot monitor lizards, monkeys, and exotic birds. The safari includes a visit to a cinnamon island and a Buddhist temple on water. Ticket price: ₽3 000 ≈ $32 per person (2 hours). Hours: 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM. How to get there: 30-minute drive from Unawatuna — book a tour via GetYourGuide for hassle-free transport. Tip: Go early morning (8:00 AM) for the best wildlife viewing and cooler weather.

13. Snorkeling at Hikkaduwa Coral Sanctuary

About 30 minutes north, this marine park has some of the healthiest coral in Sri Lanka. The shallow lagoon is perfect for beginners, with turtles often swimming right up to you. Ticket price: Free (park entry); snorkel gear rental ₽1 000 ≈ $11. Hours: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM. How to get there: Bus (₽100 ≈ $1) or tuk-tuk (₽1 200 ≈ $13) from Unawatuna. Tip: Bring an underwater camera — the visibility is often 15–20 meters.

14. Koggala Lake

A serene lake with islands, temples, and a spice garden. You can take a boat tour to see stilt fishermen and visit a cinnamon factory. Ticket price: Free to walk around; boat tour ₽2 000 ≈ $22. Hours: 7:00 AM – 6:00 PM. How to get there: 15-minute tuk-tuk ride (₽500 ≈ $5). Tip: Combine this with a visit to the Handunugoda Tea Estate — they’re 10 minutes apart.

15. Surfing at Unawatuna

While not as famous as Arugam Bay, Unawatuna has gentle waves perfect for beginners. Several surf schools line the beach offering lessons and board rentals. Ticket price: Board rental ₽1 500 ≈ $16 per hour; lesson ₽3 000 ≈ $32 for 1.5 hours. Hours: Best waves from November to March, 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM. How to get there: Walk to the southern end of the beach. Tip: Take a lesson with GetYourGuide — they provide rash guards and reef-safe sunscreen.

1-Day, 2-Day, 3-Day Itineraries

1-Day Itinerary: Unawatuna Highlights

Morning (7:00 AM – 12:00 PM): Start at Unawatuna Beach for a sunrise swim (7:00 AM). Walk to the Japanese Peace Pagoda (8:00 AM) for views, then hike through Rumassala Sanctuary (9:00 AM – 11:00 AM). Finish with a tuk-tuk to Yatagala Raja Maha Viharaya (11:30 AM – 12:00 PM). Lunch (12:00 PM – 1:00 PM): Eat at Kingfisher Restaurant (₽1 500 ≈ $16 for a seafood platter). Afternoon (1:00 PM – 5:00 PM): Snorkel at Unawatuna Coral Reef (1:30 PM – 3:00 PM), then relax on the beach. Evening (5:00 PM – 9:00 PM): Visit Galle Fort for sunset (5:30 PM – 7:00 PM), then enjoy the Night Market (if it’s Friday) or dinner at The Shack Beach Cafe (₽1 200 ≈ $13).

2-Day Itinerary: Beach & Culture

Day 1: Follow the 1-day itinerary above. Day 2 Morning (7:00 AM – 12:00 PM): Take a whale watching tour from Mirissa (6:30 AM – 10:30 AM). Lunch (12:00 PM – 1:00 PM): Eat at Weligama Bay’s beachfront cafe (₽1 000 ≈ $11). Afternoon (1:00 PM – 5:00 PM): Visit the Sea Turtle Hatchery (1:30 PM – 2:30 PM) and Handunugoda Tea Estate (3:00 PM – 5:00 PM). Evening (5:00 PM – 9:00 PM): Take a Madu River Safari (5:30 PM – 7:30 PM) — sunset tours are stunning — then dinner at a local rice-and-curry joint (₽800 ≈ $9).

3-Day Itinerary: Full Exploration

Day 1: Same as 1-day itinerary. Day 2: Same as Day 2 above. Day 3 Morning (7:00 AM – 12:00 PM): Surf lesson (7:00 AM – 8:30 AM), then snorkel at Hikkaduwa Coral Sanctuary (9:30 AM – 12:00 PM). Lunch (12:00 PM – 1:00 PM): Eat at Hikkaduwa’s Beach Wadiya (₽1 200 ≈ $13). Afternoon (1:00 PM – 5:00 PM): Explore Koggala Lake (1:30 PM – 3:30 PM) and visit a spice garden (4:00 PM – 5:00 PM). Evening (5:00 PM – 9:00 PM): Relax at Jungle Beach for sunset (5:30 PM – 7:00 PM), then farewell dinner at The Rock Beach Restaurant (₽1 500 ≈ $16).

Tours & Excursions

To maximize your time, consider booking guided tours. They save you the hassle of negotiating tuk-tuks and provide local insights. Here are the best options:

  • Whale Watching Tour (Mirissa): ₽6 000 ≈ $65 per person — includes breakfast, snacks, and hotel pickup. Book via GetYourGuide.
  • Madu River Safari: ₽3 000 ≈ $32 per person for a 2-hour boat ride with mangrove forest and island visits. Check GetYourGuide for combo deals.
  • Galle Fort Walking Tour: ₽1 200 ≈ $13 per person — a 2-hour guided walk through history. Available on Sundays.
  • Surf Lessons: ₽3 000 ≈ $32 for 1.5 hours with equipment. Available through local schools or GetYourGuide.
  • Snorkeling Combo (Unawatuna + Hikkaduwa): ₽4 500 ≈ $49 per person — includes transport, gear, and lunch. Look for deals on GetYourGuide.

All tours can be booked online in advance to secure your spot and often offer free cancellation. For airport transfers, use Kiwitaxi for reliable, fixed-price rides from Colombo or Mattala airports.

Free Attractions

Unawatuna is budget-friendly — many top experiences cost nothing. Here’s what you can enjoy for free:

  • Unawatuna Beach: Swim, sunbathe. and people-watch without spending a rupee.
  • Japanese Peace Pagoda: Stunning views and a serene atmosphere.
  • Rumassala Sanctuary: Hike through the jungle and spot wildlife.
  • Galle Fort: Walk the ramparts and explore the streets.
  • Unawatuna Night Market: Browse stalls and enjoy live music (free entry).
  • Jungle Beach: A quiet alternative to the main beach.
  • Sunset at the Rock: Climb the large boulder at the southern end of Unawatuna Beach for a free sunset view.
