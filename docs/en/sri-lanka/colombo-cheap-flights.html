How to Find Cheap Flights to Colombo 2026

Updated: July 2026 · Expert guide · ⏱ 5 min read

Planning a trip to Colombo, the vibrant capital of Sri Lanka, is an exciting adventure, but finding affordable flights can sometimes feel like a puzzle. I've been there myself—scrolling through endless options, trying to balance budget and convenience. For 2026, the key is knowing where to look, when to book, and which airlines offer the best value. Colombo is served by Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB), located about 35 kilometers north of the city center. Major airlines like SriLankan Airlines, Emirates, Qatar Airways, and flydubai operate frequent services, but prices vary widely depending on your departure city and travel dates. Let me walk you through everything you need to know to snag a great deal.

Direct Flights

If you're looking for the most convenient option, direct flights to Colombo are available from several key cities. While prices fluctuate based on demand, here's a general idea of what to expect in 2026:

  • From Moscow (SVO/DME): SriLankan Airlines operates direct flights, taking about 8–9 hours. Expect prices from ₽45 000 (≈ $490) in low season to ₽75 000 (≈ $820) in peak months.
  • From Dubai (DXB): Emirates and flydubai offer direct services, with a flight time of around 4.5 hours. Prices range from ₽18 000 (≈ $195) to ₽30 000 (≈ $325).
  • From London (LHR): SriLankan Airlines and British Airways have direct flights (10–11 hours). Fares start at ₽55 000 (≈ $600) and can go up to ₽90 000 (≈ $980).
  • From Delhi (DEL): IndiGo and SpiceJet provide budget-friendly direct options (4 hours), with prices as low as ₽12 000 (≈ $130) to ₽20 000 (≈ $220).

Direct flights are ideal if you want to minimize travel time, but they're not always the cheapest. For budget-conscious travelers, connecting flights often offer better value.

Connecting Flights

Connecting flights can be significantly cheaper, especially if you're flexible with layovers. The most common transit hubs for Colombo include Dubai (DXB), Doha (DOH), Abu Dhabi (AUH), and Istanbul (IST). Here's a breakdown of the cheapest options:

  • Via Dubai: Flydubai and Emirates offer connections from many global cities. A flight from Moscow to Colombo via Dubai can cost ₽35 000 (≈ $380) to ₽55 000 (≈ $600), with layovers of 2–6 hours.
  • Via Doha: Qatar Airways is a popular choice, with flights from Europe and the Middle East. Prices from London to Colombo via Doha start at ₽40 000 (≈ $435), with total travel time around 14–16 hours.
  • Via Istanbul: Turkish Airlines offers competitive fares from many European cities. A flight from Berlin to Colombo via Istanbul can be as low as ₽38 000 (≈ $415), with a layover of 3–5 hours.
  • Via Bangkok (BKK): Thai Airways and AirAsia provide budget-friendly connections from Asia and Australia. A flight from Sydney to Colombo via Bangkok might cost ₽45 000 (≈ $490), with a 4-hour layover.

10 Hacks for Cheap Flights

After years of travel planning, I've learned a few tricks to save money on flights. Here are my top 10 hacks for scoring cheap tickets to Colombo in 2026:

  1. Use flight comparison tools: Start your search on Aviasales to compare prices across hundreds of airlines. It's the best way to spot deals.
  2. Be flexible with airports: Check flights into Colombo from alternative airports like Mattala Rajapaksa (HRI), though it's farther from the city. Sometimes, flying into HRI and taking a bus can save ₽10 000 (≈ $110).
  3. Book on a Tuesday or Wednesday: Airlines often release sales early in the week. I've found flights drop by 10–15% on these days.
  4. Set price alerts: Use Aviasales or Hotellook to get notifications when prices drop for your desired route.
  5. Consider budget airlines for short hauls: For regional flights, check Agoda for package deals that include flights and hotels, especially from cities like Dubai or Delhi.
  6. Mix and match airlines: Book one-way tickets on different carriers. For example, fly Emirates from Moscow to Dubai, then fly SriLankan Airlines from Dubai to Colombo. This can save ₽15 000 (≈ $165).
  7. Use incognito mode: Browsers track your searches, which can raise prices. Always search in private mode to avoid this.
  8. Check for student or youth discounts: If you're under 26, some airlines offer up to 20% off. Services like Booking sometimes have youth rates for flights.
  9. Book a flight + hotel package: Sites like Hotellook and Booking often have bundle deals that are cheaper than booking separately.
  10. Use a VPN: Prices vary by country. Set your VPN to a country like India or Thailand, where fares are often lower, and compare on Aviasales.

When to Book

Timing is everything when it comes to flight deals. For Colombo, the optimal booking window is 2–4 months before departure. For peak seasons (December–January), book at least 4–5 months in advance to avoid the last-minute price surges. For low season (May–September), you can often find good deals 6–8 weeks out. I also recommend checking GetYourGuide for tours and activities in Colombo—they sometimes offer flight discounts when you book experiences. And if you need airport transfers, Kiwitaxi is a reliable option for private transport from CMB to your hotel. For staying connected, grab an eSIM from Airalo before you go—it's affordable and saves you from roaming fees.

As a final note, if you're transiting through the Maldives on your way to Colombo (a common routing), remember: Перед вылетом заполните декларацию IMUGA на imuga.immigration.gov.mv и получите QR-код — без него не посадят на рейс. This is crucial for any stopover in the Maldives, even if you're not staying long.

With these tips, you'll be well on your way to finding a cheap flight to Colombo in 2026. Happy travels, and don't forget to enjoy the incredible food, culture, and beaches of Sri Lanka!
