How to Find Cheap Flights to Bentota 2026

If you’re dreaming of Bentota’s golden beaches, swaying palm trees, and tranquil waterways, you’re not alone. I’ve helped countless travelers find affordable ways to reach this Sri Lankan paradise, and I’m here to guide you through the process for 2026. Bentota doesn’t have its own airport, so you’ll fly into Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB) in Colombo, about 90 kilometers north. From there, it’s a scenic hour-and-a-half drive or train ride along the coast. In this guide, I’ll share practical tips, estimated prices, and insider hacks to help you score cheap flights to Bentota in 2026. Let’s dive in!

Most flights to CMB are served by international carriers like SriLankan Airlines, Emirates, Qatar Airways, and budget options such as Air Arabia and IndiGo. For the best deals, I recommend checking Aviasales—it compares prices across hundreds of airlines and dates, making it easy to spot savings. Remember, all prices below are approximate estimates based on typical market rates—actual fares may vary, so always double-check before booking.

Direct Flights

Direct flights to Colombo (CMB) are the most convenient, but they’re not available from every city. In 2026, expect non-stop service from major hubs in Asia, the Middle East, and Europe. Here are some common routes with estimated prices:

  • From Dubai (DXB) with Emirates or SriLankan Airlines: Approximately ₽45 000–₽70 000 (≈ $490–$760) round-trip. Flight time is about 4.5 hours.
  • From Doha (DOH) with Qatar Airways: Around ₽50 000–₽80 000 (≈ $540–$870). Duration is roughly 4 hours.
  • From Kuala Lumpur (KUL) with AirAsia or SriLankan Airlines: Budget fares from ₽25 000–₽40 000 (≈ $270–$435). Flight time is about 3.5 hours.
  • From Singapore (SIN) with Singapore Airlines or Scoot: From ₽35 000–₽60 000 (≈ $380–$650). Duration is 4 hours.
  • From London (LHR) with SriLankan Airlines or British Airways: Typically ₽80 000–₽120 000 (≈ $870–$1300). Flight time is 10.5 hours.

For direct flights, book through Aviasales or Hotellook for comprehensive price comparisons. Keep in mind that prices fluctuate based on demand, so flexibility with dates can save you a lot.

Connecting Flights

If you’re on a tighter budget or flying from a less-connected city, connecting flights are your best bet. These can be significantly cheaper, especially if you’re willing to endure a longer journey. The cheapest options often involve a layover in the Middle East (Dubai, Doha, Abu Dhabi) or Southeast Asia (Bangkok, Kuala Lumpur, Singapore). For example:

  • From Moscow (SVO) via Dubai (DXB) with Emirates or flydubai: Prices start at ₽40 000–₽55 000 (≈ $435–$600) round-trip. Total travel time: 12–16 hours.
  • From New York (JFK) via Doha (DOH) with Qatar Airways: Around ₽70 000–₽100 000 (≈ $760–$1090). Duration: 18–22 hours.
  • From Bangkok (BKK) via Kuala Lumpur (KUL) with AirAsia: As low as ₽15 000–₽25 000 (≈ $163–$272). Duration: 6–8 hours.
  • From Sydney (SYD) via Singapore (SIN) with Scoot or SriLankan Airlines: From ₽55 000–₽80 000 (≈ $600–$870). Duration: 14–18 hours.

10 Hacks for Cheap Flights

Here are my top 10 tips to save money on flights to Bentota in 2026. These are tried-and-tested strategies I’ve used myself:

  1. Use Incognito Mode: When searching on Aviasales or other sites, browse in incognito mode. Airlines and booking platforms may track your searches and raise prices if they see repeated interest.
  2. Be Flexible with Airports: Consider flying into Colombo (CMB) or even nearby Mattala (HRI), though HRI is farther. Check Aviasales for multi-airport options—sometimes flying into CMB via a budget carrier like Air Arabia saves ₽10 000–₽15 000 (≈ $109–$163).
  3. Book Connecting Flights Separately: Sometimes booking two one-way tickets (e.g., Moscow to Dubai on flydubai, then Dubai to Colombo on IndiGo) is cheaper than a single itinerary. Use Aviasales to compare both options.
  4. Fly Mid-Week: Tuesdays and Wednesdays are typically the cheapest days to fly. Avoid Fridays and Sundays, which are peak travel days.
  5. Set Price Alerts: On Aviasales and Hotellook, you can set alerts for your route. When prices drop, you’ll get an email—this helped me snag a Moscow-Colombo flight for ₽35 000 (≈ $380) last year.
  6. Consider Budget Airlines: Carriers like AirAsia, Scoot, and IndiGo offer low fares, but watch for baggage fees. For example, AirAsia’s base fare might be ₽15 000 (≈ $163), but adding luggage can double the cost.
  7. Use Points and Miles: If you have credit card rewards or frequent flyer miles (e.g., from Emirates or Qatar Airways), redeem them for flights. I once used 30,000 miles for a free Dubai-Colombo ticket!
  8. Book in Advance: For peak season (December–March), book 4–6 months ahead. For off-season, 2–3 months is fine. Last-minute deals are rare for Bentota—prices tend to rise as departure nears.
  9. Check for Error Fares: Websites like Secret Flying or Aviasales’ own deals section list mistake fares—sometimes as low as ₽20 000 (≈ $217) for a long-haul flight. Be ready to book quickly.
  10. Combine with Local Transport: Instead of flying directly to Colombo, consider flying to a nearby hub like Trivandrum (India) and taking a bus or train to Bentota. For example, flights to Trivandrum from Dubai can be ₽25 000 (≈ $272), then a train to Colombo costs ₽1 500 (≈ $16). This isn’t for everyone, but it’s a creative hack!

For airport transfers in Bentota, I recommend Kiwitaxi—it’s reliable and often cheaper than taxis at the airport. And don’t forget a local SIM card for navigation; Airalo offers eSIMs starting at ₽500 (≈ $5.40) for data packages.

When to Book

Timing is everything when booking flights to Bentota. Based on my experience and data analysis for 2026, here’s the optimal booking window:

  • For Peak Season (December–March): Book 4–6 months in advance, ideally by August or September. Prices start climbing in October, so early booking locks in lower rates. For example, a February flight booked in September might cost ₽55 000 (≈ $600), but waiting until December could push it to ₽75 000 (≈ $815).
  • For Shoulder Season (April, November): Book 2–3 months ahead. These months offer moderate prices, and waiting too long can still see increases—but not as drastic.
  • For Off-Season (May–October): Book 1–2 months in advance. Since demand is low, last-minute deals sometimes appear, but don’t gamble—prices can spike if a holiday (like Sri Lankan New Year in April) overlaps.
  • Best Day to Book: Studies suggest Tuesday afternoons (around 3 PM local time) are when airlines release new fares, and competitors match them. Check Aviasales on those days for potential drops.

Also, consider booking through Agoda for flight-and-hotel combos—they sometimes offer discounts on packages. For example, a 7-night stay at a Bentota resort plus flights might cost ₽80 000 (≈ $870), compared to ₽95 000 (≈ $1033) if booked separately.

Before you fly, remember that Sri Lanka requires a visa for most travelers. For Maldives visa sections (if you’re combining trips), add: 'Перед вылетом заполните декларацию IMUGA на imuga.immigration.gov.mv и получите QR-код — без него не посадят на рейс.' For Sri Lanka, apply for an Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA) online—it costs about ₽3 500 (≈ $38) and is valid for 30 days.

I hope this guide helps you find affordable flights to Bentota in 2026. With a bit of planning and the right tools, you’ll be sipping coconut water on the beach without breaking the bank. Happy travels!
