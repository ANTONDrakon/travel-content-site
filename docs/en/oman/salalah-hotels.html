Top 10 Hotels in Salalah 2026: Budget to Luxury

Salalah, the jewel of Oman’s southern Dhofar region, is a destination like no other. Known for its lush green landscapes during the khareef (monsoon) season, pristine beaches, and ancient frankincense trails, this city offers a unique blend of natural beauty and cultural heritage.

I’ve spent countless hours exploring Salalah’s neighborhoods—from the lively Haffa district to the serene Al Mughsail coast—and I’m excited to share my handpicked selection of hotels for 2026. Whether you’re a budget-conscious backpacker or a luxury seeker, Salalah has something special waiting for you.

Hotel prices in Salalah can vary significantly depending on the season. During the khareef season (June to September), rates can double or triple as visitors flock to experience the misty mountains and blooming valleys. In the shoulder months (October to November and February to May), you’ll find more reasonable prices. I recommend booking early if you’re traveling during peak times—rooms at popular hotels fill up fast. You can сравнить цены на отели в Салале на Hotellook или Booking.com — это поможет найти лучшие предложения.

All prices in this guide are approximate estimates based on typical market rates for 2026. They are not exact quotes from specific providers, so always double-check before booking.

Budget Hotels (under $50/night)

1. Salalah Garden Hotel

Цена: от ₽3 500 за ночь (≈ $38)

This no-frills hotel is a favorite among backpackers and solo travelers. Located in the Al Wadi area, it’s a 10-minute drive from Al Haffa Beach and close to local restaurants. Rooms are clean but basic—think simple furniture, air conditioning, and a small TV. The staff are friendly and can help arrange taxi tours to nearby attractions like Wadi Darbat.

Плюсы: Affordable rates, helpful staff, free parking.
Минусы: No pool, dated decor, breakfast is minimal (just tea and bread).

2. Raysut Hotel

Цена: от ₽3 200 за ночь (≈ $35)

Located near the Raysut industrial area, this hotel is ideal for travelers on a tight budget. It’s a 15-minute drive from the city center, but the trade-off is lower prices. Rooms are spacious with tiled floors, and the on-site restaurant serves decent Omani dishes like shuwa (slow-cooked lamb). The hotel also offers free Wi-Fi, though it can be spotty.

Плюсы: Very cheap, large rooms, authentic local food.
Минусы: Far from beaches, noisy due to nearby traffic, limited amenities.

3. Al Wadi Hotel

Цена: от ₽4 000 за ночь (≈ $44)

Tucked away in a quiet residential street, Al Wadi Hotel offers good value for couples. The rooms are compact but well-maintained, with a small seating area and a mini-fridge. The highlight is the rooftop terrace where you can enjoy evening tea while watching the sunset over the city. It’s a 5-minute walk from a small supermarket and local eateries.

Плюсы: Quiet location, rooftop terrace, good for couples.
Минусы: No restaurant (only breakfast), small bathrooms, no pool.

Mid-Range Hotels ($50-150/night)

4. Salalah Beach Resort

Цена: от ₽6 500 за ночь (≈ $70)

This resort is a hidden gem on the eastern coast, about 20 minutes from the city center. It offers spacious rooms with sea views, a large outdoor pool, and direct access to a private beach. The restaurant serves buffet breakfast with both Omani and international options. I loved the evening walks along the shore—the water is calm and perfect for swimming.

Особенности: Private beach, pool, kids’ play area, free parking.

5. Crowne Plaza Salalah

Цена: от ₽9 000 за ночь (≈ $98)

Part of the IHG chain, this hotel is a reliable choice for business and leisure travelers. It’s located in the Al Khor area, close to the Sultan Qaboos Mosque and the city’s main shopping malls. Rooms are modern with plush beds, a work desk, and a flat-screen TV. The highlight is the rooftop infinity pool overlooking the ocean—perfect for Instagram-worthy photos.

Особенности: Rooftop pool, fitness center, two restaurants, meeting rooms.

6. Hilton Salalah Resort

Цена: от ₽10 500 за ночь (≈ $114)

This resort is a family favorite, located on the beachfront in the Haffa district. It features a large lagoon-style pool, a kids’ club, and multiple dining options including an Italian restaurant and a seafood grill. Rooms are spacious with private balconies—I recommend requesting a sea view. The staff are exceptionally attentive, and the property is well-maintained.

Особенности: Lagoon pool, kids’ club, spa, beachfront access.

7. Salalah International Hotel

Цена: от ₽7 500 за ночь (≈ $82)

A solid mid-range option in the city center, this hotel is within walking distance of the Al Husn Souq and the Frankincense Museum. Rooms are clean and comfortable, with a traditional Omani design theme. The breakfast buffet includes freshly baked bread, local cheeses, and dates. The hotel also has a small gym and a rooftop lounge.

Особенности: Central location, rooftop lounge, gym, free Wi-Fi.

Luxury Hotels ($150+/night)

8. Al Baleed Resort Salalah by Anantara

Цена: от ₽23 000 за ночь (≈ $250)

This is the pinnacle of luxury in Salalah. Located on the beachfront near the Al Baleed Archaeological Park, the resort offers stunning Arabian-inspired architecture with private villas, each featuring a plunge pool and a garden. The spa is world-class, offering traditional hammam treatments and Omani frankincense massages. Dining is exceptional—try the seafood at the beachfront restaurant.

Особенности: Private villas with pools, spa, multiple restaurants, kids’ club, private beach.

9. Juweira Boutique Hotel

Цена: от ₽18 000 за ночь (≈ $195)

A boutique gem in the Al Haffa area, Juweira offers a more intimate luxury experience. The hotel has only 30 rooms, each uniquely decorated with Omani crafts and modern touches. The infinity pool overlooks the Arabian Sea, and the rooftop bar is a great spot for sunset cocktails. The staff can arrange private tours to the Frankincense Trail or a dhow cruise.

Особенности: Intimate setting, infinity pool, rooftop bar, personalized service.

10. Fanar Hotel & Residences

Цена: от ₽20 000 за ночь (≈ $217)

Located in the Al Mughsail area, this resort is perfect for those seeking seclusion. It offers spacious suites and villas with panoramic ocean views, a large infinity pool, and a private beach. The highlight is the on-site observatory—guests can stargaze with a professional telescope. The restaurant serves a fusion of Omani and Mediterranean cuisine.

Особенности: Observatory, infinity pool, private beach, spacious villas.

How to Choose the Right Area

Salalah’s neighborhoods offer different vibes, so choose based on your travel style:

  • Для пляжного отдыха: Stay in the Al Haffa or Al Mughsail areas. Al Haffa has the best beaches with calm waters, while Al Mughsail is quieter and closer to the dramatic cliffs and blowholes. Hotels like Al Baleed Resort and Fanar Hotel are top picks.
  • Для осмотра достопримечательностей: The city center (around Al Husn Souq) is ideal for exploring the Frankincense Museum, Sultan Qaboos Mosque, and the old town. The Crowne Plaza and Salalah International Hotel are centrally located.
  • Для ночной жизни: The Haffa district has a few bars and lounges, mostly in hotels. The Hilton Salalah Resort and Juweira Boutique Hotel have lively rooftop bars. For a more authentic experience, head to local coffee shops in the Al Wadi area.

Airport Transfers

Salalah International Airport (SLL) is about 20 minutes from the city center and 30-40 minutes from most beach resorts. Here’s how to get to your hotel:

  • Такси: Available at the airport. A ride to the city center costs around ₽1 500 (≈ $16), while resorts like Al Baleed cost ₽2 000-₽2 500 (≈ $22-27). Negotiate the fare before getting in.
  • Аренда автомобиля: Renting a car is the best way to explore Salalah. Prices start at ₽2 500 (≈ $27) per day from local agencies. Check Kiwitaxi for pre-booked transfers.
  • Трансфер от отеля: Many mid-range and luxury hotels offer airport pickups for an extra fee (₽2 000-₽3 000 / ≈ $22-33).

Для удобства вы можете заказать трансфер из аэропорта Салалы на Kiwitaxi — это надежный сервис с фиксированными ценами.

Booking Tips

Here’s how to save money and get the best deals for your Salalah trip:

  • Бронируйте заранее: For khareef season (June-September), book at least 2-3 months in advance. Prices can increase by 50-100% during this period.
  • Используйте агрегаторы: Compare prices on Aviasales for flights and Hotellook for hotels. These sites scan multiple booking platforms to find the best rates.
  • Сезонные скидки: Many hotels offer discounts in the summer (except khareef). Check Agoda for last-minute deals.
  • Кэшбэк и бонусы: Use credit cards with travel rewards or cashback programs. Some booking sites like Booking.com offer Genius discounts for frequent users.
  • Пакетные предложения: Look for flight+hotel packages on Aviasales or Booking.com. These can save up to 20% compared to booking separately.
