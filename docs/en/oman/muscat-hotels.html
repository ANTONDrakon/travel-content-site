When I first visited Muscat, I was struck by how effortlessly it blends ancient Omani charm with modern comfort. The city stretches along a stunning coastline, framed by rugged mountains and the turquoise Gulf of Oman. Whether you're watching dhows glide by at sunset or exploring the labyrinthine Muttrah Souq, your choice of hotel can shape your entire experience. From humble guesthouses with rooftop views to palatial beachfront resorts, Muscat offers accommodations for every traveler.

Hotel prices here tend to reflect the season: expect peak rates from November to March (the cooler months) and better deals from June to September. In this guide, I’ll walk you through 10 handpicked hotels across budget, mid-range, and luxury categories, so you can find the perfect base for your Omani adventure. Budget Hotels (under ₽4 600 / $50 per night)

1. Al Arej Inn

Tucked away in the historic Muttrah district, Al Arej Inn is a charming guesthouse that feels like a local’s home. Rooms are simple but spotless, with traditional Omani furnishings and air conditioning. The rooftop terrace offers panoramic views of the corniche and the sea — perfect for evening tea. Breakfast is included and features fresh bread, eggs, and dates. The staff are incredibly warm and can help arrange tours or taxi bookings.

Prices: Standard double room from ₽3 200 (≈ $35) per night.

Pros: Authentic atmosphere, great location near Muttrah Souq and the port, friendly staff, rooftop views.

Cons: No pool, some rooms are small, street noise can be an issue on weekends.

2. Muscat Inn Hotel

Located in Al Khuwair, a central area with easy access to restaurants and shops, Muscat Inn Hotel is a reliable budget option. The rooms are basic but well-maintained, with modern bathrooms and free Wi-Fi. The hotel has a small lobby lounge and offers a simple continental breakfast. It’s a popular choice among backpackers and solo travelers for its affordability and convenience.

Prices: Double room from ₽3 700 (≈ $40) per night.

Pros: Clean and comfortable, central location, good value, helpful front desk.

Cons: No pool or gym, breakfast is basic, some rooms lack natural light.

3. Al Jawhara Hotel Apartments

If you’re traveling with family or planning a longer stay, these apartment-style rooms in Ruwi are a smart choice. Each unit comes with a kitchenette, separate living area, and a balcony. The property is a bit dated, but it’s clean and spacious. There’s a small supermarket nearby, and the hotel offers free parking. It’s not near the beach, but it’s close to bus routes and the central business district.

Prices: One-bedroom apartment from ₽4 100 (≈ $45) per night.

Pros: Spacious, kitchen facilities, good for families, free parking.

Cons: Farther from tourist attractions, no pool, dated décor.

Mid-Range Hotels (₽4 600 – ₽13 800 / $50–150 per night)

4. Crowne Plaza Muscat

Perched on a cliff in Shatti Al Qurum, the Crowne Plaza offers sweeping views of the Gulf of Oman. The rooms are elegantly furnished with Omani touches, and many have balconies facing the sea. The hotel features a large outdoor pool, a fitness center, and multiple restaurants serving international and local cuisine. The private beach is a short walk down a path, and the hotel provides shuttle service to the city center. It’s a solid choice for travelers who want comfort without the premium price tag of a five-star resort.

Prices: Double room from ₽7 400 (≈ $80) per night.

Highlights: Stunning sea views, large pool, good dining options, close to the beach.

5. InterCityHotel Muscat

Located in Al Khuwair, this modern hotel is ideal for business travelers or those who want a no-fuss stay. The rooms are compact but efficient, with work desks, soundproof windows, and comfortable beds. The hotel has a small gym, a restaurant serving buffet breakfast and dinner, and a 24-hour front desk. It’s within walking distance of several cafes and shops, and the airport is a 20-minute drive away. The vibe is professional and clean, with friendly staff.

Prices: Double room from ₽5 500 (≈ $60) per night.

Highlights: Great value for money, soundproof rooms, convenient location, efficient service.

6. The Chedi Muscat (Mid-Range Option)

While The Chedi is often listed as a luxury property, its entry-level rooms during off-peak seasons can fall into the mid-range bracket. This iconic resort on the beach in Ghubra is known for its minimalist architecture, long infinity pools, and lush gardens. The standard rooms are spacious and feature floor-to-ceiling windows, rain showers, and Omani-inspired décor. The hotel has a world-class spa, a fitness center, and several restaurants including a beachfront seafood spot. If you can snag a deal, it’s a fantastic upgrade.

Prices: Double room from ₽11 000 (≈ $120) per night in low season.

Highlights: Stunning design, three pools, private beach, exceptional service.

7. Park Inn by Radisson Muscat

This cheerful hotel in Al Khuwair is part of the Radisson group and offers consistent quality. The rooms are bright and modern, with colorful accents and all the essentials: flat-screen TV, minibar, and free Wi-Fi. The hotel has a rooftop pool, a fitness center, and a restaurant serving international buffet meals. The location is convenient for exploring the city, with the Grand Mosque a 10-minute drive away and the Muttrah Souq about 15 minutes. The staff are known for their warm hospitality.

Prices: Double room from ₽6 400 (≈ $70) per night.

Highlights: Rooftop pool, good location, reliable brand, family-friendly.

Luxury Hotels (₽13 800+ / $150+ per night)

8. Al Bustan Palace, a Ritz-Carlton Hotel

This legendary hotel is a destination in itself. Set on a private beach in Qantab, Al Bustan Palace features grand Arabian architecture with soaring domes, intricate mosaics, and a stunning atrium. The rooms and suites are palatial, with marble bathrooms, plush bedding, and panoramic sea views.

The resort offers multiple pools, a world-class spa, tennis courts, and a selection of fine-dining restaurants. The service is impeccable, from the welcome drink to the nightly turndown. It’s the ultimate choice for a romantic getaway or a splurge-worthy vacation.

Prices: Double room from ₽23 000 (≈ $250) per night.

Features: Private beach, multiple pools, luxury spa, exceptional dining, grand architecture.

9. Shangri-La Al Husn, Muscat

Perched on a cliff in the Shangri-La complex, Al Husn is an adults-only sanctuary that exudes peace and sophistication. The rooms are elegantly decorated with Omani textiles and offer stunning views of the Gulf. Guests have access to a private beach, a serene infinity pool, and the exclusive CHI, The Spa. The hotel’s restaurant serves fine Mediterranean and Omani cuisine, and the service is attentive without being intrusive. It’s perfect for couples seeking tranquility and luxury.

Prices: Double room from ₽20 000 (≈ $220) per night.

Features: Adults-only, private beach, infinity pool, world-class spa, personalized service.

10. W Muscat

For a modern, vibrant experience, the W Muscat in Al Mouj is a standout. The hotel is part of a chic marina development with shops, restaurants, and a golf course. The rooms are bold and playful, with neon accents, floor-to-ceiling windows, and oversized bathrooms. The W has a rooftop pool with a bar, a lively restaurant, a nightclub, and a spa. It’s the go-to for travelers who want a trendy, social atmosphere with top-notch amenities.

Prices: Double room from ₽18 500 (≈ $200) per night.

Features: Rooftop pool, marina location, vibrant design, nightlife options, spa.

How to Choose the Right Area

Muscat is a sprawling city, so picking the right neighborhood matters. Here’s a quick guide based on your travel style:

For Beach Lovers

Head to Qantab or Shatti Al Qurum. These areas have the best beaches and luxury resorts like Al Bustan Palace and Crowne Plaza. You’ll be close to the sea, with easy access to water sports and sunset walks. Budget options are limited here, but the mid-range Crowne Plaza offers a good balance.

For Sightseeing

Stay in Muttrah or Ruwi. Muttrah puts you steps from the souq, the corniche, and the Sultan’s Palace. Ruwi is more central for museums and the Grand Mosque. Budget hotels like Al Arej Inn are perfect here, and mid-range options are a short taxi ride away.

For Nightlife and Dining

Al Khuwair and Al Mouj are your best bets. Al Khuwair has a mix of restaurants, cafes, and bars, plus the Park Inn and InterCityHotel. Al Mouj, home to the W Muscat, offers a marina with upscale dining and a lively scene. Luxury seekers will enjoy the W’s rooftop bar and club.

Airport Transfers

Muscat International Airport (MCT) is about 20–30 minutes from most hotels, depending on traffic. Taxis are available at the airport, but prices can vary — expect to pay around ₽2 300 (≈ $25) to central areas. For a stress-free arrival, I recommend pre-booking a transfer.
