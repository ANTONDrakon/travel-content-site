Information

Practical information

🛂 Visa

Visa on arrival (20 OMR / ~$52) for Russian citizens, up to 14 days.

💱 Currency

Omani Rial (OMR). 1 OMR ≈ $2.6. Expensive currency, but prices moderate.

🗣 Language

Arabic. English everywhere.

🚌 Transport

Car rental — best option (from $25/day). Buses available but rare.

🛡 Safety

Very safe. One of the world's safest countries.

📶 Internet & SIM

Wi-Fi in hotels. Local SIM (Omantel, Ooredoo) — from $5 with 10 GB.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a visa for Oman?

Yes, visa on arrival. 20 OMR (~$52), up to 14 days.

When is the best time to visit Oman?

October–April. Perfect weather. Summer — only Salalah.

How much does a trip to Oman cost?

Budget: from $800/week. Comfort: from $1800.

What to see in Oman?

Muscat, Nizwa, Wahiba, Musandam, Salalah, forts.

Is Oman safe?

Yes, very safe. Conservative but hospitable.

Can I rent a car?

Yes, essential. Best way to travel. From $25/day.
